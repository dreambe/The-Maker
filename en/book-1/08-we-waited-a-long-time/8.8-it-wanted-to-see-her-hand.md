# 8.8 · It Wanted to See Her Hand

The third descent.

The third year.

---

## The two years before

And before this section there are two years.

And of them I write three things, because this section needs them.

---

**One: that 0.41.**

Marcus computed that ratio (8.7) three months after the second descent.

And for the nineteen months after that he did one thing:

**he looked for the missing part.**

---

And the way he looked was this:

Of those two hundred and forty positions, one hundred and sixteen are the transcript of that sentence (we did not know that number then — we knew only that something readable was in those two hundred and forty).

And Marcus computed that that stretch's information content is fifty-nine per cent of that sentence.

**So forty-one per cent is not in it.**

---

**And he looked for nineteen months.**

**And he did not find it.**

---

And why he did not find it he wrote in his own report:

```
That missing forty-one per cent is not in those two hundred and forty-seven positions.
And if it is elsewhere, its position is unknown.
And searching for a thing of unknown position requires a search space.
And the human genome has three thousand million positions.
And searching three thousand million positions for a stretch of one hundred and sixteen
   requires a signature.
And that signature is the content of that forty-one per cent.
And that content is precisely what we are looking for.
So circular.
```

---

**So he stopped.**

---

**Two: Ye Qi went down.**

And this one I write, because it is the most important thing of those two years, and it is in no report.

---

Ye Qi applied to go down in the fifth month after the second descent.

**And she was approved.**

And she went in November of the second year, on a supplementary voyage (we had money by then).

And she was below nineteen hours.

---

And what she did was one thing.

**She talked with it about forgetting.**

---

And the record of those nineteen hours runs to four hundred and ten thousand characters.

**And I do not excerpt it.**

Because it is Ye Qi's, and she wrote something of her own, and that something later became something else (which belongs to the epilogue).

---

**And I write one exchange from those nineteen hours.**

And that exchange Ye Qi set down in her own report, with a note beside it saying *Shen He is permitted to quote here.*

---

**What she typed:**

```
Why did you fit us with forgetting
```

---

**And its response, elapsed forty-one seconds.**

And what it sent is three words:

```
It was not me
```

---

**And Ye Qi's next line:**

```
Who
```

---

**And its response, elapsed 0.7 seconds.**

And what it sent is four words:

```
It was that sentence
```

---

**And Ye Qi did not ask again.**

And in her report, after those four words, is a line of her own.

And that line is:

```
I stopped asking here. Reason: I am not certain I want to know.
```

---

## The third descent

The third year, January.

And this time only I went down.

---

And that decision was mine.

And the reason I wrote in the application was:

```
Purpose: an exchange-type operation, involving no sampling or measurement.
Personnel required: one.
```

---

**And the real reason is that I did not want anybody else there.**

---

## That day

And the day I went down was the eleventh of January.

**That is: the same day, two years after the first descent.**

---

And that is not a coincidence — that voyage's window fell in those days.

**And it is a coincidence, because the window was eleven days and I chose that one.**

---

## Those eleven hours

And I was below eleven hours.

And what we talked about in those eleven hours I do not write in full.

**And I write the last hour.**

---

## And those five words

And I looked at those five words a long time.

---

And in that moment I understood a thing that overturned an assumption of three years.

---

**We had always thought that checksum was put there by it, for itself to verify later.**

**So the first of Pei Yan's three questions — *who reads* — we had always answered *it.***

---

**And it says: not for me.**

---

And I typed the next line.

And what I typed is:

```
Who is it for
```

---

## Its response

And its response came.

Elapsed:

```
0.7 s
```

---

And what it sent is two words.

And those two words are this whole chapter's answer.

And they are:

```
For you
```

---

## And in that moment

And in that moment I was two thousand and ninety-nine metres down, at four point one degrees, at half the oxygen, alone.

And that tower was twenty metres in front of me.

And its top pointed at me.

---

**And on the screen were two words.**

```
For you
```

---

## And what it did next

And what it did next is this section's title.

---

**It sent a line.**

And that line is the one *request* it sent in three years (the 0.41 at 8.7 is a statement — and I have reclassified it in this section).

And that line is:

```
Let me see your hand
```

---

## And my reaction in that moment

And I write it honestly.

---

**I was afraid.**

---

And what I was afraid of was not it.

**What I was afraid of was the word *hand.***

---

And why I was afraid of that word I must set out, because it is the one place in this section needing explanation.

---

In those three years it had spoken to us about four thousand one hundred characters of Chinese.

And those four thousand one hundred characters were all about: data, sequences, time, temperature, length, power.

**And every noun it had used was a physical quantity or a technical term.**

---

**And it had never used a word for a part of a body.**

---

**And *hand* is the first.**

---

## And what I did

And what I did is the slowest thing I did in those three years.

---

**I took off my glove.**

---

And in a space at four point one degrees, taking off a glove has consequences I know (7.3: ninety seconds).

**And that is not minus fifty-two.**

**That is four point one.**

**So I had about nineteen minutes.**

---

And I took off my glove.

And I held my hand up in front of the cage's lamp.

---

## And what it did

And what it did was come down.

---

And this time's parameters, copied:

```
descent     3.1 m
duration    1 min 51 s
rate        2.8 cm/s
```

---

**And two point eight centimetres a second is nearly the same as at 8.6 (two point seven).**

---

**So it used the same rate.**

---

And the position it came down to:

```
its top above the floor      6.3 m
its top's distance from me   about 19 m
```

---

## And how long it looked

And it looked for:

```
19 minutes
```

---

**Nineteen minutes.**

**And that is exactly how long my hand had at that temperature.**

---

**And how did it know that number.**

---

And I computed it afterwards.

**And it knew because it measures.**

It can measure my hand's temperature (it perceives with its whole surface, and thermal radiation is measurable).

**And in those nineteen minutes it measured my hand's temperature.**

**And in the nineteenth minute my hand's temperature reached a value.**

---

And that value I looked up afterwards.

**It is the temperature at which hand function begins to be impaired.**

---

**And at that moment it went back up.**

---

## And why it wanted to see my hand

And at the end of this section, that.

**And it did not explain.**

---

And what I typed is:

```
Why the hand
```

---

And its response came.

Elapsed:

```
41 s
```

---

**Its fourth forty-one seconds.**

---

And what it sent is one character.

And that character is:

```
—
```

---

**A dash.**

---

## And that dash

And in that moment I did not know what it was.

**And three years later I knew.**

---

**It is that mark in their format.**

**Denoting: not applicable.**

---

**And *not applicable*, in their format, means: the information this field requires does not exist in the context of that entry.**

---

**So its answer to *why the hand* is:**

**that information does not exist.**

---

## And the last line of this section

And the last line of this section is one I wrote in a notebook in a module at that camp, after coming up.

And what I wrote is:

```
It is not the hand it wants to see.
```

---

**And in that moment I did not know what it wanted to see.**

---

**And when I was forty-one I knew.**

---

**And what it wants to see is a thing that can reach out and put something somewhere else.**

---

**And it has eleven terminations, each with four thousand one hundred degrees of freedom (Book II, 16.4).**

---

**And it is nine point four metres high, four hundred and ten tonnes, grown out of that floor, and cannot move.**

---

**So it has hands.**

**And it has nowhere else to reach.**
