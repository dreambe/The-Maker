# 8.2 · It Moved

Four minutes later.

---

## Those four minutes

The four of us stood in that space for four minutes.

And in those four minutes what we did was **look.**

And I write what each of us was looking at, because those four were looking at different things, and that difference mattered afterwards.

---

**Marcus was looking at the layers.**

From his first second he went to that tower's base, crouched, and looked at the layers.

And in those four minutes he said nothing.

**And he counted.**

---

**Marija was looking at the roof.**

She looked up at that space's roof for about two minutes.

And afterwards she told me what she was looking for.

She said:

"I was looking for how it got in."

I said, "Meaning."

She said:

**"This thing is nine point four metres high and eleven across at the base."**

**"And the only opening is zero point four one."**

**"So it did not come in from above."**

She said:

**"So it grew here."**

---

**Ilan was looking at its top.**

That is, the zero point four one metre top.

And he was looking at it because he was computing something.

And in the third minute he said it.

He said:

**"That top points at that opening."**

I said, "I know. The dimensions match."

He said:

**"Not the dimensions. The direction."**

He said:

**"That opening is offset zero point one nine metres from the centre of the roof."**

**"And this tower is dead centre."**

**"So that top is not directly beneath that opening."**

He said:

**"And that top is tilted."**

---

And I looked up.

**And it is tilted.**

The top of that tower is not vertical.

**It leans about eleven degrees to one side.**

**And the direction of that lean is straight at the opening.**

---

**So that top points at that opening.**

---

## And what I was looking at

And in those four minutes I was looking at where its base meets the floor.

And I was looking at that because of a very small thing.

**I was looking for a seam.**

That is: whether there is a seam between that tower and that space's floor.

And I looked for four minutes.

---

**None.**

---

**It and that floor are one piece.**

**It was not put there.**

**It grew out of that floor.**

---

And the technical import of that, because it is this section's premise.

**A thing that grew from the floor cannot be carried away.**

**So it is here because it grew here.**

**And it grew here because something made it here.**

**And that something is not here.**

---

## The fourth minute

And at the end of the fourth minute I did a thing.

**I took three steps forward.**

---

And those three steps I write out, because they are the start of this section.

The four of us, having come down, stood beneath that opening — that is, zero point one nine metres off centre.

And that tower is at the centre.

**So there were twenty point five metres between us.**

---

And I took three steps forward.

**About two point one metres.**

---

**And as I completed the third step, it moved.**

---

## That movement

I write that movement precisely, because it is the first movement in three hundred thousand years, and it is very small.

---

**Its top turned.**

---

And by how much:

```
angle       about 4.1 degrees
direction   toward me
duration    (see below)
```

---

`Duration (see below).`

**We did not capture the duration.**

Because it was too fast.

And our camera (the one fixed to the cage; we brought it down this time) runs at sixty frames a second.

**And that turn completed between two frames.**

**So its duration is under sixteen milliseconds.**

---

## And what that means

And Marcus computed that afterwards.

What he computed was: **the power required for a four-hundred-and-ten-tonne thing to turn its top four point one degrees in sixteen milliseconds.**

And what he got:

```
power required    about 1.1 x 10^7 W
```

**Eleven million watts.**

**And a large locomotive is six million.**

---

**So in those sixteen milliseconds it used the power of two locomotives.**

---

And the power it uses to maintain that four degrees we had also computed (from that space's heat loss):

```
power to maintain temperature    about 4.1 x 10^2 W
```

**Four hundred and ten watts.**

---

**So in those sixteen milliseconds it used twenty-six thousand times its everyday power.**

---

## And why it moved like that

And what I felt in that moment, because it is not fear.

---

**I stopped.**

Having completed the third step, I stopped.

And I stopped not because I was afraid.

**Because in that moment I understood a thing I had not worked out in the year before.**

---

**It is following me.**

---

And *following* must be explained.

That top turned four point one degrees, toward me.

And I, at twenty point five metres from it, had moved two point one metres forward.

And two point one metres, at twenty point five metres, subtends:

```
arctan(2.1 / 20.5) = 5.8 degrees
```

---

**And it turned four point one degrees.**

---

**Not five point eight.**

---

And that difference I did not compute on the spot.

**And Marcus did.**

And having computed it he said something, and it is the core of this section.

He said:

**"It turned four point one. And it should have turned five point eight."**

I said, "So it's inaccurate."

He said:

**"No."**

He said:

**"It turned the angle it needed to turn."**

**"And how much it needs to turn depends on what it is looking at."**

---

## And what it was looking at

And at the end of this section, that.

Marcus's derivation, as I copy it from his report:

```
let that thing's top have a pointing direction
let that direction be required to align with something
then the angle the top must turn as that something moves
   = the angle subtended, at the top, between that something's positions before and after

and Shen He moved 2.1 m, subtending 5.8 degrees at the top
and the top turned 4.1 degrees

so what the top aligns with is not Shen He.

and the position of what it aligns with can be computed:
   let the top's original direction be P and its new direction P'
   with 4.1 degrees between them
   and Shen He's displacement changing her subtended angle by 5.8 degrees
   so what P' points at lies behind Shen He
   its distance from the top       about 29 m
   its position relative to Shen He   8.5 m directly behind her
```

---

`8.5 metres directly behind her.`

---

And what is eight point five metres directly behind me.

---

**Marija.**

---

## And what Marija was doing

And what was Marija doing while I took those three steps.

We looked at the recording when we got back.

And in and around those sixteen milliseconds, what Marija was doing is:

**Her head was up, looking at that space's roof.**

---

**And her hand was holding an instrument.**

And that instrument is a laser rangefinder.

And she was using it to measure the roof's height.

---

**And a laser rangefinder, in operation, emits a laser.**

---

## So

**It was not looking at me.**

**What it turned toward is that laser.**

---

And that laser's wavelength is in our instrument's specifications:

```
wavelength    1 550 nm
```

---

**And 1 550 nanometres is infrared.**

**Invisible to the human eye.**

---

**And it saw it.**

---

## And the end of this section

And the last line of this section.

The first movement in three hundred thousand years.

**It did not turn toward a person.**

**It turned toward a beam of light.**

---

**And that beam came from a rangefinder, and that rangefinder was measuring the height of the space.**

---

**And in sixteen milliseconds it used the power of two locomotives to turn four point one degrees and align with that beam.**

---

**And that beam lasted zero point zero eight seconds, and then Marija lowered the instrument.**

---

**And it did not turn back.**

---

**Its top, from that moment, points at the place where Marija was standing.**

**And Marija moved seventeen times afterwards.**

**And it did not move again.**
