# 9.1 · It Began to Speak

The fourth descent.

The third year, April.

---

## The three months before

And before this section there are three months.

And of what happened in them I write one thing.

---

**Lu Ting came out of hospital.**

And having come out, he came to that eighteen-square-metre room.

And on his first day the first thing he did was walk to that whiteboard.

---

And he stood there, looking at the heading.

> **Phenomena that cannot be explained by　　　　**

---

And he looked a long time.

Then he said a thing, and I have thought about it many times in the year since.

He said:

**"Those letters. I erased them."**

---

I said, "I know."

He said, "How do you know."

I said:

"Because the last one out that night was me, and I turned off the light and locked the door."

I said:

"And the first one in the next morning was me too."

I said:

**"And in between, only you have a key."**

---

And he laughed.

And he said:

**"Then why did you say nothing for two and a half years."**

---

And I did not answer.

And he did not ask again.

---

## And what he asked me to do

And on that day he asked me to do one thing.

And that thing is the reason for this whole chapter.

---

He said:

**"I want you to ask it something."**

I said, "What."

He said:

**"I want you to ask it why we die."**

---

I said, "We die because of entropy."

He said:

**"No."**

He said:

**"Entropy explains why a thing breaks."**

**"And entropy does not explain why a thing's lifespan is seventy years and not seven hundred."**

---

He said:

**"And I am seventy-seven."**

**"And I have just come out of hospital."**

**"And I lay in there a hundred and ten days."**

He said:

**"And in those hundred and ten days I worked something out."**

---

And what he worked out he wrote on a sheet of paper, and he gave it to me.

And that sheet is in that envelope now (which by then would not hold everything — I had changed to a larger one).

---

And what is written on it is:

```
A thing's lifespan is a parameter.
A parameter has a value.
A value is fixed by somebody.
```

---

**Three lines.**

---

And he said:

**"He. I have spent my life digging things up."**

**"And every one of them has a lifespan."**

**"Trilobites, two hundred and seventy million years. Dinosaurs, a hundred and sixty. Genus Homo, two and a half."**

He said:

**"And I have never once asked who fixed an individual's lifespan."**

---

He said:

**"Because I thought entropy fixed it."**

**"And entropy is physics."**

**"And physics has no reasons."**

---

He said:

**"And now there is a thing down there that has reasons."**

---

**"Go and ask it."**

---

## The fourth descent

And the fourth descent was in April.

And this time I went down alone.

And this time I took something with me.

---

**I took Lu Ting's sheet of paper.**

---

And I took it not in order to show it (it does not need paper — it can measure the current in that laptop, 8.3).

**I took it because Lu Ting told me to.**

And what he said was:

"Take it. Have it with you when you ask."

I said, "Why."

He said:

**"Because you will want to change the wording."**

**"And with that paper in your hand, you will not."**

---

## Those eleven hours

And I was below eleven hours.

And this section is the first forty-one minutes.

---

## The first minute

And I went down, landed, stood, and opened the laptop.

And it came down.

And this time's parameters:

```
descent     6.3 m
duration    3 min 45 s
rate        2.8 cm/s
```

---

**Identical to 8.9.**

---

**And it came down to three point one metres, and stopped.**

---

## And the line I typed

And the line I typed is the first of Lu Ting's three.

And what I typed is:

```
A thing's lifespan is a parameter
```

---

**And I did not add a question mark.**

---

**And I did not add one not because I knew that rule (I did not know it then — that belongs to the epilogue).**

**Because there was no question mark on Lu Ting's sheet.**

---

## Its response

And its response came.

Elapsed:

```
0.7 s
```

---

And what it sent is one word.

And that word is:

```
Yes
```

---

## And in that moment

And in that moment I did the most important thing I did in those eleven hours.

**I did not ask the next question.**

---

**I waited.**

---

And how long I waited:

```
41 s
```

---

**And after those forty-one seconds, it sent a second time.**

---

**And that is the first time in three years it sent twice.**

---

## What it sent the second time

And what it sent is a line.

And I copy it:

```
41 to 110 years
```

---

**A range.**

---

And in that moment I typed a line.

And what I typed is:

```
What is that
```

---

And its response, elapsed 0.7 seconds.

And what it sent is four words:

```
That is the value
```

---

## And in that moment

And in that moment I sat down.

And I sat on that four point one degree floor.

---

**And I sat down not because my legs gave.**

**Because I had to write.**

---

**And I had to write because I realised that what came next I would have to get down without an error.**

---

## And what it said next

And what it said next I copy.

And I copy it in the order it sent, one at a time.

And what I typed in between I copy too.

---

**It:**

```
That is the value
```

**Me:**

```
Who fixed it
```

**It:**

```
We did
```

**Me:**

```
Why
```

---

## And after that *why*

And after that *why*, it stopped.

And it stopped for:

```
41 s
```

---

**And after those forty-one seconds it did a thing.**

**And that thing is the whole of this chapter.**

---

**It began to speak.**

---

## And *it began to speak*

And those four words must be made clear, because they are this section's title and they have a specific sense.

---

In the three years before, everything it sent took the form:

**one at a time.**

That is: it sends one, waits for one from me, sends one more.

---

**And this time not.**

---

**It sent nineteen in a row.**

**And I typed nothing in between.**

---

**And those nineteen are one whole thing.**

---

## And its register

And one thing here, and it is where this whole chapter's cruelty is.

---

**Its register changed.**

---

And *register*, in a thing that sends only text, how does it change.

---

And I must set out the three places.

---

**One: sentence length.**

Before, its sentences averaged three point one words.

**And those nineteen average nineteen.**

---

**Two: connectives.**

Before, it had not used a single connective (no *and*, no *so*, no *because*).

**And in those nineteen there are forty-one.**

---

**Three — and this one is the cruelty — it used a word that had not once appeared in the four thousand one hundred characters it had spoken in three years.**

---

And that word is:

```
good
```

---

## And that word

And it used that word in the nineteenth.

And that one I write at the end of this chapter (9.5).

---

**And here I write one thing only.**

---

**It believed it was reporting good news.**

---

## And the end of this section

And the last lines of this section.

I was sitting on that four point one degree floor.

And I had a pen and a notebook.

And I was writing.

---

**And on that laptop's screen, one at a time, words appeared.**

---

**And it was speaking.**

---

**And it had waited three hundred thousand years.**

**And now there was a person listening.**

---

**And what it had to say was a thing it held to be very good.**

---

**And in those forty-one minutes I did not interrupt it once.**

---

**And I did not interrupt not because I was listening.**

---

**Because my hand was writing, and my hand is faster than I am.**

---

**And in the thirty-seventh minute my hand stopped.**

---

**And my mind had not caught up.**

---

**And that is 9.6.**
