# 9.7 · She Runs Out

The sixth of April 2033, a Wednesday, eleven minutes past two in the afternoon.

---

## And I was at my mother's

And I was at my mother's.

---

```
1 hour 51 minutes
```

---

**And one hour fifty-one is the figure from 9.4.**

---

**And it is a coincidence.**

**And to this day I do not believe it is a coincidence.**

And we were shelling beans.

**And she was faster at it than I was.**

**And when I was a child she piled them to the left as well.**

And at nine minutes past two she said a thing.

And here I must stop.

## And that circle is not a pit

And that circle is not a pit.

---

**Because the floor of a pit is disordered.**

---

---

**It is concentric.**

## And here I must set down what I thought in that moment

And here I must set down what I thought in that moment, because it is the fastest piece of reasoning of my life.

**In 8.9.**

**And at the time I took it for a flower.**
