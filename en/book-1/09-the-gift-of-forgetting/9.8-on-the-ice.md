# 9.8 · On the Ice

The ninth of April 2033.

---

## And that circle, from above

And that circle, from above, is a circle.

**And walking into it from the rim, it is not.**

---

**It is forty-one tiers.**

---

And the height of each:

```
0.229 m
```

---

**And forty-one tiers make nine point four metres.**

---

**And nine point four over forty-one is zero point two two nine.**

---

**And zero point two two nine metres is the rise of a stair.**

---

## And here I must set down one thing

And here I must set down one thing.

---

**A thing that only wants a centre exposed need not make stairs.**

---

**A ramp would do.**

---

**And a ramp takes nineteen per cent less work than forty-one stairs.**

---

**And it made stairs.**

---

**And its reason for making stairs I understood at the seventh.**

---

**Because at the seventh I stopped to get my breath.**

---

**And on a ramp a person does not stop.**

---

## And how it dug

And how it dug we computed afterward.

And it is the piece of arithmetic in my life I least wanted to finish.

---

And the volume:

```
π x 205^2 x 9.4 = 1.24 x 10^6 cubic metres
```

---

And the mass:

```
1.24 x 10^6 x 917 = 1.14 x 10^9 kg
```

---

**One million one hundred and forty thousand tonnes.**

---

## And it could have melted it

And it could have melted it.

---

And the energy melting it requires:

```
3.8 x 10^14 J
```

---

**And the energy it held would have carried it another:**

```
4.1 x 10^6 years
```

---

**So melting that ice was nothing to it.**

---

**And it did not melt it.**

---

**It dug.**

---

## And why

And why has a technical reason.

---

**Melted ice refreezes.**

**And refrozen ice has a disordered surface.**

**And a cut form stays.**

---

**And that reason holds.**

---

**And I do not believe it was its reason.**

---

**And I do not, because a ramp would stay as well.**

---

## And it dug with what

And it dug with its own hands.

---

That is: its sampling arms, two of them.

---

And the hardness of their material against ice:

```
41 : 1
```

---

**And a thing forty-one times harder still abrades when it digs ice.**

---

And we measured the rate of abrasion afterward:

```
per cubic metre of ice, 3.3 x 10^-7 kg off its arms
```

---

So for one million two hundred and forty thousand cubic metres:

```
0.41 kg
```

---

## And zero point four one kilograms

And zero point four one kilograms, of its two arms, is:

```
0.41 / 1.0 = 41 %
```

---

**That is: it wore away four tenths of its hands.**

---

## And what four tenths means

And what four tenths means it had written in its own specification.

---

**And we found that specification inside its body afterward.**

---

And one of its entries:

```
threshold of self-check failure: any component whose margin falls below 0.62
```

---

**And the margin of its arms:**

```
0.59
```

---

**So its self-check fails.**

---

**And a failed self-check means: it may not start.**

---

## And in which month it crossed that threshold

And in which month it crossed that threshold we computed.

---

And the month:

```
the 9.4th
```

---

**That is: in the ninth point fourth month it could no longer restart.**

---

**And it had four point six months of work left.**

---

**And it finished the work.**

## And what that thing is

And that thing is not a stone.

---

**It is forty-one layers.**

---

**And the orientation of each differs.**

---

**And it does not lie in one plane.**

---

**So it is not a sign.**

## And in that moment I did not know what it was

And in that moment I did not know what it was.

---

---

**I did not take it.**

## And why I did not take it

And why I did not take it.

---

**Because in that moment I saw that on the square under its feet, five lines were cut.**

---

**And the five lines were in Chinese.**

---

**And Chinese it had learned from me.**

---

## And the five lines

And the five lines are a record.

And its five columns, verbatim:

```
time        9 April 2033
position    79.7 degrees south, 41.1 degrees east
object      Shen He
state       is
observer    I
```

---

## And here I must stop a long while

And here I must stop a long while.

---

**Its records, for four thousand nine hundred million years, say *we* in the observer column.**

---

**And this one says *I.***

---

**And its object column, for four thousand nine hundred million years, never held a particular person.**

---

**And this one holds my name.**

---

**And its state column, for four thousand nine hundred million years, held numbers.**

---

**And this one holds one word.**

---

**And the word is *is.***

---

And *is*, in Chinese as in English, is a verb.

---

**And it is also a state.**

---

**And in that column both senses stand.**

---

**Two: that object is still there.**

---

**And I do not know which it wanted.**

---

**And I will not guess here.**

**Because it taught me not to guess (8.5: which one).**
