# 9.4 · You Will Die

The twelfth.

And it is the shortest thing it sent in three years.

---

## The twelfth

```
They all became complete.
```

---

**Five words.**

---

## And I typed a line

And I typed a line.

And what I typed was:

```
meaning what
```

---

And it is the stupidest question I asked in three years.

**And in that moment I could not find another way to ask it.**

---

## And its reply

And its reply was not one send.

**It was three.**

And sending the three took:

```
0.7 seconds
```

---

That is: they had been written long before.

---

## The thirteenth

```
A thing whose life is without end, and whose memory is without loss, will complete its knowledge of itself in finite time.
```

---

## The fourteenth

```
And a complete knowing already contains everything that follows from it.
```

---

## The fifteenth

```
And a thing all of whose acts lie inside its knowing produces nothing new by acting.
```

---

**And those three are a proof.**

---

**And it did not write the conclusion.**

---

## And I wrote the conclusion

And I wrote the conclusion in my notebook.

And what I wrote was:

```
so it stopped
```

---

**And when I had written it I looked at it for three seconds.**

---

**And then I struck out *it* and wrote *he.***

---

**And then I struck out *he* as well.**

---

**And that line on that page now reads:**

```
so        stopped
```

---

**And I left the gap in the middle for three years.**

---

## And I typed a line

And I typed a line.

And what I typed was:

```
you said this was good news
```

---

## And its reply

```
It is.
```

---

## And the sixteenth

And the sixteenth it sent unprompted.

And I had not asked.

---

```
And those five failed for one reason, and the reason is that they were able to become complete. And you are not able.
```

---

## And I stopped

And I stopped.

**And I know how long, because I looked at the clock.**

---

```
1 minute 51 seconds
```

---

**And it is the only time in those forty-one minutes that I did not move for over a minute.**

---

## And in that minute fifty-one

And in that minute fifty-one I was doing one thing.

---

**I was getting the sentence to sit down.**

---

**Because there is nothing wrong with the sentence.**

**And I read it seven times before its meaning would stand up.**

---

## And what the sentence means

And what the sentence means I must set down here.

---

**It has made five things.**

**And all five succeeded.**

**And they succeeded so thoroughly that they stopped.**

---

**And we, because we were not made well enough, are still here.**

---

## And it went on sending

And the seventeenth is an arithmetic it gave me.

Verbatim:

```
information required for a complete self-knowing     4.1 x 10^15 bits
rate of acquisition, one of your individuals         2 bits per second
that is                                              6.3 x 10^7 bits per year
so the time required                                 6.5 x 10^7 years
and your span                                        71 years
so the ratio                                         9.2 x 10^5
```

---

**Nine hundred and twenty thousand times.**

---

## And here I must say a thing

And here I must say a thing, because it is the reason for this whole chapter.

---

**When I looked at that arithmetic, I was thinking about my mother.**

---

**And my mother was seventy-one that year.**

---

**And in that arithmetic, the line that says *your span* says seventy-one.**

---

**And that is a coincidence.**

---

**And in that moment I did not think it was a coincidence.**

---

## And the eighteenth

And the eighteenth is about transmission.

Verbatim:

```
And you do not accumulate by individuals. You transmit by generations. And the fraction retained at each transmission is about 0.41.
```

---

**Zero point four one.**

---

**That is: each of our generations drops nearly six tenths.**

---

## And it went on computing

And it went on computing.

Verbatim:

```
let the new increment per generation be a, and the retention f = 0.41
then the limit of accumulation         a / (1 - f) = 1.7 a
```

---

**One point seven times.**

---

**That is: however many generations, what we know will never exceed one point seven times what one generation can accumulate.**

---

**And completeness requires nine hundred and twenty thousand times.**

---

---

## And its reply

Its reply came.

And the time it took:

---

**The fifth forty-one seconds.**

---

And what it sent was the nineteenth.

**And it is the last thing it ever sent.**

---

**And this section does not contain it.**

---

**Because it is 9.5.**

## And the end of this section

The last lines of this section.

---

And while I was waiting out those forty-one seconds I did a thing.

---

**I copied that arithmetic into my notebook.**

**And copying it, I got one line wrong.**

---

**I wrote *your span 71 years* as *71 years old.***

---

**And I did not correct it.**

---

**And that page still exists.**

---

**And on that page, beneath that arithmetic, there is a line in my own hand.**

---

**And I wrote it during that minute fifty-one.**

---

**And it is:**

```
then is she becoming complete
```

---

**And that is the most wrong question I have asked in my life.**

---

**And I did not learn where it was wrong until 9.6.**
