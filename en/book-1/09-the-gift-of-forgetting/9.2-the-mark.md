# 9.2 · The Mark

The first through seventh of those nineteen.

---

## The first

```
Forty-one to a hundred and ten years is fixed by us. And fixing it is a design. And a design has a reason.
```

---

**Nineteen words.**

---

**And that is the longest thing it said in three years.**

---

## The second

```
And before that reason we must say a thing that comes before it. Because that thing is prior, and that reason comes from it.
```

---

## And it went on

And what it went on with is the fourth.

And that one is the core of this section.

And I copy it:

```

```

---

**The first through fourth.**

---

And in that moment I typed a line:

```

```

---

And its response, elapsed 0.7 seconds.

And what it sent:

```
A format identifier. And it is useless to you. And we knew it would be useless to you. And we wrote it by procedure.
```

---

## And in that moment

And in that moment I stopped writing.

And I stopped not because of those four positions.

**Because of *and we knew it would be useless to you.***

---

And why I stopped must be clear, because it is this chapter's first fissure.

---

**A thing doing something it knows to be useless.**

**And doing it because of procedure.**

---

**And that procedure is its own.**

---

**So it obeyed a rule it had made itself, and that it knew to be useless in this instance.**

---

**And I know that.**

---

**Because I have done it.**

---

**Forty-one days, thirty-two thousand words, the largest thing in my life made a subordinate clause in Appendix 3 (5.1).**

**And I knew that every word of that document was useless to the thing I had to say.**

**And I wrote it anyway.**

---

**Because that is the procedure.**

---

## And that is the third time in this book

**The first in the prologue, with no provenance.**
