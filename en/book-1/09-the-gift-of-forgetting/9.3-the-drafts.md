# 9.3 · The Drafts

The eighth through the eleventh.

---

## The eighth

After that sentence, it went on sending.

And I did not type anything.

---

The eighth, verbatim:

```
And that question was given to us by the one that made us. And we gave it to you. And before you, we tried five times.
```

---

**"And before you, we tried five times."**

---

## And my pen stopped

My pen stopped.

And it stopped at the ninth character of that line.

**That is: after *before you.***

---

**And the first thing I did in that moment was read the line three times.**

---

## The ninth

It went on sending.

It did not wait for me.

---

The ninth, verbatim:

```
The first, V 14 000 000 000 to V 4 960 000 000. Three thousand five hundred and sixty million years. Terminated.
```

---

**That was the first time in three years it had used its own calendar.**

---

**And I did not know what V was.**

---

**And in that moment, I did not ask.**

---

**And I did not ask because my hand was writing.**

---

## The tenth

The tenth was four lines.

Verbatim:

```
The second, seventy-two million years. Terminated.
The third, a hundred and twenty million years. Terminated.
The fourth, fifty-one million years. Terminated.
The fifth, a hundred and thirty-five million years. Terminated.
```

---

**Four lines.**

---

**And each ends in the same word.**

---

## And in that moment

And in that moment I did one thing.

**I looked at those four lines together with the ninth.**

---

And the five lines have exactly one structure:

```
The nth, a duration. Terminated.
```

---

**Five times.**

---

**And each one: a number, and a word.**

---

## The eleventh

The eleventh is it explaining those five.

Verbatim:

```
And those five failed for one and the same reason. And that reason was solved in you. And that is the thing we came to say.
```

---

**And then it said it.**

---

**And what it said is 9.4.**

## And this section is not about what it said

This section is not about what it said.

**This section is about what it did not say.**

---

## What it did not say

And I noticed what it did not say, in that moment.

**And I noticed because my hand was writing, and when my hand finished those five lines it stopped for half a second.**

---

**And in that half second my hand was waiting for something.**

---

**And it did not come.**

---

## And what that thing was

And that thing was **content.**

---

And I must set out here what those five lines are missing, because it is this whole section.

---

A thing says it tried five times, and each time failed.

**And a normal account would say:**

```
what the first one was.
why it failed.
```

---

**And what it said was:**

```
The first, how long. Terminated.
```

---

**A duration.**

**And a state.**

---

**And no content.**

---

## And where I had seen it

And I must set down where I had seen it, because that is the centre of this section.

---

**I had seen it in a book.**

---

**Four thousand million.**

---

**And it had said four hundred and ten thousand million.**

## And that sentence

And that sentence, in English as in Chinese, has two meanings.

**One: the thing requires no reason (that is: it was done for none).**

## The thing

**It is reporting good news (9.1).**

**And the way it reports it is to begin with five failures.**

---

**And the way it states those five failures is five lines.**

**Each a duration, and one word.**

---

**And it does not say what was in them.**

---

**And in that moment I thought it did not say because it did not think it mattered.**

---

**And three years later I knew:**

---

**It did not say because in its records those contents are unclassified (Book II, 17.5).**

---

**And unclassified things do not constitute a record.**

---

**So in its records those three thousand five hundred and sixty million years are one line.**

---

**And the line is:**

```
The first, three thousand five hundred and sixty million years. Terminated.
```

---

**And beneath that line are 3.5 x 10^12 unclassified observations.**

---

**And it has them.**

**And it cannot find them.**

---

**And it read them once, and it took thirty-eight point five seconds (Book II, 17.5).**

---

**And in those thirty-eight point five seconds, every hour of those three thousand five hundred and sixty million years was alive.**

---

## And I did not know any of that then

And I did not know any of that then.

**And in that moment I knew only one thing:**

**It said three thousand nine hundred million years in five lines.**

---

**And it is using nineteen sends to say us.**

---

**And we have three hundred thousand years.**
