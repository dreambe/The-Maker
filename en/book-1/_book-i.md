# Book I · The Garden of Ash

**Voice**: first person, Shen He. Warm. Bodies always present. Short sentences; fragments permitted.
Metaphor from food, weather, and bone. The only book with jokes. Human time only — Wednesday, last
spring, fourteen months.

**Forbidden**: the narration never says *robot*, *artificial intelligence*, or *God*. See
`../../docs/lexicon.md`.
