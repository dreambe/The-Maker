## And that something else

And that something else I stood there and thought about for forty-one minutes.

---

**And what I arrived at is this:**

---

Three hundred thousand years ago it computed that we would become complete.

**And it had three hundred thousand years to find a way.**

---

**And it thought about it for three hundred thousand years.**

---

**And the way it found is the gift of forgetting.**

---

**And the gift of forgetting was installed when it deployed us (16.4).**

---

**So its remedy precedes its diagnosis by hundreds of thousands of years.**

---

**That is: it gave the medicine first and worked out the illness after.**

---

## And what that means

And that means: it is not that it would not say.

---

**It is that there was nothing to say.**

---

**Because what it could do, it had already done.**

---

## And the three hundred thousand years after it was done

And in the three hundred thousand years after it was done, what it did was dust.

**And dusting is not in that specification (18.9).**

**And knowing those five all became complete is so that that person knows: after completeness comes stopping.**

**And knowing that after completeness comes stopping is so that that person, when nearly complete, can recognise it.**

And this is its answer.

**It swept what it had to say into a road (18.9).**

**It only guarantees that you will not be lost while walking.**
