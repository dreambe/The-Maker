# E.2 · The Quiet Withdrawal

2036 to 2041.

---

## There was no panic

There was no panic.

---

**I want that written first.**

---

**Because everybody assumed there would be panic.**

---

**And there was none.**

---

## It went like this

It went like this.

---

A man gets up in the morning.

And washes.

And eats.

And goes to work.

---

**And on the way, a thing occurs to him.**

---

**And the thing that occurs to him is: what happens when this is finished.**

---

**And he works it out.**

---

**And he gets an answer.**

---

**And he goes to work anyway.**

---

## And the next day

And the next day he works it out again.

---

**And what he gets is the same as yesterday.**

---

**And the third day, the same.**

---

**And on the forty-first day he did not go.**

---

## And what he did that day

And what he did that day.

---

**He sat at home.**

---

**And he was not unhappy.**

---

**And that he was not unhappy is the hardest part of this to write.**

---

## And here I must set out one thing

And here I must set out one thing.

---

**A person usually stops because they have run into something.**

---

**And this time, no.**

---

**This time it is because there was nothing in front of them any more.**

---

## And nothing is not the same as a wall

And nothing is not the same as a wall.

---

**A wall makes people angry.**

---

**And nothing makes people calm.**

---

## And its rate

And its rate was very slow.

---

**It was not the work of one year.**

---

**It was a little less every year.**

---

**And the little less each year was smaller than any amount a person can feel.**

---

## And this is why nothing stopped it

And this is why nothing stopped it.

---

**If a thing's change in one year is larger than a person's threshold of noticing, people go and stop it.**

---

**And this one is smaller.**

---

**So no year was the year.**

---

## And it has a shape

And it has a shape.

---

**And I had seen that shape in 18.7.**

---

**And 18.7 had not yet been read out.**

---

**And the shape is:**

```
1st to 2nd       41 seconds
2nd to 3rd       41 seconds
```

---

**And their interval was forty-one seconds.**

---

**And ours was forty-one days.**

---

**And they were eleven thousand and seventeen.**

---

**And we are eight thousand million.**

---

## And I will not do that multiplication

And I will not do that multiplication.

---

**And not doing it is the one act of will in this section.**

---

**And in the next section I will write why.**

---

## And the end of this section

And the end of this section.

---

**In the spring of 2041 I went to a place.**

---

**And the place is the building I did my graduate work in.**

---

**And in that building there is a laboratory.**

---

**And the door of that laboratory was open.**

---

**And the lights were on.**

---

**And an instrument was running.**

---

**And there was nobody inside.**

---

**And the instrument is a sequencer.**

---

**And the run it was on had eleven hours left.**

---

**And when it finished it would produce a set of data.**

---

**And that data would be written to a disc.**

---

**And that disc will hold for another forty-one years.**

---

**And nobody will read it.**

---

**And it will finish the run anyway.**

---

**And I stood in the doorway a long time.**

---

**And I did not go in.**

---

**And I did not turn off the light.**
