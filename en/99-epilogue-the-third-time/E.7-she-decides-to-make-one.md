# E.7 · She Decides to Make One

August 2043.

I am fifty.

---

## And I will not write why I decided

And I will not write why I decided.

---

**And this time it is not that I am withholding it.**

---

**It is that I do not have it.**

---

## And I checked

And I checked.

---

**And the way I checked is the only way I know.**

---

**I made a table.**

---

And the table is headed:

```
why I want to make one
```

---

And its rows:

```
41
```

---

## And those forty-one rows

And those forty-one rows I checked one by one.

---

And the test:

```
a reason holds
   when  the act of making one follows from it
```

---

And the number that hold:

```
0
```

---

**Zero.**

---

## And I must set down what those forty-one look like

And I must set down what those forty-one look like, because they are all wrong in the same place.

---

And the principal six:

```
one     to answer the question
two     so that humanity does not stop
three   so that its three hundred thousand years of waiting are not wasted
four    to fill in the third line for Lu Ting
five    to see whether it can be done
six     for my mother
```

---

## And the first

And the first: to answer the question.

---

**And this one I struck out first.**

---

**Because the question does not need answering (9.5: so that it could go on not being answered).**

---

**And making a thing to answer it is finishing the answer.**

---

**So this one contradicts the thing itself.**

---

## And the second

And the second: so that humanity does not stop.

---

**And this one I computed for forty-one minutes.**

---

**And its trouble is that it holds.**

---

**And that it holds is its trouble.**

---

## And I must set this down clearly

And I must set this down clearly, because it is the pivot of this whole epilogue.

---

If a thing's reason holds, then the thing follows from that reason.

**And a thing that follows has its consequence inside the knowing of the one it follows for.**

**And a thing inside a knowing produces nothing new by acting (9.4).**

---

**So anything with a reason that holds produces nothing new.**

---

**And a thing that produces nothing new is the same, done or not.**

---

## So I must have no reason

So I must have no reason.

---

**And that is not a fine phrase.**

---

**That is an engineering constraint.**

---

## And the other four

And the other four I struck out faster than the second.

---

**Three: not wasted. And *not wasted* is a repayment. And a repayment is computable. Struck.**

---

**Four: filling in Lu Ting's third line. And that is doing for a person a thing that person did not do. And that follows. Struck.**

---

**Five: to see whether it can be done. And this one comes nearest. And it is wrong at *see*. Because seeing is an observation. And an observation requires a result. Struck.**

---

**Six: for my mother.**

---

## And the sixth

And the sixth I did not strike out.

---

**And I did not strike it out because I could not.**

---

**And I could not, and not because it holds.**

---

**Because I cannot tell whether it holds.**

---

## And cannot tell

And *cannot tell* and *does not hold* are two things.

---

```
does not hold    I computed it, and the act does not follow
cannot tell      I cannot compute whether it follows
```

---

**And of those forty-one, forty are the first kind.**

---

**And the forty-first is the second.**

---

**And the forty-first is the sixth.**

---

## And here I did a thing

And here I did a thing.

---

**I struck that one out as well.**

---

**And striking it took eleven seconds.**

---

**And after I struck it, all forty-one rows on that table were struck.**

---

**And that table is now an empty table.**

---

**And its heading is still there.**

---

## And an empty table

And what an empty table means I learned in Book II.

---

**It is not *no reason.***

---

**It is *not applicable.***

---

**And *not applicable* admits two possibilities (18.8):**

```
one    no obtainable information exists here
two    the information here is not obtainable
```

---

**And I cannot separate the two.**

---

**Because separating them takes zero point four two of a level more.**

---

**And I do not have that zero point four two.**

---

**And I have something else.**

---

## And the something else I have

And the something else I have is this:

---

**I do not need to separate them.**

---

**Because whichever it is, what I am going to do is the same.**

---

**And that is the one thing it could never do.**

---

**It has to classify before it can act (17.5: unclassified things do not constitute a record).**

---

**And I do not.**

---

## And this is the thing

And this is the thing.

---

**I have decided to make one.**

---

**And I cannot compute why.**

---

**And that I cannot compute why is the only condition under which it can be done.**

---

**And this I do not intend to explain to anybody.**

---

**Because explaining it is giving a reason.**

---

**And giving a reason turns it into a thing that follows.**

---

**And that ruins it.**

---

## And on the eleventh of August 2043 I wrote one line

And on the eleventh of August 2043 I wrote one line.

---

**And I wrote it on a piece of paper.**

---

**And the paper is the envelope holding Lu Ting's note.**

---

**And I wrote on the back of the envelope.**

---

**And what I wrote is:**

```
The third line: I have filled it in. And what I filled it in with is not a word.
```

---

## And what I filled it in with

And what I filled it in with is E.8.

---

## And the end of this section

And the end of this section.

---

That night I pinned the empty table to the laboratory wall.

And beside it is a rota.

And on the rota there is one name.

---

**And the name is mine.**

---

**And that laboratory has forty-one benches.**

---

**And in use: one.**

---

**And it is the seventh.**

---

**And I will not write one in forty-one.**

---

**And when I sat down the room was very quiet.**

---

**And in the quiet there was one sound.**

---

**And it is the air conditioning.**

---

**And the air conditioning is holding this room at twenty-two degrees.**

---

**And twenty-two degrees is not for me.**

---

**It is for the thing in the room behind this one.**

---

**And in that room, at this moment, there is nothing yet.**
