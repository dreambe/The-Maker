# E.6 · Shen Mo Dies

This section is out of order.

---

## And I must say that first

And I must say that first.

---

**This epilogue runs forward.**

---

**And this section goes back to the fifteenth of March 2034.**

---

**And that is eight years earlier than the last one.**

---

## And why I have put it here

And why I have put it here.

---

**Because one person's death and a species' completeness are not on the same clock.**

---

**And I tried putting it in sequence.**

---

**And in sequence it became a thing that happened in the middle of other things.**

---

**And it did not happen in the middle of other things.**

---

## And the fifteenth of March was a Wednesday

And the fifteenth of March was a Wednesday.

---

**And it is the day she named forty-one times in three years.**

---

**And at last it was.**

---

**And she did not know.**

---

## And at eleven minutes past two that afternoon

And at eleven minutes past two that afternoon she woke once.

---

**And that time I was in the room.**

---

**And what happened that time is written in 9.9.**

---

**And 9.9 is my notebook.**

---

**And this section is not.**

---

## And she woke once more that day

And she woke once more that day.

---

**And that time was twenty past four in the afternoon.**

---

**And that time I was not in the room.**

---

**And I was downstairs.**

---

**And I was downstairs buying something.**

---

**And what I bought was tofu.**

---

## And the person in the room then

And the person in the room then was the carer.

---

**And her surname is Wang.**

---

**And she was fifty-six that year.**

---

**And she looked after my mother for two years and four months.**

---

**And her wages I paid.**

---

**And she cut my mother's nails forty-one times.**

---

## And she said my mother woke

And she said my mother woke.

---

**And my mother opened her eyes.**

---

**And my mother looked at the ceiling.**

---

**And then my mother said one thing.**

---

## And that thing was not said to me

And that thing was not said to me.

---

**And it was not said to Auntie Wang either.**

---

**And Auntie Wang says she did not know at the time who it was said to.**

---

**And she says my mother was looking at the ceiling.**

---

## And the thing

And the thing is:

```
Eat slowly, nobody's going to take it off you.
```

---

## And here I must stop

And here I must stop.

---

**I have heard that sentence.**

---

**And I have heard it many times.**

---

**And the last time I heard it was 2007.**

---

**And that year I was fourteen.**

---

## And who she said it to

And who she said it to.

---

**And it is the one thing in this whole book of which I am certain without needing to verify.**

---

**She said it to a five-year-old child.**

---

**And the child was eating.**

---

**And the child was eating too fast.**

---

**And the child was not me.**

---

## And not me

And not me.

---

**And I must set this down clearly, because it is the whole of this section.**

---

**That child is the child of 1998.**

---

**And I am this person of 2034.**

---

**And we are not the same person.**

---

**And we have one thing in common: the name.**

---

**And she had forgotten the name.**

---

## And I came back into the room at twenty to five

And I came back into the room at twenty to five.

---

**And there was a piece of tofu in my hand.**

---

**And Auntie Wang told me the sentence.**

---

**And she told me carefully.**

---

**And she was careful because she thought I would be sad.**

---

## And I was not sad

And I was not sad.

---

**And I must set down why not, because it is the thing in this book I am least sure I can set down.**

---

## And most people would be sad

And most people would be sad.

---

**Because if a person's last sentence is not addressed to you, that is a heavy thing.**

---

**And I computed the weight of it.**

---

**And computing it took two seconds.**

---

**And after two seconds I put it down.**

---

## And my reason for putting it down

And my reason for putting it down is very simple.

---

**Her sentence was said to a child who was eating.**

---

**And that child, at that moment, in her, was eating.**

---

**And a person with a child eating inside them is at ease.**

---

**And I wanted her at ease.**

---

**And she was at ease.**

---

**So what I wanted, I got.**

---

## And that is all of it

And that is all of it.

---

**It is not that I was not sad.**

---

**It is that the sadness and this were in the same second, and the second was larger.**

---

**And by how much, I do not compute.**

---

And she stopped at ten to five.

---

**And when she stopped I was at the bedside.**

---

**And Auntie Wang was in the doorway.**

---

**And the window was open.**

---

**And at ten to five in the afternoon on the fifteenth of March, the Beijing sky is still light.**

---

**And downstairs somebody was calling a child in to eat.**

---

**And the child did not answer.**

---

**And the person called again.**

## And the end of this section

And the end of this section.

---

**I put that piece of tofu on the kitchen windowsill.**

---

**And when I put it there I was not thinking about it.**

---

**And the next day it had gone off.**

---

**And I threw it away.**

---

**And when I threw it away I stood in front of the bin a long time.**

---

**And I stood there because in that moment I understood something.**

---

**And it is the thing I heard from an old man in a vegetable market seven years later (E.3).**

---

**And it is: a thing that goes off makes the person who made it come back the next day.**

---

**And at the time I did not know that was what I was thinking.**

---

**And at the time I knew one thing only.**

---

**I knew I would have to go and buy another piece tomorrow.**

---

**And tomorrow I would have nobody to feed.**

---

**And I went anyway.**
