# E.4 · Lu Ting Stops

November 2041.

He is seventy-seven.

---

## And he stopped differently from the others

And he stopped differently from the others.

---

**The others stopped going to work.**

---

**And he published the manuscript.**

---

## And the manuscript

And the manuscript had been in his drawer thirty years (3.4).

---

**And it was tied with a rubber band gone tacky.**

---

**And in October 2041 he cut the rubber band.**

---

**And he cut it because it would no longer come undone.**

---

## And what the manuscript is

And what the manuscript is I only learned then.

---

**It is not a paper.**

---

**It is a list.**

---

## And the list

And the list has one column.

And its rows:

```
4 100
```

---

And its content:

```
one species per row.
```

---

**And what those four thousand one hundred species have in common is a break in their fossil record.**

---

**And the length of every one of those breaks is under ten thousand years.**

---

**And ten thousand years, in strata, is a thin layer.**

---

## And why he kept this

And why he kept this.

---

**Because if there is a break between a species' vanishing and its appearing, that break is usually incompleteness of the record.**

---

**And incompleteness is the normal state.**

---

**So nobody attends to it.**

---

**And Lu Ting attended to it for thirty years.**

---

## And the way he attended

And the way he attended was to put them in order.

---

**And he ordered them by the depth of the break in the strata.**

---

**And having ordered them, he did a thing.**

---

**He looked at the intervals between those four thousand one hundred depths.**

---

## And those intervals

And of those intervals he drew a figure.

---

**And the figure is on page forty-one of the manuscript.**

---

**And I looked at that figure for one minute fifty-one.**

---

**And on that figure the intervals are not random.**

---

**They fall into five clusters.**

---

## And five

And five.

---

**And he finished that figure in 1993.**

---

**And in 1993 he wrote one line beneath it.**

---

**And the line is:**

```
Five times. Why five.
```

---

**And he was twenty-nine that year.**

---

**And he did not know what those five were.**

---

**And he knew that they were five.**

---

## And why he did not publish

And why he did not publish, he wrote on the last page of the manuscript.

---

**And what he wrote is:**

```
All four thousand one hundred rows of this list admit an explanation as undersampling.
And I cannot exclude that explanation.
So this list does not constitute a finding.
```

---

**Three lines.**

---

**And those three lines are correct.**

---

**And he was correct for thirty years.**

---

## And in 2041 those three lines changed

And in 2041 those three lines changed.

---

**Because by then those five had names.**

---

```
Draft I to Draft V
```

---

**And their termination dates came from its sends (9.3).**

---

**And the depths of the five clusters on his page forty-one we converted.**

---

**And the error of that conversion:**

```
0.41 %
```

---

## And in November 2041 he published it

And in November 2041 he published it.

---

**And he did not change a word.**

---

**And he added one page only.**

---

**And on that page is one line.**

---

**And the line is:**

```
May 1993. And at that time nobody had told me.
```

---

## And what that line means

And what that line means I must set out.

---

**He is not saying that he knew all along.**

---

**He is saying: a person, with strata only, with a trowel only, with thirty years only, can get there.**

---

**And that is what he wanted from his life.**

---

**And what he wanted was not the answer.**

---

**It was that the road itself goes through.**

---

## And on the eleventh day after publication he stopped

And on the eleventh day after publication he stopped.

---

**And the place he stopped is the desk in his house.**

---

**And he was sitting when he stopped.**

---

**And there was nothing in his hands.**

---

**And on the desk was a note.**

---

## And the note

And on the note is an inference.

---

**And the inference has three lines.**

---

**And I copy them:**

```
one     I did a thing I wanted to do thirty years ago.
two     I finished it.
three   —
```

---

**And the third line is a dash.**

---

## And a dash

And a dash, in its format, is *not applicable* (18.7).

---

**And Lu Ting did not know that format.**

---

**And he drew a dash.**

---

**And he drew it in pencil.**

---

**And the length of that dash:**

```
41 mm
```

---

## And I stood in front of that note a long time

And I stood in front of that note a long time.

---

**And what I was thinking about was not those three lines.**

---

**It was the thing he said in 3.4.**

---

**And the thing is: "No reason."**

---

**And that is why he kept the manuscript.**

---

**And thirty years later, why he published it, he gave a reason for.**

---

**And why he stopped, he gave a dash.**

---

## And the end of this section

And the end of this section.

---

I took the note with me.

And it is in my drawer now.

And I did not tie it with a rubber band.

---

**And what I used is an envelope.**

---

**And on the envelope I wrote one line.**

---

**And what I wrote is:**

```
The third line: I do not agree.
```

---

**And I do not agree because I have computed it.**

---

**And what I computed is whether a person who has finished what they wanted to do has nothing left to be done.**

---

**And the result I got is his.**

---

**And I do not agree anyway.**

---

**And disagreeing with a result one has computed oneself has no equivalent in its format.**

---

**And in mine it has.**

---

**And this whole epilogue is that entry.**
