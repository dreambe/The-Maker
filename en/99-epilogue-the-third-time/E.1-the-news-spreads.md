# E.1 · The News Spreads

It was not news.

---

## And news has one property

And news has one property: there is a day on which it is new.

---

**And this had no such day.**

---

## And this is how it came in

And this is how it came in.

---

The eleventh of April 2033, the first photograph.

**And on that photograph, a circle and forty-one tiers.**

**And that photograph was passed on twenty-three million times that week.**

---

And the eighteenth of April 2033, the first refutation.

**And it pointed out that synthetic-aperture radar generates artefacts.**

**And it was right.**

---

And May 2033, the second refutation.

**And it pointed out that those five lines of Chinese could have been cut by a person.**

**And it was right.**

---

And July 2033, the third.

**And it pointed out that a thing waiting three hundred thousand years under ice does not add up energetically.**

**And it was wrong.**

**And it was wrong because it had not done the xenon clathrates.**

**And doing them takes three months.**

---

## And this is the shape of those three years

And this is the shape of those three years.

---

**Not somebody announcing a thing and everybody believing it.**

---

**Every person walking, alone, one road that they themselves could check, all the way to its end.**

---

**And at the end of every one of those roads is the same place.**

---

## And what I did in those three years

And what I did in those three years was answer letters.

---

And their number:

```
41 000
```

---

**And of those, the number that asked me what the sentence was:**

```
one
```

---

**And all the rest asked how the checksum is computed.**

---

## And that one

And that one was from a fourteen-year-old.

---

**And what she asked was:**

```
The sentence it asked. What is it
```

---

**And I answered.**

---

**And what I sent was the sentence.**

---

**And I did not add a question mark.**

---

## And she answered

And she answered.

---

**And what she sent was:**

```
So did you answer it
```

---

**And I did not reply.**

---

**And at the time I thought I did not reply because I did not know how to answer.**

---

**And ten years later I knew: I did not reply because answering it would end it.**

---

## And three years later

And three years later, in April 2036, a thing happened.

---

**And the thing is that the Tracing Back stopped accepting new hypotheses.**

---

**And its reason is the first line of that notice:**

```
The objective of this project has been achieved.
```

---

**Nine words.**

---

## And those nine words

And those nine words I read many times.

---

**And there is nothing wrong with them grammatically.**

---

**And it is the first time this species has said that sentence.**

---

## And that day I did a thing

And that day I did a thing.

---

**I looked up a number.**

---

**And what I looked up was the ratio of new research proposals submitted worldwide that year to the year before.**

---

And the value:

```
0.41
```

---

## And I sat a long while looking at that number

And I sat a long while looking at that number.

---

**And I sat a long while because I had seen that shape before 18.6.**

---

**And 18.6 had not yet been read out.**

---

**And at that time nobody knew there was a Book II.**

---

**And I knew the shape.**

---

**Because it taught it to me:**

```
a knowing is complete
   when  every item it seeks to know has a value
```

---

**And thereafter: there is nothing to be done.**

---

## And that day I wrote a letter

And that day I wrote a letter.

---

**And the person I wrote to was the fourteen-year-old.**

---

**And she was seventeen by then.**

---

**And what I wrote was one line:**

```
Don't finish answering it yet.
```

---

**And she replied with two words:**

```
Too late
```

---

## And the end of this section

And the end of this section.

---

**It was not news.**

---

**It was water.**

---

**And water does not come into a house by the door.**

---

**It comes in through the walls.**

---

**And by the time you see the mark on the wall, there is no dry place left in the house.**
