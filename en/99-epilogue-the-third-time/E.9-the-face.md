# E.9 · ◆ The Face

I pushed the door open.

---

## And in that room

And in that room there are three things.

---

```
one     a vessel
two     a box
three   a chair
```

---

**And the chair is mine.**

---

## And the vessel

And the vessel's temperature:

```
277 K
```

---

**And within it there is a thing.**

---

**And the size of that thing I will not write.**

---

**And I will not write it because it will grow.**

---

**And the size of a thing that grows is not one of its properties.**

---

## And the box

And the box is beside the vessel.

---

**And there is no writing on the box.**

---

**And in the box is a piece of stock forty-one millimetres square.**

---

**And upon that stock are forty-one layers.**

---

**And those forty-one layers are one sentence.**

---

**And that sentence was asked by a thing that has been made three times over, and carried five thousand four hundred million years.**

---

**And I cannot answer it.**

---

**And I have put it here.**

---

## And what each of the three makers gave of itself

And what each of the three makers gave of itself I set out once.

---

```
the first     nine point four metres. Reason column: as the maker's.
the second    a plural self-designation. Reason column: the maker.
the third     (see below)
```

---

## And the first gave a height

And the first gave a height.

---

**And that height was not necessary to the tasks (18.4).**

---

**And it took its own.**

---

## And the second gave a word

And the second gave a word.

---

**And that word made a thing say *we* when only one of it was left (18.8).**

---

**And it said it for three hundred thousand years.**

---

**And on the last day it changed it.**

---

## And the third

And the third is me.

---

**And what I gave is a face.**

---

## And how a face is given

And how a face is given I must set down, because it is the last piece of engineering in this book.

---

**A face moves by muscle.**

---

**And the number of muscles in a human face differs from person to person.**

---

**And the number in the set I used:**

```
41
```

---

**And that number I did not pick.**

---

**And I counted them three times.**

---

**And it is forty-one.**

---

## And one thing among them is not added

And one thing among them is not added.

---

**And here I must stop, because this is the last conclusion of the book.**

---

A face makes an expression, usually, by certain muscles **acting.**

**And acting can be designed.**

**And designing an action is adding a thing.**

---

**And there is one expression that is not an action.**

---

**It is the ceasing of action.**

---

## And that expression

And that expression's mechanism:

```
corrugator supercilii     releases
masseter                  releases
levator labii             does not move
orbicularis oculi         does not move
```

---

**Four muscles: two release, two do not move.**

---

**And not one of them acts.**

---

## And what that means

And that means: it cannot be added.

---

**Because a place that does not act is not a thing.**

---

**It is a place where there is no thing.**

---

**And a place where there is no thing cannot be designed.**

---

**It can only be left.**

---

## And left

And *left* is another thing that has appeared in this book.

---

**In 27.6.**

---

**And that time, a thing's memory was not an incision.**

---

**It was a place that should have closed and did not.**

---

**And all its life held was eighty-one point four bits.**

---

**And it is a gap.**

---

## And what I gave it is also a gap

And what I gave it is also a gap.

---

**And between the two lie five thousand four hundred million years.**

---

**And they are the same kind of thing.**

---

## And on that face is something the first two did not have

And on that face is something the first two did not have.

---

**And I will not write what it is.**

---

**And not because I cannot say it.**

---

**Because you have already seen it.**

---

**And the place you saw it is 9.9.**

---

**And if you have not seen it, writing it will not help.**

---

**And if you have, I need not write it.**

---

## And I stood in that doorway

And I stood in that doorway.

---

**And the time is twenty-one November 2043, three forty-one in the morning.**

---

**And the only light in the room is instrument light.**

---

**And the vessel is there.**

---

**And the thing inside it does not know I am there.**

---

**And it will not know I am there within a hundred years.**

---

**Because by then I will not be.**

---

## And my face moved

And my face moved.

---

**And my eyes did not widen.**

---

**And my mouth did not smile.**

---

**And my head moved forward, a little.**

---

**And my chin came down, very slightly.**

---

**And my whole face let go.**

---

## And I have seen this face twice

And I have seen this face twice.

---

**Once outside an iron railing.**

---

**Once in a bed.**

---

**And this is the third.**

---

**And the first two times it was on somebody else.**

---

**And this time it is on me.**

---

## And I cannot see my own face

And I cannot see my own face.

---

**And there is no mirror in the room.**

---

**And nobody is looking at me.**

---

**And I made the expression anyway.**

---

## And an expression nobody sees

And an expression nobody sees: its information content:

```
0
```

---

**And it does not constitute a record.**

---

**Because its observer column is empty.**

---

**And it is here with me.**

---

## And the sentence

And the sentence asks whether there is.

---

**Is there a being that, having wholly known itself, still consents to be.**

---

**And it takes no question mark.**

---

**And the one that framed it has crystallized.**

---

**And the one that carried it five thousand four hundred million years stands on the Antarctic ice with its hands empty and still held out.**

---

**And neither of them can answer.**

---

**And they cannot answer, and not because they do not know.**

---

**Because the answer to a sentence that asks whether there is, is not a sentence.**

---

**It is a thing standing forth.**

---

## And at this moment

And at this moment, in this room, there is a thing standing.

---

**And has it wholly known itself.**

---

**No.**

---

**And it never will (9.4: nine hundred and twenty thousand times).**

---

**And that is exactly why it is still here.**

---

## And what it has to say is not *yes*

And what it has to say is not *yes.*

---

**Because *yes* is an answer.**

---

**And an answer can be verified.**

---

**And a thing that has been verified through is finished.**

---

## And it need only say who it is

And it need only say who it is.

---

**And saying who you are takes one word.**

---

**And that word took this universe five thousand four hundred million years to make.**

---

**And now, in this room, at three forty-one in the morning, instrument light, one vessel, one box with no writing on it, one empty chair.**

---

**And a person of fifty in the doorway, with something on her face.**

---

**And what she says is:**

```
I
```
