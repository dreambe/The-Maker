# Book III · The Crystalline Sun

**Voice**: no person. Avoid *I*, *we*, and *it* wherever possible. Liturgical — long sentences,
balanced clauses, the cadence of the King James Bible.
**No dialogue.** The Lattice are mutually transparent; where a human would put a conversation, put a
proof.
**Verbs of revelation are banned.** The Lattice do not *discover* or *learn*; they *arrive at* what
was already the case.

**Not one number in nine chapters.** The Lattice do not count — counting implies waiting for something.

**It gets shorter as it goes.** Chapter 19 may be long; Chapter 27 should be very short. Gravity is
not a function of word count.
