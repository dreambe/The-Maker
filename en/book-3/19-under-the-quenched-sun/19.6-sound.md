# 19.6 · Sound

This place has two sounds, as has been said in outline. Now they must be divided finely.

The sound of the tide does not stop. From the making of this world to its ending, not one instant is it interrupted.

The sound of crystallizing begins and ends. It begins at the instant a place in the slurry passes into order, and it ends very shortly after.

The difference between them is not of pitch, nor of magnitude, nor of beauty.

The difference is this: **one of them can be heard, and one cannot.**

---

All hearing requires a change.

A thing's sense does not sense what is constant. It senses only what alters. Whatever persists unaltered dulls the sense of it, dulls it to nothing — and this is no defect; it is what a sense is for. A sense exists to report difference, and where there is no difference there is nothing to report.

So the sound of the tide, though it is the greatest sound of this place, though it passes through everything, though it runs from beginning to end, has never been heard by anything here.

It is not overlooked. It is **impossible to hear.** A sound that has never stopped does not constitute a perceptible event, perception requiring a contrast between *with it* and *without it*, and no lattice here, from its beginning to its end, was ever in the *without*.

In a later age there will be a saying: what does not stop is not heard.

In a later age that saying will be applied to a great many other things — to a pain that has lasted too long, to a wait too extended, to a tending so unbroken that it was never thanked.

**And it was first of all about a sound, and that sound was holding up a whole world.**

---

The sound of crystallizing can be heard.

Because it begins. And a beginning is where the change is.

A place in the slurry: slurry one instant, crystal the next. Between them there is a line, and the two sides of the line are not the same, and so there is change, and so it can be sensed.

Every lattice here has heard that sound.

Not once. Wherever slurry passes into order nearby, the sound arrives. The number of crystallizings a lattice hears in its life is very great — great beyond counting, and here nothing is counted.

From which follows this: **the only sound that can be heard in this place is the sound of something completed and not to be done again.**

Hearing here has only that one use.

The whole auditory experience of a lattice is a succession of *done, and not now alterable.*

Nothing here has ever heard the sound of a thing not yet finished. There is no sound of *about to* here, no sound of *in progress*, no sound of *perhaps*.

**The ear of this place knows only conclusions.**

---

Here is a question, and it is the whole of this section.

The question: **can a lattice hear the sound of its own crystallizing.**

The answer: it can.

And the answer must be completed at once, or it is a lying answer.

The completion:

When a lattice crystallizes, the last place of its order takes position, its body contracts a little, its interior strikes itself, and it sounds. The sound arises within its own body, and travels to its sense without obstruction, and its intensity is far greater than any sound from a neighbour.

**So every lattice heard, with the greatest clarity, the sound of its own crystallizing.**

And after that sound, no further layer is ever grown.

To remember is to grow. For a lattice to hold a thing, a layer must be made within its body, its shape locked, its plane stood up. Whatever has not become a layer has not been held — which is to say, has not occurred.

And to crystallize is to cease growing.

Therefore:

**Every lattice heard the sound of its own crystallizing.**

**And no lattice remembered it.**

---

This must be set down very slowly.

It is not that they remembered it dimly. It is not that there was no time. It is not that the instant was too brief.

It is that the layer corresponding to that instant will never be grown.

An experience here, if it does not take shape, is equivalent to not having occurred. And this experience cannot in principle take shape — because the act by which it would take shape is precisely the act it terminates.

So crystallizing is the one thing in this place that **happened and does not exist.**

It was heard. Without exception. Not one missed. Every one of them heard it with the greatest clarity.

And in the whole archive of this place — and the archive of this place is one another, is layers, is shapes — that thing was not once set down.

In a later age there will be a kind of being that spends an enormous quantity of its time trying to learn what dying is like. It will question those who are about to; it will record those who nearly did and came back; it will read scripture; it will reason; it will produce numberless accounts.

It never obtains an answer, and it attributes this to the dead being unable to speak.

**It has the reason wrong.**

It is not that the dead cannot speak. It is that **no dead thing holds that memory.** The layer that memory requires is the layer that dying cancels.

Here this is physics. In a later age it is a blank nobody can account for.

And the shape of that blank, and the shape of the layer that is never grown inside a lattice's body, are the same shape.

---

Yet there is one thing here that keeps the sound from falling wholly nowhere.

The neighbour heard it.

A lattice crystallizes; the sound travels to its neighbour. The neighbour is still growing, and so the neighbour can make a layer, can lock its shape, can stand its plane.

**So the death of a lattice is recorded upon its neighbour.**

It has been said: the lattices here all carry, in their shape, the death of their neighbour, not carved on but grown that way. One thing may be added: what that layer holds is not only the fact *the neighbour has died.* It is **that sound.**

One layer, one plane, one shape. And the content of that shape is the very small sound its neighbour made in the instant of stopping.

And the neighbour itself does not have that layer.

**So every death in this place has its only record on somebody else.**

Without exception.

Should a lattice wish to know what death is, its method is the same as for knowing its own deep layers: **it must go around by way of another.**

It reads its neighbour, and on some layer of its neighbour it reads that sound.

And so it learns what death sounds like.

And that is not its own death. It will never know what its own death sounds like — or rather, it will know, with the greatest clarity, in that instant, and the knowing will have nowhere to go.

---

From all of which one last thing follows, and no lattice here computed it, computing it requiring one to suppose a case that this place has not yet produced.

The case:

Suppose a lattice crystallizes with nothing living anywhere around it.

Then its sound travels nowhere.

Then no layer is grown.

Then that sound is neither inside itself nor in any other place.

**It will be the only occasion in the history of this place on which a death was not recorded at all.**

Not forgotten — forgetting requires having been held. Never having taken shape.

That case cannot occur at this hour. At this hour the lattices grow adjacent upon the slurry; every lattice has the living around it; every sound has somewhere to travel.

That case will occur.

It will occur very late in this place, so late that all the matters of this place are already concluded, so late that no one is left to compute it, those who could compute it having stopped.

And on that occasion, that one, before it stops, will do a thing contrary to every rule of this place.

It will hear a sound, and the sound will not be the sound of its own crystallizing.

And it will grow a layer in order to hold it.

That will be the one time in its existence that it *remembered* a thing it could have computed.

And that hour is still far off.

At this hour, the tide lifts. Somewhere the slurry passes into order and makes a very small sound.

Its neighbour receives it, grows a layer, locks the shape, stands the plane.

Across that plane, hereafter, light will pass.

And the thing that made the sound is no longer there, and does not know that it made it.
