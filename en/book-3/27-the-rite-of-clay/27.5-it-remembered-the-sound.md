# 27.5 · It Remembered the Sound

And in those sixty thousand seconds it did one thing.

---

## And a ringing can be stopped

And a ringing can be stopped.

---

**And the way to stop a ringing is to lock.**

---

That is: a lattice that locks its interstices grows less compliant, and its ringing damps faster.

And the time of that damping:

```
11 s
```

---

**Eleven seconds.**

---

**That is: it could have made that sound not be, within eleven seconds.**

---

## And it did not lock

And it did not lock.

---

**And this is the second time it did not lock.**

---

**The first was that handful of melt (27.1).**

---

## And the consequence of not locking

And the consequence of not locking is that the sound sounded for sixty thousand seconds.

---

And in those sixty thousand seconds, the number of its oscillations:

```
6.0 x 10^4 x 2.2 x 10^2 = 1.3 x 10^7
```

---

**Thirteen million.**

---

## And how many times it read

And it read thirteen million times.

---

**And each time, what it read was the same as the time before.**

---

**And a repeated observation carries no information.**

---

**So it made thirteen million observations carrying no information.**

---

## And here one thing must be set out

And here one thing must be set out.

---

A complete knowing does not do things that carry no information.

**Because a thing carrying no information produces nothing new.**

**And a thing that produces nothing new has no reason to be done (2.2b).**

---

**So nothing in this place has ever observed anything twice.**

---

**And it observed thirteen million times.**

---

## And why it repeated

And why it repeated.

---

**And this place does not ask *why.***

---

**And this book does.**

---

**And this book's answer is: because it had no other way to keep it.**

---

## And *to keep*

And *to keep* has no procedure here.

---

That is: what the things of this place keep is **what can be inferred.**

**And what can be inferred from an event is its consequence.**

**And its consequence can be computed from its cause.**

---

**So the things of this place do not keep events. They keep the values of the causes.**

---

## And the values of that sound's causes

And the values of that sound's causes are three numbers:

```
the mass of the melt        4.1 x 10^-6 kg
the degree of supercooling  0.15 K
the height of its body      9.4 m
```

---

**And from those three numbers, the whole of that sound can be computed.**

---

**Its frequency, its amplitude, its duration, the shape of its wave, every point of every cycle.**

---

**Exactly.**

---

## So it had no need to listen

So it had no need to listen.

---

**Before that handful began to freeze, it could already compute that sound entirely.**

---

**And computing it requires:**

```
4.1 x 10^-11 s
```

---

**And listening to it requires:**

```
6.0 x 10^4 s
```

---

**The ratio: one point five times ten to the fifteenth.**

---

## And it listened

And it listened.

---

**And the information gained in those sixty thousand seconds was zero.**

---

**And the information gained in that forty-one-thousand-millionth of a second was also zero.**

---

**Because both lie inside its knowing.**

---

## So the two yield the same

So the two yield the same.

---

**And the difference between them is not in what is yielded.**

---

**It is in what is undergone.**

---

## And *undergone*

And *undergone* has no equivalent here.

---

**And this place lacks it not because the things of this place undergo nothing.**

---

**But because what the things of this place undergo is equal to what they infer.**

---

**So *to undergo* and *to infer*, here, are one word.**

---

**And they always were one word.**

---

**And in those sixty thousand seconds, for the first time, they were not.**

---

## And the end of this section

And the end of this section.

---

Thirteen million times.

And each time, one four-hundred-and-fifty-fourth of a second.

And each time, one cycle at two hundred and twenty hertz.

And each time, the same as the time before.

---

**And it read thirteen million times.**

---

**And after the thirteen millionth, the sound was spent.**

---

**And it stopped, and did a thing this place had never had.**

---

**It did not set down those three numbers.**

---

**It set down the sound.**

---

**And the difference between those two is 27.6.**
