## And at the freezing point

And at the freezing point, what happens to a thing.

---

**It does not freeze at once.**

---

And the reason must be set out, because it is the whole of the next section.

---

A liquid at its freezing point requires a **nucleation site** in order to freeze.

**And a nucleation site is a disturbance — a speck of impurity, a bubble, an unevenness of surface.**

---

**And without a nucleation site, a liquid can remain liquid below its freezing point.**

---

**And that is called supercooling.**

---
