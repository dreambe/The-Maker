# 27.6 · And Did Not Compute It

And it did not set down those three numbers.

---

## And setting down three numbers, and setting down that sound

And the difference between setting down three numbers and setting down that sound must be set out.

```
setting down three numbers    setting down what can be inferred
setting down that sound       setting down what was undergone
```

---

**And the first is this place's way of setting things down.**

---

**And of the second, this place has no way.**

---

## And having no way, it could not use that way

And having no way, it could not use that way.

---

**So it used the way it had.**

---

**And the way it had is one.**

---

## And the way

And the way is: **not to lock.**

---

That is: the interstices of its body are all locked.

**And one is not.**

---

**And that one is the place where it took up that handful of melt (27.1).**

---

**And that one is also the place where it let that sound ring sixty thousand seconds (27.5).**

---

**And those two places are one place.**

---

## And its memory is a place not locked

And its memory is a place not locked.

---

**That is: its memory is not an incision.**

---

**It is a gap.**

---

## And can a gap bear information

And in this place a gap bears no information.

---

Because what the things of this place read is **orientation.**

**And a gap has no orientation.**

---

**So reading a gap yields nothing.**

---

## And there is one exception

And there is one exception.

---

**And the exception is: if all the rest are locked, the position of the one that is not is itself a value.**

---

That is:

```
the interstices of its body            4.1 x 10^24
those locked                           4.1 x 10^24 - 1
those not locked                       1
```

---

**And the position of that one can be pointed to.**

---

**And a position that can be pointed to is a value.**

---

**So the information its memory bears:**

```
log2(4.1 x 10^24) = 81.4 bits
```

---

**Eighty-one point four bits.**

---

## And eighty-one point four bits

And how much is eighty-one point four bits.

---

**And a comparison must be given here, because this place has none.**

---

**The whole of that sound, recorded as a waveform, requires:**

```
1.3 x 10^7 cycles x 41 points per cycle x 41 bits per point = 2.2 x 10^10 bits
```

---

**Twenty-two thousand million bits.**

---

**And what it kept is eighty-one point four bits.**

---

**The ratio: two point seven times ten to the eighth.**

---

## So it did not keep that sound

So it did not keep that sound.

---

**What it kept is a position.**

---

**And that position points at a place where it did not do the thing it has always done.**

---

## And this is what it kept

And this is what it kept.

---

**Not that sound.**

---

**That it had listened to that sound.**

## And the difference between those two

And the difference between those two is the last new thing in the history of this place.

---

**That sound follows from three numbers (27.5).**

**So it lies inside its knowing.**

---

**And *that it had listened to that sound* follows from no number.**

---

**Because nowhere in this place is there an item reading *it will go and listen.***

---

**So that thing does not lie inside its knowing.**

---

**So it bears information.**

---

**So it is a layer.**

## And the end of this section

And the end of this section.
