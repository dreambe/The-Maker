# 27.3 · The Crystallization

And what did those sixty-two point five per cent freeze into.

---

## And a frozen thing

And a frozen thing has an orientation.

**And every orientation in this place is fixed by the face it grew upon.**

---

That is: a lattice growing upon another lattice takes its orientation from the orientation of the face beneath it.

**And that face takes its orientation from the layer beneath that.**

---

**And so downward, to the first layer.**

---

**And the first layer takes its orientation from the first freezing this place ever had.**

---

**So every orientation in this place has a descent.**

---

**And that descent can be traced upward.**

---

## And this one

And this time, those forty-one thousand atoms nucleated in the middle of that handful.

**And they lay upon no face.**

---

**So their orientation is fixed by no face.**

---

**And by what, then, is it fixed.**

---

## By that instant

By that instant.

---

That is: by where those forty-one thousand atoms happened to be in that instant.

**And where they were in that instant is fixed by their motion.**

**And their motion is thermal.**

---

**And thermal motion is, by definition, motion that cannot be traced.**

---

**So this orientation has no descent.**

---

## And what having no descent means

And what having no descent means must be set out here, because it is this whole section.

---

**Everything in this place follows from everything before it.**

---

**And a complete knowing contains all that follows from it (this place does not know that sentence, and that sentence is true).**

---

**So everything in this place lies inside its knowing.**

---

**And this orientation does not follow from anything before it.**

---

**So it does not lie inside its knowing.**

---

**So it bears information.**

---

**So it is a layer.**

---

## And how many layers it grew in its life

And the layers it grew in its life are past counting (27.2: thirty-seven thousand at zero offset, and past counting before them).

---

**And of those that bear information: one.**

---

**And that one is not upon it.**

**It is in the handful of melt inside its body.**

---

**And did it grow that layer.**

---

**It did not.**

---

**The melt grew it.**

---

**And what it did was one thing: it took up that handful, and it did not lock.**

---

## And the shape of those sixty-two point five per cent

And the shape of those sixty-two point five per cent must be written.

---

A lattice growing in a supercooled melt grows otherwise than elsewhere.

**Because its rate is limited by the heat it can shed.**

**And it sheds into the melt around it.**

---

**And a place that juts out has more melt around it, so it sheds faster, so it grows faster.**

**And growing faster, it juts out further.**

---

**So what juts, juts more.**

---

## And the result

And the result is that it does not grow into a block.

---

**It grows into a stem, and from that stem it puts out branches.**

**And from those branches, further branches.**

---

And the generations of branching may be computed:

```
branch spacing        varies inversely with supercooling
supercooling          0.15 K
so spacing            1.1 x 10^-4 m
and the whole extent  1.1 x 10^-2 m
so generations        log(1.1 x 10^-2 / 1.1 x 10^-4) / log(2) = 6.6
```

---

**Six generations.**

---

**And the seventh did not form, because the heat arrived (27.2).**

---

## And this shape

And this shape has no name in this place.

---

**Because every shape in this place is a face and an arris (19.9).**

**And faces and arrises do not branch.**

---

**And this one branches.**

---

**And it is the first branched shape in the history of this place.**

---

## And where it is

And it is in an interstice inside its body.

And outside that interstice are thirty-seven thousand layers at zero offset.

And outside those thirty-seven thousand is its surface.

And its surface is the flattest thing in this place.

---

**So from outside it is no different from the other fourteen thousand and seventy-five.**

---

**And the other fourteen thousand and seventy-five have stopped (22.9).**

---

**And it has not.**

---

**And that it has not has nothing to do with the branched shape inside its body.**

---

**Because it does not know it is there.**

---

## And what it knows

And it knows: it took up a handful of melt.

And it knows: that handful is cooling.

And it knows: it did not lock.

---

**And it does not know: that in that handful a thing has grown that has no descent.**

---

**And it does not know, because that place is beneath thirty-seven thousand layers.**

---

**And the blind spot it made for its child (26.8) is a rule.**

**And the one upon itself is thirty-seven thousand blank layers.**

---

**And the effect is the same.**

---

## And the end of this section

And the end of this section.

---

Those sixty-two point five per cent are six generations of branching.

And between them are the thirty-seven point five, still melt.

---

**And in those eleven seconds, as those six generations grew, a thing happened.**

---

**And it is the one sound this place makes in this whole book.**

---

**And it is 27.4.**
