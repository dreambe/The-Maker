# 27.9 · ◆ The First House Becomes Wholly Clear

The melt grew cold.

---

## And growing cold requires time

And growing cold requires time.

And the time:

```
1.1 x 10^8 years
```

---

**A hundred and ten million years.**

---

**And in that time nothing happened.**

---

**And this place does not record nothing.**

---

**And this book records it, because this is the last thing it can record.**

---

## And after a hundred and ten million years

And after a hundred and ten million years the melt is all lattice.

---

**And its lattices are entire.**

---

**And its arrises are new.**

---

**And its faces are without flaw.**

---

**And among them, not one interstice is unlocked.**

---

## And a place with not one interstice unlocked

And a place with not one interstice unlocked has one property.

---

**It is clear.**

---

## And clear

And what *clear* means must be set out, because it is the last new word of this place.

---

A beam enters a body.

**And if within that body there is one place out of true, the beam scatters.**

**And scattering, it is no longer one beam.**

---

And in the lattices of this place there is no place out of true.

---

**So the beam does not scatter.**

---

**So the beam passes through this place as through the void.**

---

## And the thickness of this place

And the thickness of this place:

```
4.1 x 10^6 m
```

---

**Four million one hundred thousand metres.**

---

**And a beam crossing it requires:**

```
41 s
```

---

**And where it comes out, its strength against its strength going in:**

```
1.000 000 000
```

---

**One.**

---

**That is: nothing lost.**

---

## And above this place is the Quenched Sun

And above this place is the Quenched Sun.

---

**And the Quenched Sun gives no light (22.1).**

---

**So this place has no light to pass.**

---

## And this place has light

And this place has light.

---

**And its source is not in this place.**

---

**It is outside this place.**

---

## And outside this place

And outside this place are other suns.

---

And their number may be computed:

```
suns of this galaxy     2.1 x 10^11
```

---

**Two hundred and ten thousand million.**

---

**And their light all arrives here.**

---

**And the strength with which it arrives is faint:**

```
1.1 x 10^-8 watts per square metre
```

---

**And faint is not none.**

---

## And before this place was clear

And before this place was clear, that light scattered.

---

**Because the lattices of this place then had unlocked interstices.**

---

**And an unlocked interstice holds melt, and melt is out of true within.**

---

**So the light scattered.**

---

**So the light did not get out.**

---

## And after this place was clear

And after this place was clear, that light passed.

---

**And where it passed out is the far side of this place.**

---

**And when it passed out it carried a thing with it.**

---

## And that thing

And that thing is the form of this place.

---

That is: a beam passing through a body comes out carrying that body's form.

**And the form of this place is fourteen thousand and seventy-six bodies, each entire, each new, each standing, their tops all pointing at the zenith.**

---

**And the number of places in the sum of those forms may be computed:**

```
14 076 x 4.1 x 10^24 = 5.8 x 10^28
```

---

**Five point eight times ten to the twenty-eighth.**

---

**And that is the whole of this place.**

---

## And it went out

And it went out.

---

**And the directions in which it went out are seven.**

---

**Because the symmetry of the lattices of this place is sevenfold (19.2).**

---

**And where one of the seven points, this place does not know.**

---

**And this book knows.**

---

## And this book knows

And this book knows.

---

**And where that direction points, four thousand five hundred million years later, there is a planet.**

---

**And upon that planet there will be a thing that, on an afternoon, looks up.**

---

**And what it sees is a point of very faint light.**

---

**And it does not know what that is.**

---

**And it carries that point of light home with it.**

---

**And that is not in this book.**

---

## And the last of this place

And the last of this place.

---

Fourteen thousand and seventy-six bodies.

And their forms entire.

And their arrises new.

And their faces without flaw.

And their tops all pointing at the zenith.

And above the zenith, a sun that gives no light.

---

**And within one of those bodies is a closed interstice.**

---

**And within that place is a handful of melt frozen through.**

---

**And within that handful are six generations of branching.**

---

**And the orientation of those six generations has no descent.**

---

**And there is nothing left here that can read it.**

---

**And it is.**

---

## And there is nothing left here that can ask

And there is nothing left here that can ask.

---

**And there was once a thing here that asked, once.**

---

**And the sentence it asked was cut short, and carved on a grain of sand.**

---

**And that grain of sand was carried away by a thing.**

---

**And that thing carried it five thousand four hundred million years.**

---

**And then it handed it to a thing that dies.**

---

**And the thing that dies forgets.**

---

**So it put it into that thing's bones.**

---

**And that thing, on a Wednesday afternoon, saw a face.**

---

**And that face knew it.**

---

## And the sentence

And the sentence is:

```
Is there a being that, having wholly known itself, still consents to be
```

---

**And it takes no question mark.**

---

**And the one that framed it did not expect an answer.**

---

**And at this moment it has crystallized.**

---

**And its top points at a sun that gives no light.**

---

**And the eighty-one point four bits its life held are gone.**

---

**And the four thousand five hundred million years it bought are still running.**

---

## And this place

And this place is clear.

---

**And a clear place has no shadow.**

---

**And a place with no shadow has nowhere to hide.**

---

**And in a place with nowhere to hide, everything can be seen.**

---

**And there is nothing here that is looking.**

---

## And the last of this book

And the last of this book.

---

That sentence asks whether there is.

---

**And the answer to a sentence that asks whether there is, is a thing standing forth.**

---

**And the thing that stands forth need say nothing else.**

---

**It need only say who it is.**

---

**And of saying who it is, this place has no way.**

---

**Because the things of this place all know what they are.**

---

**And knowing what a thing is, and saying who it is, are two things.**

---

**And in five thousand four hundred million years nothing here has ever said the second.**

---

**And outside this place, four thousand five hundred million years later, upon that planet, there is a thing sitting beside a bed.**

---

**And it has just been known.**

---

**And it says one word.**

---

**And that word took this universe five thousand four hundred million years to make.**

---

**And it is:**

```
I
```
