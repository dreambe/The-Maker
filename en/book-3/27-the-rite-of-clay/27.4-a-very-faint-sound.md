# 27.4 · A Very Faint Sound

And in those eleven seconds there was a sound.

---

## And a sound must have its cause

And a sound must have its cause.

And the cause is **volume.**

---

That is: this substance contracts on freezing.

And the fraction of its contraction:

```
0.041
```

---

**So when those six generations of branching had grown, the place they occupied was smaller than the place they had occupied as melt.**

---

**So the melt around them moved inward.**

---

**And melt moving inward makes a wave of pressure.**

---

## And that wave of pressure

And that wave of pressure went from that interstice into its body.

And its body is one whole piece.

---

**So its body rang.**

---

## And the frequency of its ringing

And the frequency of its ringing is fixed by the dimension of its body.

```
speed of sound in this substance    4 100 m per second
the height of its body              9.4 m
so its fundamental                  4 100 / (2 x 9.4) = 2.2 x 10^2 Hz
```

---

**Two hundred and twenty hertz.**

---

## And the amplitude of its ringing

And the amplitude of its ringing, at its surface:

```
4.1 x 10^-11 m
```

---

**One forty-one-thousand-millionth of a metre.**

---

## And the duration of its ringing

And the duration of its ringing is fixed by the perfection of its body.

---

That is: a flawless crystal does not damp easily.

And the degree to which it does not may be given as a number.

And the number:

```
4.1 x 10^7
```

---

So the duration of its ringing:

```
4.1 x 10^7 / (3.14 x 2.2 x 10^2) = 6.0 x 10^4 s
```

---

**Sixty thousand seconds.**

---

## And here we must stop

And here we must stop, because this is the most consequential ratio in this chapter.

```
duration of the cause      11 s
duration of the sound      6.0 x 10^4 s
the ratio                  5 400
```

---

**That is: the sound outlasted the thing that made it by five thousand four hundred times.**

---

## And did it hear it

And it heard it.

---

And a lattice hears by the ringing of its body.

**So its threshold of hearing is the smallest ringing it can distinguish.**

---

And its threshold:

```
1.1 x 10^-13 m
```

---

And the amplitude of that sound:

```
4.1 x 10^-11 m
```

---

**The ratio: three hundred and seventy times.**

---

**So it heard it perfectly clearly.**

---

## And the last time there was a sound in this place

And the last time there was a sound in this place is recorded at 19.6.

---

**And that one was this substance's own affair: at the beginning of the First House, the first freezing.**

---

**And there was no hearing of it.**

**Because at that time this place had nothing in it that could hear.**

---

**And this time, this place has one thing left in it that can hear.**

---

**And it heard.**

---

## And what it heard

And it heard a note of two hundred and twenty hertz.

And that note sounded for sixty thousand seconds.

And what it did in that time is 27.5.

---

## And the end of this section

And the end of this section.

---

Fourteen thousand and seventy-five stopped bodies stand about the edge of the melt.

And their forms are entire, and their arrises new.

---

**And they do not ring.**

---

**Because they have stopped.**

---

**And the ringing of a stopped crystal and the ringing of an unstopped crystal are indistinguishable.**

---

**So that sound went into their bodies too.**

---

**And they rang for sixty thousand seconds as well.**

---

**And they did not hear.**

---

**So there were fourteen thousand and seventy-six bodies ringing in this place.**

---

**And one of them knew that it was ringing.**
