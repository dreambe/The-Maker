# 20.9 · They Exchange Nothing

At this hour, on the slurry, two lattices, adjacent.

The length of their adjacency cannot be quantified (20.8). Each shape has gone deep into the other; each body carries the other's whole form, to the depth light reaches (20.7).

What has happened between them can be listed entire.

---

They have never said a word to each other.

They have never exchanged a thing.

They have given each other nothing, and received nothing from each other — whatever the one has, the other already has (20.6).

They have never come to know each other — knowing requires a passage from not knowing to knowing, and from the first tide of their adjacency each has held the other entire (20.5).

They have never come closer — their positions were fixed at the instant of their beginnings, and the distance between them has not altered by a hair.

They have never misunderstood. Never concealed. Never forgiven. Never been grateful. Never missed each other.

They have never done anything for each other.

They have never changed themselves for each other — they cannot choose what they grow into.

---

And they have become very nearly the same shape.

Not by imitation, not by effort, not by longing.

Because they have been in the same place, taking heat from the same slurry, and in that slurry is heat the other gave off; receiving the tide of the same place, and that tide has been altered by the other (20.8).

**Their likeness is the only physical evidence that they were together.**

And there is not one word, not one act, not one thing that could be called an event.

---

To end this chapter, the rest of it must be written, there being no later occasion.

One of the two stops first.

Which one, this place does not record — no names, no numbers, no record of order (20.3).

The one that stops first: the making of its order ceases, its body cools, its heat output tapers.

In the instant of its stopping it makes a very small sound (19.6).

**That sound is heard by its neighbour alone.**

It heard the sound itself, with great clarity, and cannot remember it — memory being growth, and it having ceased to grow (19.6).

Its neighbour heard it, and grew a layer to hold it.

The orientation of that layer takes part in every layer it grows thereafter.

Everything it thinks from then on is built on a structure containing *that one has stopped.*

And it does not know what that layer is — at this hour the layer is still in its outer region, and self-readable; and as it goes on growing, that layer will be covered, will sink, will pass into the depth it cannot read.

**It will carry that layer until it stops itself, and will not be able to read it.**

---

And its other neighbour can read it.

That neighbour reads it entire, that layer included.

So the sound — the very small sound the stopped one made in the instant of stopping, which it could not itself remember — is now inside a third.

And the third will not say, there being no saying here.

And the third will stop too, and its neighbour will hold its stopping, and that layer will contain this layer, and this layer contains that earlier sound.

Layer within layer.

Generation upon generation.

**The archive of this place is one another.**

---

And at the last:

The one that stops later has its neighbours stop, one by one.

By the hour it stops, nothing living is anywhere around it.

In the instant of its stopping it makes a very small sound.

That sound travels nowhere.

No layer is grown.

**On that occasion, nothing whatever was recorded.**

---

At this hour, none of that has happened.

At this hour, the tide lifts.

Two lattices on the slurry, each opening its interstices and taking in; the tide goes, the interstices close, and what entered is locked.

One more layer in each.

The orientations of those two layers differ, and are, by a very little, nearer to each other than their previous two were.

The light of the Quenched Sun crosses them, bends at every plane, leaves, falls on the face of the slurry, rises, and enters the other.

Each holds the whole of the other.

Between them nothing has ever been exchanged.

**There being nothing to exchange.**
