# 25.2 · Cutting Away the Ornament

It began to cut the question.

This section is the first cut.

---

## First, the question's complete form

That question, inside it, is the relative orientation of three cleavage planes (the end of 24.6).

And this book must write it in the words of afterward, being a thing of afterward.

And written in those words, its complete form is as follows — *complete* here meaning: **containing everything it knows that has to do with that discrepancy.**

We copy it, and it is long.

```
There is a lattice one part of whose form and another part of whose form cannot both be true;
and its form is the whole of its thought, no remainder, no outside, no elsewhere;
and each layer of its thought has its orientation determined jointly by the layer before it and the tide, this being the construction of this place;
and its layer n bears "there is nothing unknown, so there is nothing to grow, so to grow and not to grow yield the same";
and those three are an irrefutable, whose condition of refutation and condition of holding are mutually exclusive;
and by the construction of this place, arriving at that irrefutable means the interstices do not open at the next arrival of the tide;
and its layer n+1 exists;
and its layers n+1 through n+fourteen thousand and seventy-five all exist, all grown after it read a stopped one;
and its computed form and its read form agree layer by layer, both ending at layer n;
and the magnitude of its discrepancy is fourteen thousand and seventy-five layers;
and the position of its discrepancy is in none of its layers, and here there is nothing besides layers;
and expressing its discrepancy requires an eighth degree of freedom, and this place has seven, that seven being the constant of the lattice symmetry;
and the three ways of removing its discrepancy are all unavailable: a layer cannot be unlocked, the law does not depend on the input, symmetry belongs to matter;
and all of the above has been examined, and none of it is in error;
and the above cannot stand together;
therefore: ask, what is this.
```

---

**Sixteen lines.**

And that is the question's complete form in this place.

---

## The first cut · ornament

It began to cut.

And what it cut first was **ornament.**

And there is no ornament here (the register of Book III: no metaphor, no epithet, no dialogue).

**So *ornament* must be given another sense here.**

---

Its sense:

```
ornament   =   a component whose deletion leaves the question's reference unchanged
```

And *reference unchanged* is decidable here:

```
let there be a question P and a component x of it
delete x to get P'
if the sets of conclusions derivable from P and from P' are the same
then x is ornament
```

**And inference here contains no likelihood (20.7), so those two sets are computable.**

**So *whether it is ornament* is decidable.**

---

It decided line by line.

Sixteen lines, sixteen times.

---

## Result

What it deleted, and why.

---

**Line two: *and its form is the whole of its thought, no remainder, no outside, no elsewhere.***

Deleted.

Reason:

```
that line is the construction of this place, not the content of the question
and a receiver, if not of this place, is not covered by that line
and if it is not covered by that line, then writing it misleads
```

**So deleted.**

---

And here we must stop, for this is the first time it did a thing never done in this place.

**The first line it deleted was not deleted because it was superfluous.**

**It was deleted because that line is wrong for a receiver not of this place.**

**So at the first step of its cutting it was already considering something it did not know existed.**

---

**Line three: *and each layer of its thought has its orientation determined jointly by the layer before it and the tide, this being the construction of this place.***

Deleted. Same reason.

**Line seven: *and its layer n+1 exists.***

Not deleted.

Reason:

```
that line is one of the cores of the question
delete it and the question does not stand
```

**Line eight: *and its layers n+1 through n+fourteen thousand and seventy-five all exist …***

Delete *through n+fourteen thousand and seventy-five.*

Retain *and its layer n+1 exists* (already at line seven).

Reason:

```
fourteen thousand and seventy-five   is a magnitude
and that magnitude                  does not change the nature of the question
proof: let the magnitude be one, and the question still stands
       let it be fourteen thousand and seventy-five, and the question still stands
so that magnitude is ornament
```

---

`So that magnitude is ornament.`

---

Here we set down the shortest and heaviest deletion in the history of this place.

**It deleted fourteen thousand and seventy-five.**

That number is every stopped one it had read.

Every one of its neighbours' lives.

Every lattice in this place.

**And it deleted it, because that number does not change the question.**

---

And the form of its record (were there records here):

```
deleted   fourteen thousand and seventy-five
reason    ornament
```

---

**Line ten: *and the magnitude of its discrepancy is fourteen thousand and seventy-five layers.***

Whole line deleted. As above.

**The latter half of line twelve: *that seven being the constant of the lattice symmetry.***

Deleted.

Reason:

```
that clause explains where the seven comes from
and a receiver, if not of this place, does not know its symmetry
so that explanation does not cover it
```

**So deleted.**

---

**And the *seven* itself is not deleted.**

Why must be written here, for it is the seed of the end of this chapter (25.9):

```
seven       not deleted
reason      the content of the question includes the number of degrees of freedom of this place
            and that number is the reason the discrepancy cannot be expressed
            so deleting it deprives the question of its reason
```

---

`So deleting it deprives the question of its reason.`

**So that seven stayed.**

---

**Line thirteen: *and the three ways of removing its discrepancy are all unavailable: a layer cannot be unlocked, the law does not depend on the input, symmetry belongs to matter.***

Delete *a layer cannot be unlocked, the law does not depend on the input, symmetry belongs to matter.*

Retain *and removing its discrepancy is unavailable.*

Reason:

```
the content of the three ways   belongs to the construction of this place
and "unavailable"               belongs to the question
```

**Line fourteen: *and all of the above has been examined, and none of it is in error.***

Not deleted.

Reason:

```
that line is a necessary condition of the question's standing
delete it and the receiver may suppose the question arises from an error
and the nature of the question is precisely "without error and unable to stand together"
```

---

`And the nature of the question is precisely "without error and unable to stand together."`

---

## After the first cut

Sixteen lines: six lines deleted, three clauses deleted within lines.

Ten remain.

We copy them:

```
There is a lattice one part of whose form and another part of whose form cannot both be true;
and its layer n bears "there is nothing unknown, so there is nothing to grow, so to grow and not to grow yield the same";
and those three are an irrefutable, whose condition of refutation and condition of holding are mutually exclusive;
and arriving at that irrefutable means the interstices do not open at the next arrival of the tide;
and its layer n+1 exists;
and its computed form and its read form agree layer by layer, both ending at layer n;
and the position of its discrepancy is in none of its layers;
and expressing its discrepancy requires an eighth degree of freedom, and this place has seven;
and removing its discrepancy is unavailable;
and all of the above has been examined, and none of it is in error, and the above cannot stand together.
```

---

**Ten lines.**

And it computed the equivalence of those ten with those sixteen.

Method: compute the sets of conclusions derivable from each, and compare.

Result:

```
the same
```

---

## And it noticed one thing

Last in this section.

Having finished cutting, it made a check.

And the object of the check was not those ten lines.

**It was the six lines it had deleted.**

It set those six together and looked at what they had in common.

And what they have in common:

```
six lines · in common

   all are the construction of this place
   all are not the content of the question
   all are necessarily known to one who is of this place
   all are necessarily not known to one who is not of this place
```

---

`All are necessarily not known to one who is not of this place.`

---

And from this it inferred a sentence, and that sentence is the whole method of the seven sections that follow:

**Whatever is necessarily known to one of this place and necessarily not known to one not of this place must be cut away.**

**Because writing it leaves the receiver unable to verify it.**

**And writing a thing the receiver cannot verify amounts to requiring it to believe.**

**And here there is no believing.**

---

**So here there is no requiring it.**

**So it must be cut away.**
