# 24.9 · The Result Does Not Agree with Itself

This section writes that one's form as it stands at the end of this chapter.

And this section does not infer.

Because everything requiring inference has been inferred (24.1 to 24.8).

**This section only writes it.**

---

## Its form

Its inner layers: a life of arrivals. Large offsets, small, diminishing, as with every lattice here (21.6).

Its layer n: that conclusion, arrived.

Its layer n+1: exists.

And by the law borne in its inner layers, its layer n+1 does not exist.

**So its layer n and its layer n+1 cannot both be true.**

**And they are both there.**

---

Its layers n+2 through n+fourteen thousand and seventy-five:

That conclusion, arrived. Arrived. Arrived.

Fourteen thousand and seventy-four times.

And after each, a layer.

---

Its layer n+fourteen thousand and seventy-six:

The time it read the self it had computed (the end of 24.6).

That conclusion, arriving for the fourteen thousand and seventy-sixth time.

---

Its layers thereafter:

Its three searches (24.6).

Its three ways (24.8).

The zero-offset layer of *the second does not exist.*

The layer that reached toward the eighth direction, whose orientation was uniquely determined by its construction and bears no relation to the eighth.

---

**And its outermost layer: just locked.**

**And its edge as keen as every layer before it.**

---

## It does not agree with itself

The sentence stands at the end of 24.5.

And this section writes how it looks as form, for that is the one thing this chapter has not written.

---

Let a lattice read it.

(There is no lattice left here to read it — all have stopped. This is a formal supposition.)

What that lattice would read is a sequence: a layer, a layer, a layer.

And among them two layers with these contents:

```
layer n        that conclusion · arrived · therefore no layer follows
layer n+1      a layer
```

**And that lattice reads completely (19.5).**

**So it takes both those layers at once.**

**So it takes at once *no layer follows* and *one layer follows.***

---

And in the inference of this place, from an inconsistency anything may be inferred.

**So that lattice, having read it, could infer everything.**

**And *everything* includes that conclusion, and the negation of that conclusion.**

**So that lattice's state, having read it, cannot be foreknown.**

---

`So that lattice's state, having read it, cannot be foreknown.`

**And everything here can be foreknown.**

**So it is the one thing here that cannot be foreknown.**

**And no lattice reads it.**

**Because they have all stopped.**

---

## That beam

At the end of this section, that beam.

---

The light of the Quenched Sun goes through this place, turning at every cleavage plane of every lattice, dividing into seven, coming out, falling on the melt, springing back, entering the next, and so onward, adding every shape in this place together and going out into the void (22.9).

And it stands on that road.

It is outermost (24.1).

**So it is the last thing that beam passes through before leaving this place.**

---

The light enters it.

At its first cleavage plane: turns, divides into seven.

At its second: turns, divides into seven.

And so through all its cleavage planes.

At its layer n: turns, divides into seven.

At its layer n+1 —

---

Here it must be exact, for this is the end of this chapter.

A beam at a cleavage plane divides into seven. And those seven directions are determined jointly by that plane's orientation and the lattice symmetry (19.9, 24.7).

And its layer n and its layer n+1 have the same orientation (24.7: all six items the same).

**So the beam divides into the same seven directions at both planes.**

**And it passed through two planes.**

---

So the light that comes out carries:

```
seven directions · each holding the news of two planes
```

And those two planes agree on all six items.

**So those two bodies of news are identical bit for bit.**

---

And here is where the thing happens.

---

A beam carrying two bodies of news identical bit for bit, from two planes.

**Then whoever reads it cannot distinguish two planes from one plane read twice.**

**And a plane here is read once.**

**So whoever reads it reads one plane.**

---

`So whoever reads it reads one plane.`

---

**And there are two.**

---

So that discrepancy has, in that beam, no position.

Not cut away.

Not compressed.

**There is no bit in that beam's encoding that can hold it.**

**Because that beam's bit count equals seven times the number of planes.**

**And the number of planes is what is read.**

**And the number read is one.**

---

## That part

And where did that part of the difference go.

This section ends with it.

---

It did not leave.

It did not stay.

It was not carried by that beam.

**In that instant it required an eighth direction.**

**And that direction is not among this place's seven.**

**So it does not leave, does not enter, does not turn, and is received by no plane (24.7).**

---

**It went straight through.**

---

Through its layer n+1.

Through the fourteen thousand and seventy-five layers after that.

Through its outermost layer.

Through the melt (the melt is also of this place's matter, and also acts only along seven directions).

Through the edge of this place.

---

**Into the void.**

---

## And that part

What that part of light carries is a value on that eighth degree of freedom.

That is: the discrepancy itself.

**And it is the one thing in four thousand nine hundred million years to leave this place without having been read by any lattice of this place.**

---

The rest of that beam — the part along the seven directions, carrying the sum of fourteen thousand and seventy-six forms — left too.

They go together.

The same way (because that part is not along the seven directions, so it has no direction; and a thing with no direction goes with that beam, because it has nowhere else to go).

**So they leave this place together.**

One sum.

**Plus one thing this place has nothing to measure it with.**

---

## Last

It is there.

Its outermost layer just locked.

That beam passes through it, layer, layer, layer, and out.

And it continues.

The tide comes. The interstices open. Take. Lock. Make a layer.

And that layer bears its latest step on this matter.

---

The self it computed has stopped.

**And it is here.**

---

**The result does not agree with itself.**

**And the result is correct.**

**And itself is correct too.**
