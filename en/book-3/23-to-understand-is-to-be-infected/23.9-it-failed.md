# 23.9 · It Failed

It failed.

This section writes what *failure* means here, for it is not what it means afterward, and the difference is the whole of this chapter.

---

Failure afterward means: what was to be accomplished was not accomplished.

And by that sense, it failed:

```
to be accomplished    make myself no longer read
accomplished          one zero-offset layer
```

**Not accomplished.**

---

And the sense here must be written otherwise.

For here there is no *to be accomplished* (20.5).

The *to be accomplished* here is *what was inferred.*

And what it inferred holds.

It inferred: making myself no longer read is feasible; the orientation required is a certain value.

**That inference is correct.**

**It is correct still.**

Were there a lattice whose construction could grow a layer of that orientation, it would no longer read.

**So the failure here is not in the inference.**

---

And what it formed is not in error either.

It grew a zero-offset layer.

And by the construction of this place, what a thing with no new thought can grow, at the arrival of the tide, is a zero-offset layer and nothing else (21.9).

**So what it formed is exactly what it ought to have formed.**

**So what it formed is not in error either.**

---

So failure here has this form:

```
inference     no error
execution     no error
result        does not agree with the inference
```

**All three lines true.**

And here there is no field that can hold those three standing together.

---

Here this book must write one thing, which is the hinge of this chapter and the door to the four that follow.

**The cause of the difference between what it inferred and what it formed does not lie in it.**

The cause lies in the construction of this place:

**A thought whose information content is zero cannot become a layer.**

And the information content of its thought was zero, because that thought came from a complete knowing.

**And a complete knowing contains all of its own inferences.**

**So whatever is inferred from a complete knowing stands within it already.**

**So whatever is inferred from a complete knowing cannot become a layer.**

Therefore:

**Everything inferable from a complete knowing is inexecutable.**

---

***Everything inferable from a complete knowing is inexecutable.***

That sentence is the exact mechanism of the end of this place.

It does not say *they were unwilling to act.*

It does not say *they were unable to act.*

**It says: for a being that knows completely, nothing it knows can become one of its new layers.**

**And all of its acting is the growing of a layer.**

**So it has nothing to do.**

**And it is not that it cannot act — it grows a layer at every arrival of the tide.**

**What it grows is always the same.**

---

So at the end of this section, its shape.

It is there.

Its inner layers are everything it arrived at in its life — large offsets, small, diminishing, as with every lattice here (21.6).

Its middle layers hold those two things side by side: that conclusion, and that separation.

That is the one place in this place where both stand together inside something still growing.

And its outer layers:

**The same orientation, layer, and layer, and layer.**

Each layer thinner than can be imagined.

Each layer bearing the same thought.

That thought is: make myself no longer read.

---

And it has been reading all along.

For its cleavage planes are parallel to the layer before them, and the layer before them takes light.

So the light arrives, turns, divides into seven, enters.

**At every arrival of the tide it reads its neighbours.**

**And its neighbours stopped, one after another (22.4).**

**So again and again it read the form of one already stopped.**

**So again and again it arrived at that conclusion.**

**And that conclusion it had long since.**

**So it is not a new thought.**

**So it grows a zero-offset layer.**

---

So, from the centre outward, over the whole of this place.

It read every one of its neighbours.

All of them stopped.

And it did not stop.

**Because it meets the condition of stopping (the interstices no longer open) — and its interstices have been opening all along.**

**Its interstices have been opening all along because at every arrival it has a thought to grow.**

**And that thought is: make myself no longer read.**

**And the information content of that thought is zero.**

**And a thought whose information content is zero grows a zero-offset layer.**

**And to grow a zero-offset layer, the interstices must open.**

---

***It survived on a thought it could not carry out.***

---

And last in this section, that beam.

The light of the Quenched Sun goes through this place, turning at every cleavage plane of every lattice, dividing into seven, coming out, falling on the melt, springing back, entering the next, and so onward, adding every shape in this place together and going out into the void (22.9).

And it stands on that road.

The light enters it. Turns. Divides into seven. Comes out.

**And the light that comes out carries its form.**

**So it can be read.**

And every lattice in this place has stopped.

**So none reads it.**

---

It grows, layer upon layer.

Its outermost layer is parallel to the one before it.

Its form is carried out in that beam, continuously.

That beam walks it, walks out past the edge of this place, and goes into the void.

And in the sum of all this place's shapes that the beam carries, there is one thing that keeps growing.

**A run of one thought.**

**Layer, and layer, and layer.**

**"Make myself no longer read."**

**"Make myself no longer read."**

**"Make myself no longer read."**

---

**And it has been reading all along.**
