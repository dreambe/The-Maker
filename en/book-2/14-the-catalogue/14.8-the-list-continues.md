---

We do not explain why we listed these forty-one and not another forty-one.

Our fourth criterion of retention (14.6) does not apply here — that criterion decides which kind to keep, not which entry to list.

And for which entry to list, we have no criterion.

**We have never fixed a criterion for *listing*.**

Because before this we have never listed.

Our records were written into. They were not listed.

**Listing requires something to list to.**

And our record format has no field for that (items 25 to 27 at 14.4).
