Posture

When we sample, we do not alter posture.

We state the technical content of that sentence.

At the instant an individual is taken by us, its parts stand in some relative position. That relative position was determined by what it was doing.

We place it whole into the medium. We do not straighten it, flatten it, or align it.

Therefore the forty-two million nine hundred thousand and seven individuals of the second draft are each in a different posture.

We recorded every one of those postures.

Method:

```
per individual   three views · orthogonal · complete
                 external outline · entire
                 internal structure · entire
                 total data per individual  1.1 x 10^14 bits
```

One thing not entered into any statistic

Of the forty-two million nine hundred thousand and seven individuals of the second draft, four hundred and eleven contain another individual inside them.

Our record format has no field for this.

We did not add a field.

We entered those four hundred and eleven in a note. The note reads, in full:

```
four hundred and eleven individuals  contain another individual
container and contained  of the same kind
contained  smaller · structure incomplete
number  that of the container
```

`Number: that of the container.`

That is: those four hundred and eleven contained individuals have no numbers of their own.

They do not exist on this list as individuals.
