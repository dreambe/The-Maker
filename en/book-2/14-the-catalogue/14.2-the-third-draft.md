
Two million pairs.

Our numbering does not express pairing.

We did not modify the numbering.

We entered those two million pairs in a separate table.

That table has two columns:

```
number A          number B
```

There is no third column.

That is: the table does not record what the behaviour between A and B was.

That information is in the two individuals' own records, recorded once in each.

To read *what A is doing to B*, one must call up A's record.

To read *what is being done to B by A*, one must call up B's record.

Those two records we wrote separately, and never joined.
