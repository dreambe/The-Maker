# 14.7 · One Exception

The ordinary method of retention:

```
extraction    take a part of the individual
              usually its hard structures · or all of its hard structures
              after this operation, that individual does not continue
```

`After this operation, that individual does not continue.`

That is: we extract, and it stops.

One hundred and four thousand one hundred and twenty million entries, their extraction records identical in format.

**One is not.**

---

That one's number:

```
5 · 041 100 000 000 · 000 000 004 100
```

That is, the one belonging to the forty-first name of 14.5.

---

That entry reads as follows.

We copy it, because it is identical in format to the other one hundred and four thousand one hundred and twenty million, with one line more.

```
number       5 · 041 100 000 000 · 000 000 004 100
sampled      V 5 338 004 100
position     coordinates (omitted)
posture      three views · orthogonal · complete
environment  temperature 281.1 kelvin
             pressure 0.98 standard
             illumination 4.1 x 10^-3 (dark)
             other individuals within 4.1 metres   0
behaviour    none (at rest)
object       —
lineage      backward  complete
             forward   —
name         its two light-receiving structures oriented to agree with our position
extraction   —
retained     the whole individual
note         Of this individual we took no material.
             We waited for it.
```

---

On that note.

`Of this individual we took no material.`

That is: we performed no extraction on it.

**So after being recorded, it continued.**

`We waited for it.`

That is: we stayed where we were and kept observing it, until it stopped of itself.

---

The record of observation:

```
began         V 5 338 004 100
ended         V 5 338 004 103
duration      3 years
interval      1 hour
entries       26 280
interruptions 0
```

Twenty-six thousand two hundred and eighty entries.

For each hour of those three years we hold one entry.

We do not set out those twenty-six thousand two hundred and eighty here.

Only three of them, because they are identical in format and their contents give the shape of those three years.

---

**Entry 1**

```
V 5 338 004 100 · hour 1
position     as at sampling
behaviour    none (at rest)
others within 4.1 metres   0
```

**Entry 13 140** (the midpoint of the three years)

```
V 5 338 004 101 · hour 4 380
position     1.1 x 10^3 metres from the point of sampling
behaviour    feeding
object       one individual · number 5 · 041 100 041 000 · 000 000 000 007
others within 4.1 metres   4
```

**Entry 26 280**

```
V 5 338 004 103 · hour 26 280
position     4.1 metres from the point of sampling
behaviour    none (at rest)
others within 4.1 metres   0
state        stopped
```

---

`4.1 metres from the point of sampling.`

In three years it went to places one thousand one hundred metres from the point of sampling.

And the position at which it stopped is four point one metres from the position at which we first recorded it.

We do not explain this.

We record it.

---

And after it stopped we did one thing.

```
V 5 338 004 103   operation
   extraction      no
   retained        the whole individual
   method          placed in the medium · not dismembered · not sectioned · not sampled
   posture         its posture at stopping preserved
```

`Its posture at stopping preserved.`

That is: we did not straighten it, unfold it, or align it (14.1).

**Its posture now is its posture at stopping.**

That posture, under the clustering of 14.1, belongs to cluster 0 041.

The shared feature of that cluster is:

```
the parts drawn inward
outer parts enclosing inner parts
```

---

It is eleven thousand metres below the surface, in that void of forty-one thousand million cubic metres.

It is the only whole individual in that void.

The samples of the other four hundred and eleven million kinds are all products of extraction — hard structures, or all the hard structures.

**Only it is whole.**

---

At the end of this section one thing must be written, because it is a formal repetition in our records that we have never pointed out.

The word *wait* occurs twice in four thousand nine hundred and sixty million years of record.

**The first** at the thirty-third name of 14.5:

```
thirty-three    waiting
```

That name violates our own third rule of grammar (a name contains no judgment).

We wrote at 14.5: **So this name is wrong. We know it is wrong. We keep it.**

**The second** here:

```
note         Of this individual we took no material.
             We waited for it.
```

`We waited for it.`

The *wait* in that line and the *wait* in the thirty-third name are the same word.

And by our own statement (14.5):

> *Waiting* is a judgment — it requires holding that the individual's action is directed at something that has not happened.
> We cannot observe what has not happened. So we cannot observe waiting.

And here, the subject of that action is us.

**We did not need to observe it.**

**We performed it.**

---

Apart from those two places, the word does not occur in our records.

And in those three years the interval was one hour, twenty-six thousand two hundred and eighty times, interruptions zero.

Our record format has no field that can hold those three years.

**So we put them in the note.**

**Four words.**
