One thing not predicted

One million one hundred thousand years after the fourth draft terminated, sampling stopped.

The reason, in our record:

```
reason    number of sampleable kinds fell to 0
```

And four thousand one hundred years after that stop, a routine observation detected:

```
one kind    characteristics matching a kind recorded before termination
            marked, in the pre-termination record, as gone
            at this time  present
            individuals  1.1 x 10^3
```

Our action:

```
resume sampling     yes
individuals sampled 411
number of that kind  4 · 411 000 000 000
```

That is: that kind is the fourth draft's last number.

It was taken after we had declared the number of sampleable kinds to be zero.

In our records that entry is identical in format to the four hundred and eleven thousand million before it.

We added no note to it.

We left the line *number of sampleable kinds fell to 0* where it was, unaltered.

**That line is wrong.**

We know it is wrong.

We do not alter what has been written into our records (20.2 applies to us as well: we do not delete).

So in our record of the fourth draft, a line certainly wrong and an entry that disproves it stand together.

They are four thousand one hundred years apart.
