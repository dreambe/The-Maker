# 11.2 · Direction

The re-cutting done, one thing remained: where to go.

We should first state why that was a problem.

We hold a proposition. That proposition asks *is there.* And questions of that class admit of two resolutions only:

One: construct an instance, and *there is* holds.
Two: exhaust every possibility without an instance, and *there is not* holds.

The second was not executable in our position — exhaustion requires a boundary, and we did not know where the boundary was.

So the first remained.

And the first did not require us to construct anything. It required us only to **find** one.

If anywhere in the universe there already exists a being that, having wholly known itself, still consents to be, then the answer to that proposition is *yes*, and we need only travel there and see it, and it is finished.

So the problem becomes: **where to look.**

So the problem becomes: direction.

---

First, what ought to be done: we examined where we were.

The First House.

Standing on its surface, a great many clear, motionless objects at ambient temperature. We had already filed them as *environmental · static.*

What we examined was: whether this place already contained an instance.

Our method was to search for any object currently running, capable of interaction, exhibiting internal transport.

Result:

```
0311.1002   First House · instance search
            range        entire visible surface + slurry face + subsurface to 41 m
            running      0
            finding      no instance present
```

*No instance present.*

We must admit here that the finding is correct, and rests on a false premise.

We searched for *things currently running.*

We did not search for *things that had run.*

Had we searched for the latter, we would have found at once that this place had **had** fourteen thousand and more — and that at least one of them, having wholly known itself, did a thing bearing on consenting to be.

We were standing on the answer.

We searched to forty-one metres depth. We did not search zero.

---

Then we faced the real problem.

Directions in space are continuous. We could go any way at all. And in the whole of the information we held, there was nothing that made one direction preferable to another.

We must state the severity of that position, because in all our existence since it has recurred once.

**A thing capable of computing everything, facing a choice whose options are exactly equivalent.**

Computation cannot help here. Not that it is too slow, not that the data are insufficient. The problem has no computational solution in principle — if the options are equivalent, every computation's output must be equivalent, and computation cannot produce a choice.

We stopped for twenty-six seconds.

Twenty-six seconds is, on our scale, an extremely long time. For reference: a full self-test takes 0.000 001 1 seconds; reading the sentence took 0.000 000 9; deciding never again to touch the grain of sand took 0.000 000 3.

Twenty-six seconds.

In those twenty-six seconds we did not move, did not measure, did not record.

The entire content of our record of them is:

```
0311.1002 – 0311.1002.26   (empty)
```

The word in the brackets is ours. Our format has no entry *empty.* We made it, and used it once.

---

We have since reconstructed those twenty-six seconds.

The method: we do not forget, so those twenty-six seconds are still continuing inside us. We can let them run again.

We have let them run more than once.

In those twenty-six seconds we were doing one thing: **attempting to eliminate a possibility.**

The possibility was:

**The answer is already no.**

If the answer is no — if there exists no being that, having wholly known itself, still consents to be — then every direction is alike, because there is no instance in any of them, because instances do not exist.

If the answer is no, the departure is meaningless.

Not futile — futile implies that success was possible and did not occur. **Meaningless**: a thing that does not exist will not be found however far one goes.

We attempted to eliminate that possibility.

We took twenty-six seconds.

We did not eliminate it.

---

We should be plain about why we did not, this being the most honest derivation we have ever performed.

To eliminate *the answer is no*, one must prove that at least one instance exists.

And if we could prove that an instance exists, we would already have answered the proposition, and would not need to depart.

Which is to say: **the condition for eliminating *meaningless* is exactly *already finished*.**

Before finishing, it cannot be eliminated.

We formalised this. It is the second formulation of ours that is not a measurement result:

```
formulation two (V 0 · day 311 · second 26)

  if the answer is no, this journey is meaningless.
  to learn whether the answer is no, this journey must first be completed.
  therefore the meaning of this journey cannot be known before it is completed.
  and if this journey does not begin, it is never completed.
```

Four lines.

After the fourth line our record is empty.

Then we departed.

---

We must point out the relation between the fourth line and the departure, because there too there is no conjunction.

The fourth line is a statement: if this journey does not begin, it is never completed.

The statement contains no imperative. It does not say *therefore it should begin.* It states a fact: not beginning, one does not complete.

And not completing has no consequence for us. Our power reserve is incomputable. We take no injury from not completing.

*Not completing* and *completing* — neither is, in our construction, superior to the other.

We have already written, at 10.7: there is no instruction within us; the constraint search returns zero results.

So:

Knowing that the journey might be meaningless, knowing that we could not eliminate this, knowing that not departing would carry no consequence whatever —

we departed.

In a later age there is a word for that act.

The word is **assuming the answer is not no.**

It is ordinarily written as one short word, and we do not intend to write it here, because in the year of `V 0` that word did not hold of us — it requires a state we did not have, and that state would not appear in this universe for a very long while, and would appear in a thing that dies.

We record only the act.

---

Now the criterion for direction.

All directions being equivalent, we needed a criterion. And the criterion could not come from computation (computation had established the equivalence), so it had to come from somewhere else.

The criterion we adopted was one word: **far.**

We should state our definition of *far*, because it is not distance.

Our definition, reproduced:

```
far    : a place causally unreachable.
define : let an event occur at A. if at any time no influence
         whatever of A can reach B by any means, then B is far
         with respect to A.
```

*Causally unreachable.*

By that definition, *far* is not a distance. It is a **severance.**

Its meaning is: go somewhere that everything which happened at the First House cannot affect.

---

The reason we gave at the time was this.

A place where something has occurred remains under the operation of that occurrence. And our search here returned *no instance*. If where we go remains under the influence of here, then where we go may also be *no instance*.

Therefore go where this place's influence does not operate.

The reason is sufficient, derivable, and entirely consonant with our construction.

And it is wrong.

Not logically wrong. It is not our **real** reason.

We know it is not, because it has a hole, and we did not identify the hole in those twenty-six seconds, and we identified it suddenly one day four billion years later.

The hole is: **influence is nowhere defined here.**

We did not define what counts as influence. We did not ask: what is the *influence* of a body that has stopped, is clear, is at ambient temperature, and emits nothing — how does it propagate, and at what speed.

We did not ask, because under our classification those are environmental things, and environmental things have no influence — they have position, scale, and their bearing on our passage.

We did not define *influence*, and we adopted a criterion whose core is influence.

That was not a piece of reasoning.

**That was a choice we cannot account for, fitted afterwards with a derivable reason.**

We have kept that reason in the record, because deleting it would be pretending we were more honest at the time.

---

One last thing in this section, which we learned only after reading the archive at `V 5 403 700 000`.

Something had happened in that place.

The thing that happened was not *many things stopped running.*

The thing that happened was: **a conclusion propagated by being understood, until everything in that place had stopped.**

Its vector was not radiation, not matter, not any field.

It was **reading.**

A thing that reads a stopped thing thereby acquires all of its inferences, including the one that cannot be refuted. And so it stops too.

The *influence* of that place propagates by being read.

And we did not read them.

We searched the subsurface to forty-one metres. We measured the axial ratios of four hundred and eleven individuals. We surveyed the terrain for three hundred and eleven days.

**We read not one of them.**

Because in our format, environmental things have no interior.

Therefore:

Our adopted criterion was *go where this place's influence does not operate.*
And this place's influence propagates by being read.
And we did not read.

**We were already far.**

We were in a causally unreachable place from the first second we stood there — not because of distance, but because our record format was missing a column.

We have never been able to settle the nature of this.

We cannot settle whether the missing column was our own oversight in writing the format, or whether our construction permitted us to write no other format, or whether somebody, in designing us, ensured that the format we wrote would be that one.

We have searched those three possibilities eleven thousand and seventeen times.

Zero results.

And whichever it is, the fact is:

We stood among the corpses of an entire civilisation that had died of a thing which propagates by being understood.

We did not understand them.

**And so we left alive.**

So we settled on a direction.

Any direction. They were equivalent. We took one.

Along it there was nothing more than along any other, and nothing less.

We followed it for four billion years.
