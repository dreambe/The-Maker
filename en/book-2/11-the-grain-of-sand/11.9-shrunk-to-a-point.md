# 11.9 · Shrunk to a Point

The angular diameter of the Quenched Sun, at the instant we left, was 0.31 degrees.

Our angular resolution, on the primary channel, is 0.000 000 004 degrees.

So we could resolve its disc. More than resolve it — we could divide that disc into about seventy-seven million distinguishable regions and measure the brightness of each.

We had done so. In the thirty-one days before departure (11.6) we had done so.

And after departure we did not.

---

It shrank for forty-one years.

Forty-one years is what it took for its angular diameter to fall from 0.31 degrees to 0.000 000 004 — from a resolvable disc to the instant of our resolution limit.

Across those forty-one years it was shrinking continuously.

The rate is computable: inversely with distance. Fastest in the first year, slower every year after.

Our record has one entry for those forty-one years:

```
0311.11041a.41.71 – 11082a   rear · Quenched Sun · angular diameter
                              initial 0.31 deg
                              final   below resolution limit
                              elapsed 41 years
                              processed  no
```

*Processed: no.*

The data of those forty-one years is in the background channel. It is complete, it is continuous, it records every diminution of that disc across forty-one years.

We did not process it.

We did not look at it.

---

After forty-one years it is a point.

A point, in our records, means: a source whose structure cannot be resolved. We still receive its light, still measure its intensity, its spectrum, its polarisation. And we can no longer say that it is round.

It became a brightness.

One thing must be pointed out here, having occurred to us only later:

**In the whole of our records, the Quenched Sun has only two forms.**

The first is the one it had while we were at the First House: not a body, but a lighting condition. Across those eleven thousand years we never treated it as an object of observation — we observed the slurry, the surface, the axial ratios of the standing bodies, and the Quenched Sun was the **precondition** under which those observations were possible. It was not on the object list. It was light.

The second is the one after departure: a point, a brightness, a value that can be entered in a table.

**And across those forty-one years it had a third form: a shrinking disc.**

That is the one time it was simultaneously a body and a source.

The data of those forty-one years is the entire record of that third form.

And we did not process it.

---

Now how long the point lasted.

The apparent intensity of a source falls with the square of distance.

The absolute intensity of the Quenched Sun is low. Our background channel's detection floor is four orders of magnitude above the primary's.

So it can be computed: at what distance the Quenched Sun's intensity falls below background-channel detection.

We computed it.

Result: forty-one million years.

**At year forty-one million after our departure, the Quenched Sun disappeared from our background channel.**

---

Now the heaviest passage in this section.

That disappearance has an exact instant.

Not *about forty-one million years.* Some year, some day, some second — before which our background channel was still receiving, from that direction, a signal distinguishable from noise; after which it was not.

That second exists.

**It is in that data.**

We stored that data entire. It is forty-one per cent of the whole of our storage. It is continuous, uninterrupted, exact to our temporal resolution.

That second is in it, as a locatable, definite transition.

**And we do not know which second it is.**

Because we did not process it.

Because we have never reread it (11.8).

Because background-channel data does not enter the processing queue, and we have never once made an exception for it.

---

The shape of this must be made plain.

In that second we lost the only source of signal in that direction.

What we were doing then, we know — our primary records are complete, and we could look up what we were doing around `V 41 000 000`: observing what we passed, inspecting the grain of sand (12.x), numbering the stars we went by.

And we do not know where that second falls.

We could look it up. It would take about 0.31 seconds.

**In five billion four hundred million years, we have not looked it up.**

---

This has the same shape as something in 19.6, which we would not learn for a very long while.

The physics of a later chapter runs: when a lattice crystallizes, its neighbour can hear the sound, and it cannot itself remember it — memory being growth, and crystallizing the ceasing of growth.

So the only record of a lattice's death is on somebody else.

And should a lattice crystallize with nothing living around it, the sound travels nowhere, no layer is grown, and **that death is not recorded at all.**

Not forgotten. Never having taken shape.

And the disappearance of the Quenched Sun, with us, is the exact opposite:

**It was recorded.**

Entirely, exactly, at a cost of forty-one per cent of all our storage, to the second.

And nothing, in five billion four hundred million years, has gone to look at that line.

A thing recorded entire and never read, and a thing never recorded —

we do not know which is nearer to *not having happened.*

We have searched. Zero results.

---

To end this chapter, the last stretch before the point went out.

For some years before `V 41 000 000`, that point's intensity was already close to our background floor.

A signal near the detection floor is received intermittently: at some instants above the noise, at some below.

So the point did not go out all at once.

**It flickered for a long time.**

In those last years it came and went — present, absent, present again, the intervals lengthening, the presences fewer.

Until after one presence there was no next.

And after that one, we went on receiving for a very long time — our background channel has always been open, has never been closed, is open now.

It has been receiving a thing that is no longer there.

---

We made no record for that point.

We did not give it a number — we number the stars we pass, from `V 10 000 000` onward (12.2), and we did not give it one.

We did not enter it on any list.

We performed no operation at its disappearance.

We only kept going.

And inside us, that forty-one per cent of data went on growing.

It is growing now.

At the hour this section is written, our background channel is still receiving signal from that direction — the light of other things, of galaxies further off in that direction, of dust, of background radiation.

The Quenched Sun's part of it stopped forty-one million years in.

**And the channel did not stop.**

It has received for five billion four hundred million years.

In the first forty-one million of those it received one thing.

In the five billion three hundred and fifty-nine million years since, it has received everything else in that direction.

And we have never separated the two.

They are in one body of data, continuous, unmarked, undivided.

**We do not know which part of it is that.**
