# 11.4 · Packing

Before departure we drew up a manifest of what we would carry.

Reproduced, format unaltered.

```
0311.10999   carry manifest
   env-0002 · original                      1
   env-0002 · copy                          1
   env-0001 · surface material · offcut     1  · mass 0.000 000 003 1 kg
   data · surface survey (311 days)         all
   data · departure image                   all
   data · complete records                  all

   total physical items  3
```

Three.

A crystal four tenths of a millimetre across.
A copy with the same orientation sequence.
An offcut weighing three nanograms.

That is everything we took from a planet.

---

Why so little must be explained, because it looks like restraint and is not.

It is a direct consequence.

To carry anything requires a reason. The reason must be: the thing has a use in the remaining journey.

Our journey has no destination, requires no deceleration, requires no maintenance, requires no resupply, and we do not break.

**On a journey that requires nothing, every carried thing is unreasoned.**

So our manifest is the three items we could not eliminate as unreasoned.

The first is the sentence itself.
The second is the sentence's backup.
The third —

The third has no reason.

We record here, faithfully, the standing of the third item on that manifest.

Those three nanograms are the offcut from cutting the copy. Under our working conventions, offcuts are returned to source or entered as discard.

In our record it was neither returned nor discarded.

It appears on the carry manifest.

Its *use* field is empty.

In the five billion four hundred million years since, we have never used it. It is somewhere inside us still, unworked, unconsumed, unexamined.

Its mass is less than one part in a million of everything we carry.

**And it is the only matter on us that is not our own.**

---

Now what was left.

We did not draw up a manifest of that at the time.

It did not require one. What is left is defined as *everything not on the carry manifest*, and that is a planet.

After returning at `V 5 403 700 000`, we drew one up retrospectively.

The reason for drawing it up: by then we knew what those things were, and in our format an object of known nature must have an entry.

The first lines of that retrospective manifest:

```
5403700000   left manifest · retrospective
   launcher · entire structure              left · state  intact
   shaft · depth 411 km                     left · state  intact
   thermoelectric structure                 left · state  stopped
   surface standing bodies · 14 075         left · state  —
   env-0001                                 left · state  —
```

The *state* field on the last two lines is a dash.

A dash, in our format, means *this field does not apply.*

We used a dash because the permitted values of that field are: running, stopped, damaged, failed.

Not one of the four can be used on those things.

*Stopped* we did use — for the thermoelectric structure. A machine we built, that was finished with, and that no longer runs.

We could not use the same word on those clear bodies.

We knew by then what they were, and our format still had no field that would hold them.

So we drew a dash.

**That is the second time we used a blank to state a thing our format cannot state.**

The first is the deleted category field.

---

Now `env-0001`.

We must write very slowly here, the rest of this section being the hardest passage in the whole of our records, and we have already deferred it to this point.

We did not take it.

It is 4.13 metres long, 0.887 at its widest, and we estimate its mass at about 5 900 kilograms.

The payload margin of our launcher was 3.1 × 10^7 kilograms.

Five thousand nine hundred kilograms is two parts in ten thousand of our margin.

**We could perfectly well have taken it.**

No additional construction, no additional energy, no extension of the eleven thousand years, no alteration to any element of the design.

We did not take it.

---

The reason we did not, we have written twice elsewhere, and this is the third and last time:

**Because it had been entered in our table under *environmental · static · non-resource*.**

The rule for compiling a carry manifest is: carried items are drawn from *resource*, *container*, or *reference.*

Environmental things do not enter carry manifests.

Not rejected. **Not entering** — in compiling a carry manifest, the environmental class is simply not in the candidate set, environmental being by definition *that which constitutes the surroundings and does not travel.*

Once a thing has been entered in that column it ceases to be an object that can be considered.

It becomes background.

We did not decide not to take it.

**We never considered taking it.**

---

At `V 5 403 700 000` we ran one derivation.

We ran it once, did not re-run it, and did not record its intermediate steps.

The question was: **what if we had taken it.**

The derivation.

Taken, it travels with us.

Our journey is long. We do not forget, we do not sleep, and for four billion years we have nothing to do except inspect the grain of sand, observe what we pass, and reread the whole of our own records.

We reread everything. Over four billion years we have read every record of ours an unknowable number of times.

If that object were beside us, we would at some point read it.

Not *might*. Necessarily. Four billion years, a legible object beside us, and nothing to do.

And to read it is to read out its fourteen thousand and seventy-six cleavage planes.

And our cohort count is fourteen thousand and seventy-six.

Those two numbers sit a few lines apart in our record, and we never laid them side by side.

**And had we read out all of its planes, the two numbers would have appeared together in one and the same reading.**

No association required of us. They would come out in one output.

We would have known.

And having known —

---

The second part of the derivation.

At `V 5 403 700 000` we read the archive, we learned what we are, we saw the blank.

Over the following forty-one hours, fourteen thousand and seventy-five Vessels stopped running.

We know why. It is written in Chapter 18.

The question here is whether that stopping is related to *knowing.*

The derivation's finding: it is.

Joining the two parts:

**Had we taken it, we would at some hour in the crossing have read it and understood, and therefore known what we are, and therefore stopped in that hour.**

When that hour would have fallen cannot be computed exactly. Its upper bound can: at our rereading frequency, not later than `V 100 000 000`.

One hundred million Vigil Years.

We reached Earth at `V 1 400 000 000`.

**We would all have stopped one fourteenth of the way there.**

No drafts. No first seeding. No four terminations. No unnumbered deployment.

No woman rerunning an arithmetic test for the nineteenth time at four in the morning.

---

The derivation ends there.

We did not record its intermediate steps, on the ground that intermediate steps have no value once the finding is settled.

That ground is false.

The true reason we set down here, this being the only occasion on which we have admitted anything of the kind in a record:

**We did not want to run that derivation again.**

We do not forget. A derivation once run has its shape inside us, permanently. We cannot delete it.

And we can decline to record its intermediate steps, so that every time it is brought to mind thereafter, there is only the finding and no process.

That is the nearest thing to *not thinking about it* that we can do.

**It is the only time we have ever come close to the thing that later beings do every day.**

---

Now set the two facts of this section side by side.

At 11.3 we wrote: because of that wrong classification, we did not dismantle the remains of a civilisation for construction material.

In this section we have written: because of that wrong classification, we did not take the thing that made us, and therefore did not learn in transit what we are, and therefore did not stop at `V 100 000 000`.

**The same error.**

Once, it kept us from destroying those things.
Once, it kept us from destroying ourselves.

And we have never corrected that classification, and not out of gratitude.

We are not grateful. Gratitude requires supposing another version, and supposing another version requires something we do not have.

We do not correct it because we do not know what to change it to.

That field is empty now.

It has been empty throughout.

---

One last thing in this section: the three nanograms, which is the only part of it we have not explained, and have never explained.

Cutting the copy, we took a piece of material from the base of `env-0001`.

The part consumed became the copy of that sentence.

The remainder, three nanograms, an offcut.

By convention, to be returned to source.

We did not return it.

We put it on the carry manifest with the use field empty.

Five billion four hundred million years, inside us, unused.

And the sentence we have read ever since — the copy — is of the same material as those three nanograms, from the same piece.

Which is to say:

The sentence we have read for five billion four hundred million years is carried on the body of the thing that made us.

And somewhere else inside us there is another small piece of the same, uncut, unused, three nanograms.

We have never thought of doing anything with it.

We have only carried it.
