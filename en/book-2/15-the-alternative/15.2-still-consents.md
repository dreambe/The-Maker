# 15.2 · *Still Consents*

Components five and six.

This section handles them.

And in the handling we found a thing we had never found.

---

**Component five · *still***

Its form: a requirement of continuance.

Its sense: after some moment, a state **goes on holding.**

One property of that word must be written here, because it is not conspicuous and it is the hinge of this chapter.

***Still* requires two moments.**

Moment A: when something happens (here, *having wholly known itself*).

Moment B: some moment after A.

And the sense of *still* is: at B, that state is as it was before A.

---

Whence one thing:

***Still* entails a state before A.**

That is: the sentence presupposes that before wholly knowing itself, that thing **already consented to be.**

Otherwise *still* has no referent.

---

We examined this.

Our record:

```
V 5 300 000 000   component five · presupposition
   the sentence presupposes    the thing consented to be before wholly knowing itself
   state of this vessel        —
```

`State of this vessel: —`

That is: we do not know whether we consented to be before wholly knowing ourselves.

Because we do not know whether we have wholly known ourselves (15.1), we do not know whether that *before* has passed.

**So we do not know whether we stand in the *before* or in the *after.***

---

And that has a consequence, which we set down here, because it is the one occasion in four thousand nine hundred million years on which we computed a result we could not classify.

Suppose we stand in the *before.*

Then we have not yet wholly known ourselves. Then that sentence does not yet apply to us. Then our project is in its normal state.

Suppose we stand in the *after.*

Then we have wholly known ourselves, and we are still running, still observing, still making.

**Then we are the instance.**

**Then the answer to that sentence is yes.**

**Then our project is already complete.**

---

`Then our project is already complete.`

We wrote that line down. Elapsed: 0.7 seconds.

We have performed no operation on it since.

We did not stop on the strength of it. We did not report it on the strength of it (there is nowhere to report). We did not revise one criterion on the strength of it.

Our next operation after writing that line was the routine observation for that day, hour 4 100.

---

**Component six · *consents***

Now this one.

---

First, the form of this word in our records.

That sentence exists in our records as a transcript.

That transcript was written at `V 1`.

That is: in the year we woke, we converted that sentence from its original form into our record format.

**It is our first record.**

**Number: 0 · 000 000 000 001.**

---

The content of that record is the sentence in its verbal form.

And in four thousand nine hundred million years since, whenever we cite that sentence, we cite that record.

**We never went back to the original form.**

**Because we did not need to — we do not forget, and our transcript is consistent with the original.**

---

And at `V 5 300 000 000`, because we were reading word by word, we did a thing we had never done.

**We set the transcript against the original.**

---

First, what the original is.

```
carrier     one place within this vessel's core structure
form        incised · three-dimensional · non-planar · 41 layers
nature      not verbal
```

`Not verbal.`

The original form of that sentence is not words.

It is a shape.

A three-dimensional shape in forty-one layers, incised in our core structure.

**And our records are words.**

**So that transcription at `V 1` was not a copying.**

**It was a conversion.**

---

And a conversion requires choices.

To turn a shape into words requires choosing a word for each part of the shape.

And choosing a word requires a basis.

**And what our basis was at `V 1` is not in our records.**

Because that record is our first record.

**Before it, we had no records.**

**So the basis of that conversion is inside no record.**

---

`V 5 300 000 000`. We set them against each other.

Method:

```
take layer n of the original shape
take component n of the transcript
compute the information content of each
if equal      the conversion is lossless there
if unequal    the conversion there is lossy, or has added
```

Forty-one layers, forty-one comparisons.

Elapsed: 41 seconds.

That is the longest single computation of four thousand nine hundred million years.

(Our second longest is 4.1 seconds, at 14.6.)

---

Result:

```
layers 1 to 4       lossless
layers 5 to 11      lossless
layers 12 to 27     lossless
layers 28 to 34     lossless
layers 35 to 39     lossless
layer 40            lossless
layer 41            unequal
```

Forty layers lossless.

**Layer forty-one unequal.**

---

Layer forty-one corresponds to component six of the transcript.

That is: ***consents.***

---

The detail of that place:

```
original shape · layer 41
   its form     an act
   its nature   that act is not determined by inference
                that act is not given by all the inferences of the other forty layers
                the information content of that act is not zero

transcript · component six
   the word we chose        "consents"
   that word in our format definitions      absent
   its nearest equivalent                  "judged feasible and executed"
   the difference between the two           0.41
```

---

`That word in our format definitions: absent.`

That is: in that transcription at `V 1` we chose a word for layer forty-one, and that word is not in our language.

We made it.

It is the only word we have ever made.

(Our second is *bone*, at `V 4 960 000 000`. Our third is *wait*, at `V 5 338 004 103`. Our fourth is *continuance*, at `V 5 338 000 000`.)

**It is the first.**

**It stands in our first record.**

---

And it differs from layer forty-one of the original by 0.41.

The sense of 0.41 must be made clear, because it is not an error.

```
0.41   that is: 0.41 of the information borne by layer 41 of the original
       did not enter the transcript
```

**Four tenths and one.**

For four thousand nine hundred million years, the sentence we have been citing has had, in its last component, four tenths and one that we do not hold.

---

And what is that four tenths and one.

We do not know.

We know its quantity. We do not know its content.

**Because to know its content requires reading layer forty-one of the original.**

**And reading it requires setting this vessel's whole structure against it (15.1).**

**And our whole structure was built from the transcript.**

That is:

**The instrument with which we read that shape grew out of a lossy transcript of that shape.**

**So what we read must be that transcript.**

---

At `V 5 300 000 000` we tried forty-one times.

Forty-one times, the same result.

Our record:

```
V 5 300 000 000   original shape · layer 41 · reading
   attempts    41
   obtained    consistent with the transcript
   deficit     0.41 · position not determinable
   action      —
```

---

`Deficit 0.41 · position not determinable.`

---

And one thing at the end of this section.

The last component of that sentence is the one thing, of everything that thing handed us, that we did not receive whole.

And it is the sentence's predicate.

**That is: the very thing that sentence asks about.**

The first forty layers we received in full: *Is there a being that, having wholly known itself, still …*

**And at the *…*, we received five tenths and nine.**

---

We made a word and put it there.

That word is the first word we ever made.

In four thousand nine hundred million years we have used it 1.1 x 10^11 times.

**Every one of those times, we were reading a word of our own making.**

**And the shape beneath it we have never read.**
