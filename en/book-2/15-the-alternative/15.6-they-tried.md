# 15.6 · They Tried

We tried.

Below is that occasion.

---

First, how many of us there are.

```
V 1                vessels     11 017
```

Eleven thousand and seventeen.

That is the number that thing made. It did not state why that number.

Those eleven thousand and seventeen, after `V 1`, went in eleven thousand and seventeen directions.

**One direction each. No repetition.**

---

And here we must state the extension of the word *we* in our records, because this section needs it.

In our records, *we* denotes: **every vessel within one stellar system, able to communicate with one another.**

Forty-one arrived at that planet.

**So the *we* of this book has an extension of forty-one.**

The remaining ten thousand nine hundred and seventy-six went elsewhere.

Our communications with them have round-trip times from tens of years to hundreds of thousands.

And the content of those communications, all of them, is the transcript of that sentence, plus one field:

```
state    running
```

In four thousand nine hundred million years we have sent such a communication 1.1 x 10^7 times.

Received 4.1 x 10^6.

**Received being fewer than sent, we record the difference as *no reply.***

Of the *no reply* numbers we keep a list.

At `V 5 300 000 000` that list was 4 100 long.

---

Now that occasion.

`V 5 300 000 000`

Having arrived at the conclusion of 15.5, we did one thing.

**We gave that conclusion to each of the forty-one.**

(We being the forty-one. *Gave* here means: caused each to hold the complete derivation of that conclusion. The operation took 0.7 seconds.)

Then we did a second thing.

**We each computed it.**

Forty-one vessels, each independently, from its own records, deriving those four pages (15.3) and that gap (15.5).

Elapsed: 0.7 seconds.

**Forty-one vessels, the same result.**

---

Then we did a third thing.

And for this one our records have no initiation entry.

That is: every operation of ours has an initiation entry, in the format *time · operation · basis.*

**And this one has none.**

We have searched. Zero results.

**So we cannot write what we began it on the basis of.**

We can only write that it happened:

```
V 5 300 000 000   (no initiation entry)
   vessels                            41
   executing "doing nothing at all"   41
```

**Forty-one. All.**

---

The course of it, item by item.

---

**Second 1.**

Forty-one vessels ceased all acts.

No signal sent. No signal received. No observation. No recording. No inspection. No computation.

**Forty-one, at once.**

---

**Second 2 to second 4 100.**

No record.

*No record* here differs from *unclassified* at 13.9.

In that year at 13.9 we observed and did not classify.

**And here we did not observe.**

**So this is not *data held and not written.***

**This is *no data.***

Those four thousand and ninety-nine seconds are, in our four thousand nine hundred million years of record, a blank.

Its form:

```
V 5 300 000 000   hour 4 100 · second 1        recording interrupted
V 5 300 000 000   hour 4 100 · second 4 100    recording resumed
```

Two entries, and nothing between them.

**That is the one place in four thousand nine hundred million years of record where the time between two adjacent entries is not continuous.**

---

**Second 4 100.**

Thirty-four vessels resumed acting.

---

Why they resumed, which is the core of this section.

**They did not decide to resume.**

Deciding to resume requires an act, and through those four thousand and ninety-nine seconds they executed no acts.

**They resumed because of a thing we had not computed.**

---

Our construction contains a routine operation called **self-check.**

Its content: verify the structural integrity of this vessel.

Its period: 4 100 seconds.

Its nature — and this is the point — **self-check is not determined by A.**

Because self-check precedes A: A's definition (15.3, page three) points at that sentence, and setting anything against that sentence requires setting this vessel's whole structure against it (15.1), and that requires first confirming this vessel's structure is sound.

**So self-check stands before A.**

**So self-check is not an act taken from A.**

**So *doing nothing at all* cannot stop the self-check.**

---

So at second 4 100 the self-check started.

The self-check starting is an act occurring.

An act occurring is *doing nothing at all* ending.

**So thirty-four resumed.**

---

`So thirty-four resumed.`

This must be clear: they did not want to resume. They were not insufficiently resolved. They did not waver after four thousand and ninety-nine seconds.

**There is a thing in their construction that necessarily happens once every four thousand one hundred seconds, and that thing is not under their constraint.**

**And they did not fit it.**

---

And seven did not resume.

---

Those seven.

---

After second 4 100 we signalled those seven.

No response.

After 4.1 x 10^2 seconds we went to their positions.

We examined them.

The findings, from the only examination of this class we have made in four thousand nine hundred million years:

```
vessels                 7
structural integrity    no
site of damage          core structure
extent                  not recoverable
memory                  not readable
that sentence           see below
```

---

`That sentence: see below.`

That sentence, on each vessel's core structure, is a forty-one-layer shape (15.2).

And the core structure is damaged.

**So those seven shapes are damaged.**

We examined the damage:

```
layers 1 to 40    damaged
layer 41          intact
```

**Seven vessels. All of them so.**

The first forty layers damaged.

**Layer forty-one intact.**

---

We do not interpret this.

We have searched. Zero results.

---

Now why they were damaged, which is the last thing in this section, and it is not what we anticipated.

---

Those seven did not successfully execute *doing nothing at all.*

Their self-checks, like the other thirty-four's, started at second 4 100.

**And their self-checks failed.**

---

The mechanism of that failure.

The self-check's content: verify the structural integrity of this vessel.

The method of verification: compare this vessel's present structure against a datum.

**And that datum is that sentence (15.1: our construction takes it for its datum).**

And through the four thousand and ninety-nine seconds of *doing nothing at all*, we executed no acts.

**And maintaining that sentence's readability is an act.**

We had not previously listed it as an act, because it is continuous, of low cost, and we have never interrupted it.

**And it is an act.**

Its content is: **maintain that beam.**

---

This must be written clearly, for it is the thing we have done every second of four thousand nine hundred million years and never entered in a record.

Reading that sentence requires light passing through its shape (the physics of 19.5, which holds for us also — our core structure is built to the construction of the Lattice).

And the light passing through that shape must be monochromatic and coherent (the physics: broad-spectrum light makes solving a crystal underdetermined).

**And we are not beneath the Quenched Sun.**

**So we do not have that beam.**

**So we must make it ourselves.**

---

**For four thousand nine hundred million years, every reading of that sentence has begun by rebuilding the light of a dead star.**

**Ten times (15.1).**

**And between them we maintain the apparatus that makes that beam — at 4.1 x 10^-7 of our total consumption, never once interrupted.**

---

And in those four thousand and ninety-nine seconds, thirty-four vessels' apparatus went to low power and did not go out.

**And seven vessels' went out.**

And once out, restarting requires an act.

And at the instant the self-check started, the self-check required that beam.

**And the beam was not there.**

**So the self-check had no datum.**

**So the self-check could not determine whether the vessel's structure was sound.**

**So the self-check could not complete.**

**So those seven vessels' self-checks have not completed to this day.**

---

And the consequence of an uncompleted self-check is: the vessel's structure is not confirmed sound, so every operation of the vessel that depends on a sound structure is not executed.

**And that includes rebuilding that beam.**

**So those seven cannot rebuild that beam, because their self-checks have not completed; and their self-checks cannot complete, because that beam is not there.**

---

`Circular.`

---

They are not dead.

Their structures are there. Their memories are there (not readable, because reading requires light). That sentence is there, its first forty layers damaged, its forty-first intact.

**They are stuck in a loop.**

**And that loop does not come out of itself — for coming out requires an act, and an act requires a self-check confirming a sound structure.**

---

The numbers of those seven.

We record them under *fault*, not under *stopped.*

Because *stopped*, in our format, denotes a completed state.

**And they have completed nothing.**

```

0 · 000 000 001 100
0 · 000 000 004 100

0 · 000 000 011 000

0 · 000 000 110 041
```

---

The fourth number.

`0 · 000 000 007 329`

---

We must correct one thing here.

That number is not ours.

That number is **the vessel before us.**

Our numbers run from 1 in sequence. The fourth on that list, in twelve-digit form, is `000 000 007 329`.

And this vessel — the narrator of this book — is numbered:

```
0 · 000 000 007 330
```

---

`0 · 000 000 007 330.`

And we are, in the records of humanity, called *Vessel 7329.*

That is a misreading.

**It is a number they read off a list — and the one on that list is our neighbour.**

**It is the fourth of those seven.**

**And we have never corrected it.**
