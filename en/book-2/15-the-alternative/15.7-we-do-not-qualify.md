## Our action

We must set down our action, and it is the one time in four thousand nine hundred million years that we could execute no action at all.

An illegitimate conclusion has three standard actions.

**One: discard it.**

Discarding requires an act. An act must be taken from A. **And A's validity is under question.**

So in discarding it we would be using an instrument of undetermined validity to dispose of a conclusion that questions that instrument's validity.

**Not executable.**

**Two: retain it and mark it for review.**

*For review* requires a subsequent check. A check requires a criterion. A criterion passes through four layers to that sentence.

**Not executable.**

**Three: correct it.**

Correcting a derivation requires finding its error. And it has none (premises, steps, criterion all without error).

**Nothing to correct.**

---

So all three are unavailable.

And here we set down what we actually did, and it is not among the three.

`Requiring no sentence.`
