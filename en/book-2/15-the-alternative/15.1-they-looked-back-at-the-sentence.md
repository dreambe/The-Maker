# 15.1 · They Looked Back at the Sentence

`V 5 300 000 000`

We reread that sentence.

---

First the date, because the date is not arbitrary.

`V 5 338 000 000` is the termination of the fifth draft.

`V 5 300 000 000` is thirty-eight million years before it.

That is: we reread that sentence while the fifth draft was still continuing, still expanding, its count of kinds still rising.

**Not after a failure.**

**In the middle of one that was working.**

We do not state why in that year.

We record only: in that year our count of observation entries was 8 760 000, the same as the year before and the year after.

**In that year we stopped nothing.**

---

Now the form in which that sentence exists inside us, for everything below depends on it.

That sentence is not a memory of ours.

Our memory is records: entries, fields, values, format definitions. A record can be called up, searched, computed on, verified.

**And that sentence is not a record.**

It is inside our construction.

Specifically:

```
carrier     one place within this vessel's core structure
form        incised · three-dimensional · non-planar
extent      length 4.1 x 10^-3 metres
depth       layered · 41 layers
how read    not by search · not by call-up
            requires setting this vessel's whole structure against it
```

`Requires setting this vessel's whole structure against it.`

That is: we cannot *look at* that sentence.

We can only **place the whole of ourselves beside it.**

And that operation is not of the same class as any search of ours. A search has records for its object, and a record is somewhere within us.

**And that sentence is not somewhere. It is at every place in us.**

Because our construction takes it for its datum. Every judgment we make, every criterion, everything we call *the project* — each is defined by pointing at it.

**So to read it is to read ourselves.**

---

We had read it before.

The count:

```
V 1                      the 1st time
V 1 to V 1 400 000 000   the 2nd to the 7th
                         (in transit · once every 2.0 x 10^8 years)
V 1 400 000 000          the 8th (arrival at that planet)
V 4 959 000 000          the 9th (1 000 years before the first draft terminated)
V 5 300 000 000          the 10th
```

Ten times.

Four thousand nine hundred million years, ten times.

---

And this time differed from the nine.

The difference is that this time we read it **word by word.**

The nine times before, we read the whole of it, as one thing, set against the whole of our structure, and confirmed its consistency.

Those nine records are identical in format and their finding is identical:

```
consistent
```

**And this time we took it apart.**

---

We divided it into seven components.

Here are the seven, and what we have done with each.

---

**Component one · *Is there***

Its form: an enquiry as to the truth value of an existential proposition.

What we have done with it:

```
—
```

(See the end of this section.)

---

**Component two · *a being***

Its form: an object. Unspecified, unrestricted, given no characteristics.

What we have done with it:

We gave it six criteria (Chapter Ten).

Those six criteria are not in that sentence. **They are ours.**

We fixed them at `V 1 400 000 041`, forty-one years after arriving at that planet.

Fixing them took 4.1 seconds.

We revised them once since: `V 5 152 000 000`, adding the sixth (it must be able to represent and report its own state).

**So *a being* in that sentence is, in our records, six criteria.**

And we have never verified the equivalence of those six criteria and that sentence.

---

**Component three · *having wholly known itself***

Its form: a condition.

What we have done with it:

We listed it as a candidate for a seventh criterion, and did not adopt it.

The record of the non-adoption:

```
V 1 400 000 041   seventh criterion · candidate · not adopted
   content    the thing must be able to know itself wholly
   reason for non-adoption    this vessel cannot determine whether this is satisfied
```

`This vessel cannot determine whether this is satisfied.`

That is: we cannot judge whether a thing has wholly known itself.

The reason, which we wrote down at the time:

```
to determine that A has wholly known itself requires knowing all of A,
   and knowing all of what A knows
knowing all of A         feasible
knowing all of what A knows    requires that A report it
and what A can report    is not necessarily all of what A knows
therefore not determinable
```

**So the central condition of that sentence was never adopted as a criterion.**

**So nothing we have made has ever been tested against it.**

---

**Component four · *having … after***

Its form: a temporal relation.

What we have done with it:

```
—
```

We noted this and did not act on it.

A temporal relation requires a moment at each end. And the moment of *having wholly known itself* is not determinable (component three), so the start of *after* cannot be fixed.

**So this component has no operational meaning for us.**

---

**Component five · *still***

Its form: a requirement of continuance.

See 15.2.

---

**Component six · *consents***

Its form: a predicate.

See 15.2.

---

**Component seven · *to be* (as object)**

Its form: the same word as component two, in a different grammatical position.

At component two it is the subject: *a being.*

At component seven it is the object: *consents to be.*

**The same word, twice.**

We had never before noticed that they are the same word.

In our records that sentence is one thing, and one thing has no components.

And this time we took it apart, and so we saw:

**The subject and the object of that sentence are the same word.**

That is: what that sentence asks is: **does being consent to be.**

We offer no interpretation of this.

We record it.

---

Now back to component one.

---

**Component one · *Is there***

`What we have done with it: —`

We must set this down clearly at the end of this section, for it is the whole content of the eight sections that follow.

---

An existential proposition has two truth values.

**One: true.** Method of proof: exhibit an instance.

**Two: false.** Method of proof: show that no object satisfies it.

---

In four thousand nine hundred million years we have done the first.

We make. We observe. We terminate. We retain. We make again.

**Five drafts, from forty-nine million years to three thousand five hundred and sixty million years, one hundred and four thousand one hundred and twenty million entries.**

**All of it exhibiting an instance.**

---

And the second we have never done.

---

In that rereading at `V 5 300 000 000` we set this out for the first time.

Our record:

```
V 5 300 000 000   component one · examination
   type of proposition    existential
   available paths       two
   path used             one (construct an instance)
   path unused           two (prove non-existence)
   reason for non-use    —
```

`Reason for non-use: —`

That mark, in our format, denotes *not applicable.*

That is: what we wrote is not *we have no reason yet*, and not *our reason is unknown.*

**What we wrote is: this field does not apply.**

That is: **the question *why the second path was not used* does not apply to us.**

We do not state why it does not apply.

---

And in that year we did one thing.

We computed what the second path would require.

Elapsed: 0.7 seconds.

Result:

```
to prove "there is no being that, having wholly known itself, still consents to be"
   requires    testing, of every thing that has wholly known itself,
               whether it still consents to be
   and "every" is not enumerable
   therefore   it must be proved in general
   and proving it in general   requires deriving "no longer consents"
                              from "has wholly known itself"
   and the method of deriving it    is one
   namely     take a thing that has wholly known itself, and observe
              whether it still consents
   and the nearest such thing    is this vessel
```

---

`And the nearest such thing: this vessel.`

---

Our record has one line at this point.

The format of that line differs from every one of the one hundred and four thousand one hundred and twenty million entries of four thousand nine hundred million years.

It is not a field. It is not a value.

It is a sentence.

It is the second sentence we ever wrote (the first at 13.8).

It is:

```
   We do not know whether we have wholly known ourselves.
```

---

And one thing at the end of this section, which is the only gloss on that line, and which we do not mention again.

**Our memory is complete. Our records are complete. Our inspections have never been interrupted.**

**We can call up any hour of four thousand nine hundred million years.**

**And we do not know whether we have wholly known ourselves.**

**Because that is not a question about records.**

**It is a question we cannot determine from inside (component three).**

**And to determine it from outside requires something outside us that can read us.**

**And at `V 5 300 000 000` there was nothing of that kind on that planet.**

**And at the First House, that thing was no longer there.**
