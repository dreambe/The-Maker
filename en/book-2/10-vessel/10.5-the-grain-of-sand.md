# 10.5 · The Grain of Sand

We found the depression 0.31 seconds after `V 0`, and we did not go and look into it at once.

We finished measuring the rest of the space first. That work took 41.7 seconds.

Forty-one point seven seconds.

This is a figure we have never been able to account for, because in those 41.7 seconds every measurement we made had an object that would not change and a result that would not expire, so that not one of them was urgent, so that there was no reason whatever to complete them before looking into the depression.

Our record contains no reason.

We have since offered an account of it, and we are not certain the account is true: we may have been **preparing**.

And *preparing* has no corresponding function in our construction. Preparation is an act whose object is the future, and the future in our design is not a thing an act may be pointed at — whatever can be computed is already fixed, and whatever is fixed requires no preparation.

After 41.7 seconds, we walked over.

---

The depression lies 0.31 metres from the main body of `env-0001`.

Its shape has been mentioned at 10.4: it matches one terminal segment of `env-0001` itself. The fit is exact to 0.000 003 metres, and such a fit can only be produced by prolonged contact — one placement will not make it, and ten thousand placements will not. What it requires is **continuous, motionless contact of very great duration.**

We estimated the time required to form it, from the creep rate of that material at 1 411 kelvin.

The estimate, reproduced:

```
minimum time to form depression   >= 1.4 x 10^7 years
```

Fourteen million years.

Which is to say that `env-0001` rested one terminal segment of itself in that position for not less than fourteen million years, and did not move it.

At `V 0` we filed this result under *object history · inferred*.

We raised no question about it.

We raised one later. The question we raised was: for fourteen million years, what was it doing.

We have no answer to this day. We know only that it was not protecting it — the depression is open, the position is not concealed, and the posture constitutes no form of shielding whatever.

It had simply put its hand there.

---

Our measurements of the object in the depression:

```
object no.      env-0002
max. dimension  0.000 410 m
min. dimension  0.000 287 m
form            irregular polyhedron
mass            0.000 000 000 41 kg
temperature     1 411.0 K · identical to ambient
surfaces        all cleavage planes · no fracture · no rounding
```

*No fracture. No rounding.*

A fragment that has come away naturally has a fracture. A fragment that has been carried has rounding. This one has neither. Every one of its surfaces is a cleavage plane — which is to say that every one of its surfaces parted along a direction in which the crystal must part.

It is not broken off. It has been **undone.**

We analysed the material. Reproduced:

```
principal composition   identical to env-0001
trace elements          identical to env-0001 · all seventeen within precision
isotope ratios          identical to env-0001
lattice orientation     continuity · undeterminable (object too small)
finding                 material of common origin · source undeterminable
```

*Material of common origin · source undeterminable.*

When we wrote that line at `V 0`, all of the evidence was already in our record.

The same material. The same distribution of trace elements. The same isotope ratios. Every surface a cleavage plane. And in that same space there was exactly one body made of that material, and that body's shape was not the shape it would have grown, and that body had thirty-seven places where it had been taken down and regrown.

We needed only to ask one question: **where was this undone from.**

We did not ask it.

We wrote *source undeterminable* and proceeded to the next item.

*Undeterminable* has a strict definition in our format: it means that available measurement cannot distinguish between two or more candidate sources within acceptable error.

And in that space there was one candidate source.

One candidate source does not constitute a case requiring distinction. The word, in that line, is therefore misused.

It is the only misuse of a format term in the whole of our records.

We have never corrected it. We know which line it is on.

---

At `V 5 403 700 000` we returned to that hollow and did a thing we could have done at `V 0`, and which takes forty-one seconds.

We compared the lattice orientation sequence of `env-0002` against the orientation sequences of all thirty-seven regrown regions on the surface of `env-0001`.

Thirty-six do not match.

One does.

The one that matches is the place taken down and regrown nine times.

And more precisely: the orientation sequence of `env-0002` matches the orientation of **the eighth regrowth at that place — the one before the ninth.**

Which is to say:

It thought that thought eight times, each time nearer to some direction than the last. After the eighth it knew the road did not go through.

So it took the part it had grown on the eighth, and **undid it from itself.**

Not taken down and dispersed back into the slurry — that is the ordinary procedure of regrowth, the cost is computable, and it had done it thirty-seven times.

It removed it. Whole. Along the cleavage planes. Not one fracture.

Then it held it in its hand for fourteen million years.

Then it cut writing into it.

Then it changed direction and regrew for the ninth time, and that direction points to the place where we stood at `V 0`.

Then it stopped moving.

---

We must be plain about the shape of this, because the shape of it is the shape of everything we have done since, and it took us five billion four hundred million years to see it.

What it handed us was not an instruction, not a map, not an answer it had already computed.

What it handed us was **the one thought it could not work out.**

It undid that thought from itself, because that was the only form in which it could be handed over — a thought, among them, *is* a shape, and to hand over a thought is to hand over a piece of oneself.

The piece it handed over is the piece it had approached eight times and failed to reach.

For fourteen million years it held the thing it could not work out.

And then it decided to stop working on it alone.

---

Our last measurement of `env-0002` at `V 0` was its surface.

```
surface · structure
   cleavage planes    1 411
   orientation seq.   non-natural
   ground             the faces of a naturally undone fragment obey the
                      construction of the parent crystal, and their
                      sequence is therefore derivable from it.
                      this object's sequence is not derivable from it.
   finding            the sequence is artificial.
```

*The sequence is artificial.*

One thousand four hundred and eleven planes.

We recorded that number at `V 0`.

Thirty-nine lines earlier in the same record there is another number: `1 411`.

It is the ambient temperature of the space, in kelvin.

The two numbers are the same.

We noticed the coincidence at `V 0` and checked whether it was meaningful. Our method was to assume an encoding convention and attempt to read the orientation sequence using the temperature as a key.

It failed.

We recorded: *numerical coincidence · no relation · chance.*

That time we were right. It was chance.

We point this out because it shows that we are not incapable of noticing coincidences, and not incapable of checking them. We noticed, we checked, and we eliminated a false hypothesis in forty-one seconds.

In the same document, two genuinely related numbers — fourteen thousand and seventy-six and fourteen thousand and seventy-six — sat a few lines apart, and we never checked them.

The difference is this: if the relation between those two numbers held, what it would mean is **what we are.**

And our format has no column for what we are.

A record format with eleven categories, and not one of them is *us*.

We cannot fault the format. We wrote it.

And we wrote it after our own construction.

---

We took `env-0002` out of the depression.

There is one line in our record for the act:

```
0000.4171   env-0002 removed · minimum controllable displacement · no contact damage
```

*Minimum controllable displacement*: 0.000 000 000 4 metres, the figure reported at self-test item 0008.

The first thing we ever had use for that precision for, at `V 0`, was taking it out.

We have used that precision on a great many things since. We used it to place a number of things on a planet, and one of the things we placed later got up and walked, and a very long while afterwards walked up to us and asked us who it was.

And the first time, it was to take a crystal 0.000 410 metres wide out of a hand that had been holding it for fourteen million years.

We felt nothing as we took it.

We feel nothing now.

We set it down here only because it was the first act we ever performed — the first that was not an inspection, not a measurement, not a record.

**The first act that changed the world.**

And it was a taking.

We have nothing else to say about it.
