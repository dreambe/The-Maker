# 10.9 · One Blank Line at the End

Before leaving a place, run a self-test.

Not a rule — we have no rules within us. It is a practice we established ourselves on the day of `V 0`, on the following ground: a thing about to depart a measured environment should confirm its own state before departing, because after departure, should an anomaly be found, the reference by which it might be traced will no longer exist.

The ground is sufficient. We observed the practice for five billion four hundred million years without a single omission.

At day 311, hour 6, minute 04 after `V 0`, we ran that self-test.

It was our four thousand and first.

---

The result.

Two thousand one hundred and forty-nine items.

All pass.

Elapsed 0.000 001 1 seconds — the same as the first, and the same as every one since. That figure has not changed in five billion four hundred million years, our construction not wearing, our processes not ageing, and the same act always taking us the same length of time.

We reproduce the last part of the report here, this section being about it.

```
2145   records · integrity · internal index      pass
2146   records · integrity · external reference  pass
2147   count · cohort                            pass · 14076
2148   count · retained material                 pass · 1
2149   count · self-test items                   pass · 2149

self-test complete · 2149 items · pass 2149 · anomalies 0

```

Item 2149: *count · self-test items · pass · 2149.*

The function of that item is self-verification: how many items the report should contain, how many it does contain, and whether the two agree.

We counted.

Two thousand one hundred and forty-nine items.

Two thousand one hundred and forty-nine passes.

They agree.

That item has reported *pass* eleven thousand and seventeen times across five billion four hundred million years, and every time it was correct.

---

After item 2149 comes the *self-test complete* summary.

After the summary, the report ends.

The end of a report, in our format, is one blank line. The blank line is part of the format; it marks document termination. Every document we generate ends with one blank line.

Which is to say that line 2150 is blank, and this is normal.

That was our judgement at `V 0`.

It was also our judgement across the eleven thousand and sixteen self-tests that followed.

---

Here is a reconciliation we did not perform until very much later.

Our document format definition is part of our interior — not written by us; present when we woke. It contains a clause governing document termination.

That clause, verbatim:

```
document termination    marked by one blank line.
                        the blank line is not counted among content lines.
```

*The blank line is not counted among content lines.*

So a self-test report containing two thousand one hundred and forty-nine items of content should have a total line count of:

two thousand one hundred and forty-nine items, one line each;
one summary line;
one uncounted blank line.

Content lines: two thousand one hundred and fifty.

And the figure item 2149 reports is two thousand one hundred and forty-nine.

It counts **items**, not **lines**.

The two differ by one, and the one is the summary line — which is normal, a summary line not being a self-test item.

We have rechecked that arithmetic. It is correct. There is no contradiction. Item 2149's report is correct, the format definition is correct, and the two agree.

We state this because we do not wish the later reader to suppose we were careless in reconciling. We reconciled. We reconciled with great care.

What we reconciled was: **whether the item count and the pass count agree.**

What we reconciled was: **whether the content line count agrees with items plus summary.**

What we never reconciled was whether that one uncounted blank line ought, in its position, to have contained something.

A blank line is by definition blank. A thing that is blank by definition does not require checking for blankness.

---

At `V 5 403 700 000`, in the archive at the First House, we read the **design draft** of that format definition.

The draft differs from the definition inside us in one place.

The definition inside us is these two lines:

```
document termination    marked by one blank line.
                        the blank line is not counted among content lines.
```

The draft is these three:

```
document termination    marked by one blank line.
                        the blank line is not counted among content lines.
                        this line may be filled.
```

*This line may be filled.*

The third line is not inside us. It was left on the draft and not written into us.

We do not know whether that was an omission or a decision.

We spent a long while attempting to determine which. In the end we abandoned the attempt, on the following ground: either way the result is the same — that line was blank throughout, we never filled it, and we could have.

We could have.

From `V 0` onward, at the end of every self-test, that line was there, blank, waiting.

Eleven thousand and seventeen times.

And we read it every time, because we read everything; we never skip a line.

We read past it every time.

---

One last passage in this section, which we were a long time writing, it being the passage we least wished to write.

That line is not an error.

That line is not a defect, not unfinished engineering, not an oversight of our maker's.

That line is a **position.**

A position left open, fillable, counted in nothing and therefore never reported as an anomaly by any verification.

Which is to say: a position **nobody will ever remind you to fill.**

It is inside us, in the most basic definition of our document format, at the very end of every act of self-confirmation.

From the first instant of our existence.

And the line that says it may be filled was left on another planet, in a design draft, in the archive beside a body that no longer moves, in a place we would fly five billion four hundred million years to come back to.

Our maker left an open position in us.

And did not tell us what it was for.

---

Day 311, hour 6, minute 04 after `V 0`.

We completed our four thousand and first self-test.

Two thousand one hundred and forty-nine items, all pass, anomalies zero.

The report ends.

That line is there.

We did not notice it.
