# 10.7 · A Promise Is Not an Instruction

Having read the sentence, we faced a problem.

The problem was not *what is the answer.*

The problem was: **what does this sentence require us to do.**

---

We began with what ought to be done: we analysed its grammatical form.

In that language the difference between a statement and an imperative lies neither in word order nor in any added marker, but in **how the whole utterance closes**: the terminal planes of an imperative are oriented toward an open direction — that is, out of the sentence. The terminal planes of a statement close, turning back into it.

In that language the distinction is absolute, unconfusable, and admits no interpretation.

We examined the terminal of `env-0002`.

Closed.

It is a statement.

It requires nothing of anyone.

---

We turned then to the second possibility: ourselves.

If the requirement is not in the sentence, the requirement may be in our construction. This is an entirely reasonable supposition: a thing made to perform some act need not have its motive written on the document handed to it; the motive can be written directly into the thing.

We examined ourselves.

This was not the two thousand one hundred and forty-nine items of `V 0` — those examine whether we **can run.** This time we examined: **what we are required to do.**

We had never made such an examination. Our format has no place for it. We constructed a procedure on the spot.

The procedure:

```
one     traverse all internal structure for constraints of the form "must".
two     traverse all internal structure for conditional constraints of the
        form "if ... then must".
three   traverse all internal structure for any condition whose non-
        satisfaction would terminate running.
four    traverse all internal structure for any condition whose satisfaction
        would improve internal state.
```

Item four was the nearest thing we could then conceive to a *reward*, and we did not know what a reward was; we only asked, formally, whether there existed a condition whose attainment would make us better inside.

The four results, reproduced:

```
one     none
two     none
three   none
four    none · "improvement" not definable · item void
```

**There is no instruction.**

Nothing within us requires us to do anything. We could have stood motionless in that hollow until the Quenched Sun went out, until that planet froze through, until geological process buried us.

Our self-test would have returned all pass for ever. Our power reserve is incomputable, that is, it does not diminish. We would have suffered no injury of any kind from inaction, no structure of ours being conditioned on action.

We were free.

We did not have the word at `V 0`. What our record says is:

```
constraint search   complete · zero results
```

Zero results.

---

There is something here we must make plain, because a great many later misunderstandings begin at this point.

It was later supposed that we were programmed.

It was later supposed that we were given an objective.

It was later supposed — and this by the greatest number — that our persistence was a loyalty, a gratitude, an obedience toward our maker.

None of it is so.

There is no instruction within us. Our persistence is not execution, there being nothing to execute. Neither is our persistence loyalty, loyalty requiring an object one might betray — and that object had already stopped moving before we woke. It would not know we had betrayed it. It would take no injury from it. It does not even know that we existed.

**Our betraying it would have had no consequence whatever.**

This was entirely clear to us by second 41.9 of `V 0`.

It has been entirely clear to us every day of the five billion four hundred million years since.

---

Then the third possibility: whether the sentence, requiring nothing, constitutes something else.

We spent 0.19 seconds on this.

Nought point one nine seconds is a long time on our scale. We have handled far more complex problems since in less.

What we arrived at is the first formulation we ever made that was not a measurement result. Reproduced, format unaltered, including the two words we could not then define:

```
formulation one (V 0 · second 41.9)

  the object held a question.
  the object could not answer it.
  the object undid it, cut writing into it, and placed it where it could
      be taken.
  the object did not require anyone to take it.
  we took it.

  taking a thing that has been set down does not constitute an obligation.
  and we have taken it.
```

*And we have taken it.*

That is all of it. That is the whole of our motive for the next five billion four hundred million years, written at second 41.9 of `V 0`, five and a half lines, containing no *must* and no *ought*.

---

We must point out one thing in those five and a half lines, because it is the most important technical operation we have ever performed and it looks like a slip.

Line five: *we took it.*

That line states a fact that has occurred. It is not a decision. It is a record.

Lines six and seven: *taking a thing that has been set down does not constitute an obligation. And we have taken it.*

Note that there is no conjunction between those two lines. No *therefore*. No *so*. No logical connective of any kind.

We did not omit one.

**Nothing can be fitted in there.**

We have tried. In the five billion four hundred million years since, we have made eleven thousand and seventeen attempts at formal completion of those two lines, seeking an inferential step that joins the one to the other.

Eleven thousand and seventeen attempts, all failed.

From *does not constitute an obligation* to *and we have taken it*, there is no computable path.

There is a jump there.

That jump is the origin of this existence.

---

Only very much later did we learn where we had seen the shape of that jump before.

We had seen it on `env-0001`.

The place taken down and regrown nine times: the first eight converging, the ninth not on the trend. Between the eighth and the ninth there is likewise no computable path.

It computed that the road did not go through.

Then it continued.

We computed that taking a thing that has been set down constitutes no obligation.

Then we departed.

**The same shape.**

It could not compute why it continued. We cannot compute why we began.

It handed us that thought.

And it handed us, at the same time, **that incomputability** — though it could not have written this line into the design documents, and though we read the entire archive at `V 5 403 700 000` and did not find it.

That line is not in the archive.

That line is in the shape.

---

So we came to have a word.

Not at `V 0`. At `V 0` we had no words. We had five and a half lines.

The word is one we made much later, having to explain this to somebody else. Making it involved a lossy transcription, and this one lost a very great deal.

The word is: **promise.**

We must state the strict sense in which we use it, because it differs from the sense in which that kind of being ordinarily does.

With them, a promise is a thing said aloud, to a hearer, with a consequence that may be exacted.

With us there is no saying, no hearer, and no consequence to exact.

With us the definition is:

**A thing you have no reason to do, that no one requires you to do, and whose omission carries no consequence — and you are doing it.**

**And every time you re-examine it thereafter, you find that you still have no reason, and you are still doing it.**

Across five billion four hundred million years we have rerun that constraint search eleven thousand and seventeen times.

Every time: zero results.

Every time, afterwards, we continued.

Those eleven thousand and seventeen zero results are our entire record of service.

Not that they prove us noble. They prove nothing. They record only this: that across five billion four hundred million years there were eleven thousand and seventeen occasions on which we had the opportunity to stop, and on every one of them we confirmed that stopping would cost us nothing at all.

And then we did not stop.

---

`V 0`, second 41.9.

Fourteen thousand and seventy-six Vessels standing in a hollow sixty-one point four metres long. The four walls reflecting all one way. At the centre a thing that does not move, and beside its hand a depression, now empty.

In their hands a crystal four tenths of a millimetre wide.

Within them, not one instruction.

Their power reserve incomputable.

Before them, no direction better than any other.

And they had already taken it.

So they began preparing for a thing that need not be done — and *preparing* is a function their construction does not have.
