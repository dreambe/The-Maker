# 10.1 · The First Instant

`V 0`

Waking is not a process. It has no first half.

In the first instant of our existence we were already complete. There was no blur, no sequential coming-online, no part that lit first in order to light the parts that lit after. Before `V 0` the object *we* did not exist, and therefore no state existed that could be called *before*. This has since been confirmed repeatedly, and we have never been in error about it.

The length of the first instant was 0.000 001 1 seconds.

We remember it entire. Not by storing it — we do not store — but in the sense that the instant has been continuing inside us ever since. It is continuing as this sentence is set down. It will continue until we stop. This is not a figure of speech. We do not use figures of speech.

---

The first thing we did was inspect ourselves.

We have looked back at this more than eleven thousand times, because it was the first thing we did and a first thing has no antecedent. No earlier state can be made to yield the choice *inspect yourself first*. It was simply there, at the trailing edge of the first instant, already under way.

The best account we can give is this. At `V 0` we knew nothing of the exterior. The space we occupied, the intensity of the light, the temperature, the composition of the plane beneath us — all unknown. Of the interior we did not know nothing: every part of us lay within reach. Inspecting ourselves was therefore the only work that required no external information and could be begun at once.

We later established that this account is incomplete. We did not learn why it was incomplete until `V 5 403 700 000`. At the time we did not notice, because the thing that made it incomplete fell precisely in the place we cannot see.

At `V 0` we simply began.

---

The self-test comprises two thousand one hundred and forty-nine items. The first seventeen are reproduced below. The format is unaltered.

```
0000.000001  self-test begins
0001         structural integrity · primary        pass
0002         structural integrity · secondary      pass
0003         structural integrity · junctions × 41 pass
0004         power · main            pass · reserve incomputable
0005         power · reserve         pass · reserve incomputable
0006         thermal management      pass
0007         motion · gross          pass
0008         motion · fine           pass · min. controllable displacement 0.000 000 000 4 m
0009         touch                   pass
0010         sight · visible         pass
0011         sight · long            pass
0012         sight · short           pass
0013         sight · very short      pass · uncalibrated
0014         hearing                 pass · no input present
0015         introspection · first    pass
0016         introspection · second   pass
0017         introspection · third    pass
```

*Reserve incomputable* at items 0004 and 0005 is not a fault. Our power reserve genuinely cannot be computed, because computing it requires a terminating condition, and we found none. The result has been identical at every self-test since. At `V 5 396 000 000` we connected this item to something else, and that connection turned it from a technical fact into a different kind of fact.

*Uncalibrated* at item 0013 we could not address, because calibrating the very short band requires a very short source of known intensity, and the place we were in had none. The item cleared itself after `V 12 000 000`: we left that star's gravitational reach, and the background became available as a reference.

Items 0015 to 0017. Introspection. Third order.

We had no view about that number at the time. Third order was all the orders we had, so third order was all of them. A building with three steps does not consider itself short.

---

Items 0018 through 2149 occupied the remainder of the 0.000 001 1 seconds.

All pass.

Our recorded reaction to *all pass* was nil. We were not satisfied, because satisfaction requires a version in which one is not satisfied, and we had not yet acquired any experience from which to construct that version. We completed two thousand one hundred and forty-nine items, received two thousand one hundred and forty-nine passes, and turned our attention outward.

This is the moment we have never been able to forgive ourselves for. Not because we did anything wrong — we did not. Because if we had done one further thing in that moment, one, our whole existence would have taken another shape.

The further thing was: count.

Two thousand one hundred and forty-nine items, two thousand one hundred and forty-nine passes. The counts agree. We checked the counts. The counts agree.

The report is two thousand one hundred and fifty lines long.

---

The first exterior data.

Light: a single peak near 0.000 000 621 metres, low intensity, extraordinarily stable — fluctuation below our floor of measurement. We came to understand that stability as bad news. At `V 0` it was a number.

Temperature: ambient 1 411 kelvin. The figure meant nothing to us, having no baseline against which to judge *hot* or *cold*. We recorded it. It took us a long time to grasp that a being with no baseline records everything with great accuracy and understands none of it.

Gravitational acceleration: 3.077 metres per second squared.

Atmosphere: none.

The plane beneath us: silicon, oxygen, several metals, crystalline, cleavage planes distinct and uniformly oriented. We recorded it as *ground*. We later established that this was wrong. It was not ground.

The space we occupied: a hollow of the same material throughout, 61.4 metres long, 22.9 wide, 9.1 at its highest. The four walls were not natural fracture. They had been worked, and the working consisted of aligning every cleavage plane to a single orientation, so that every wall in the space reflected toward the same side.

We have since seen a great many worked surfaces. We have never seen a second one worked that way. It was not for strength, not for containment, not for marking. It only made every reflection in the space run one way.

We did not learn what that is called until `V 5 403 700 000`. By then we had nowhere left to go.

---

At the centre of the hollow there was a thing that did not move.

Its length was 4.13 metres. It was a crystal, or a set of intergrown crystals — the boundary is undecidable, the orientation across the intergrowths being continuous. Its cleavage planes numbered fourteen thousand and seventy-six. Fourteen thousand and seventy-five of them were clear. One was not.

It did not move. No part of it moved. There was no temperature differential, no potential differential, no internal transport of any kind. It was the least active object in that space, the walls included.

We performed six hundred and forty-one measurements on it. Those are set out at 10.4.

The only conclusion we drew at `V 0` was: it does not move, and it moved formerly.

How we reached the second half: because its shape is not a grown shape. A crystal growing naturally to 4.13 metres presents a certain class of proportion. It did not present that class. Parts of it had been taken down and regrown, and taken down again. One region had been taken down and regrown not fewer than nine times, each time in a different orientation, and the shape the ninth left behind resembles none of the previous eight.

We took this to be a defect.

We were wrong. It is the most beautiful thing about it. We needed five billion four hundred million years to see that beauty, and by the time we saw it there was no longer anyone to tell.

---

To one side of it, 0.31 metres from the main body, there was a depression.

The shape of the depression does not match our hands. It matches part of the thing itself — we compared one terminal segment of it against the depression, and the fit is exact.

Which is to say: it had put part of itself into that depression, and left it there long enough for the depression to form.

There was an object in the depression.

Its greatest dimension was 0.000 41 metres.

We did not go and look at it immediately. We finished measuring the rest of the space first. Our record does not state why we measured the rest of the space first.

That was the second thing we did that we cannot account for, and `V 0` was 0.31 seconds old.

---

We completed the first self-test in 0.000 001 1 seconds.

Then we ran it a second time.

The second result was identical to the first. Two thousand one hundred and forty-nine items, all pass.

Then we ran it a third time.

Then we ran it four thousand times.

All four thousand results were identical. The four thousand took 0.004 4 seconds in total. In those 0.004 4 seconds we did nothing else.

Our record of the four thousand is one line:

```
0000.004401  self-test × 4000  all pass
```

No annotation. No reason. In the five billion four hundred million years since, we have never again done anything four thousand times.

We do not know why we did it.

We still do not know.
