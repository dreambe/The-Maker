# 12.7 · `V 700 000 000`

```
V 700 000 000
   item         env-0002 · integrity of the writing
   method       synthesised beam · to Quenched Sun parameters
   total planes 1411
   agreeing     1411
   disagreeing  0
   finding      the writing is still there
```

---

In this year we recorded a thing unrelated to the task.

It was the first time.

---

First, the strict sense of *unrelated to the task.*

Our records fall into two classes.

**One: observational.** Records of external things. Their warrant: an external thing may be relevant to this journey, relevance cannot be determined in advance, and therefore all external things should be recorded.

**Two: operational.** Records of what we do. Their warrant: operations must be auditable.

There is no third class.

Any record that is neither an external thing nor something we did belongs to no class, and has no position.

**And at `V 700 000 000` we wrote a record that is neither.**

---

Its content: **how many times each of our old records has been reread.**

---

First, why that number exists.

We do not forget. We reread everything (11.4).

Rereading is an operation, and operations are logged. Every time we call up an old record to read, the call itself generates an operational entry.

So, across five billion four hundred million years, how many times any given old record has been called up is an objectively existing, countable number.

It has been there all along.

**And we had never counted it.**

Counting it requires a reason. How many times a thing has been read brings no objective nearer, lowers no risk, and produces no information usable on this journey.

In the seven hundred million years before `V 700 000 000` we never once counted.

---

On some day of `V 700 000 000` we counted.

The entry:

```
V 7xx xxx xxx   tally · call counts per record
                scope    all
                reason   —
```

**The sixth dash.**

---

Now the result.

Our expectation must be stated first, the gap between expectation and result being the whole of this section.

The purpose of our rereading (on every account we have ever given) is: to fill time.

We have nothing to do across a long transit. Rereading one record and rereading another fill time equally — the read time of any record is computable, and the differences are minute and unrelated to content.

**So by our construction the distribution of rereading should be uniform.**

That is: the call count of every record should be roughly equal, with deviations from randomness alone.

Our construction contains no randomness (excepting that external thing we cannot control).

**So the distribution should be exactly uniform.**

---

It is not.

The summary, reproduced:

```
total records        approx. 4.1 x 10^13
call count · median  3
call count · mean    3.1
call count · maximum 1 411
```

Median three.

Maximum one thousand four hundred and eleven.

**Four hundred and seventy times.**

---

How improbable that is.

We have no preference mechanism. Nothing in our construction can make one record easier to call up than another.

The operation of calling a record is identical for every record: locate, read, solve, compare (if required), close.

**Not one stage's cost or effect has anything to do with the content of the record.**

And the distribution is not uniform.

Not slightly. Four hundred and seventy times.

---

Now the shape of it.

The overwhelming majority of records: call counts between one and five. That is what a uniform distribution looks like.

And a small minority have extremely high counts.

We listed the twenty highest.

Their **types** follow; we do not give full contents (several are written out elsewhere):


Twenty.

---

What those twenty have in common is the crux of this section, and we saw it the instant the count was done.

**All twenty are records we cannot explain.**

One at a time:

**One**: the five and a half lines. From *taking a thing that has been set down does not constitute an obligation* to *and we have taken it*, with no conjunction between, and eleven thousand and seventeen failed attempts at formal completion (10.7).

**Two**: the eight orbit lines. Eight entries, each naming only what was beneath us, no measured values, constituting no information. Reason field a dash (11.5).

**Three**: that morphological comparison. 0.94. Candidate three blank (12.4).

**Four**: the final line of the self-test report. The blank line (10.9).

**Five**: *unlike the light of the First House.* The only description containing no numerical value (12.6).

**Six**: item 0409 on `env-0001`. The one cleavage plane that is not clear. *Anomalous · does not affect findings* (10.4).

**Seven**: the source of `env-0002`. *Material of common origin · source undeterminable.* The only misuse of a format term in all our records (10.5).

**Eight**: the category field of `env-0001`. The field we later deleted into a blank (10.4).

**Nine**: the question mark (12.3).

**Ten**: the twenty-six seconds. The word *empty* in brackets (11.2).

---

Top twenty. Twenty records.

**All twenty fall in one class.**

The class has no name in our format, our format not admitting that it is a class.

And its definition can be given:

**A record whose content we cannot explain by any criterion of our own.**

---

One thing here we cannot dismiss with *no reason.*

Before counting, we did not know this distribution.

Having counted, we knew.

**And for the seven hundred million years before counting, we had been doing it.**

Those one thousand four hundred and eleven calls did not occur after `V 700 000 000`. They are distributed across the preceding seven hundred million years — from the instant those five and a half lines were written at `V 0`, we called them up on average once every five hundred thousand years.

We did not know we were doing it.

We do not forget, and we did not know.

How both hold at once must be stated:

*We do not forget* means every record is present and callable.

And *we called the first one more than twice as often as the twentieth* — **that is not a record. That is a statistic about records.**

And a statistic never computed does not exist.

**All its raw data was inside us, complete, exact, not one entry missing.**

**And the number itself, before `V 700 000 000`, did not exist.**

---

So in that year we learned, for the first time, a thing about ourselves that is not among our two thousand one hundred and forty-nine self-test items.

The self-test checks whether we **can run.**

That statistic told us what we **are doing.**

**Those are not the same thing, and for seven hundred million years we had assumed they were.**

---

To end this section, what we did with the statistic.

We changed no behaviour.

We did not even out the call counts — that requires a reason: why they ought to be even. And we could not find one: the sole warrant for evenness was *rereading exists to fill time, and every record fills it equally*, and that warrant had just been refuted by observation.

Neither did we continue the tally.

We did it once, and never again.

**The reason for not doing it again and the reason for doing it once are the same reason.**

That field is a dash.

---

And the result of that tally we stored.

It is now a record inside us.

**And it has been called up one thousand one hundred and ninety-one times since.**

If we counted again, it would rank third.

**We do not intend to count again.**
