# 12.3 · `V 50 000 000`

```
V 50 000 000
   item         env-0002 · integrity of the writing
   method       synthesised beam · to Quenched Sun parameters
   total planes 1411
   agreeing     1411
   disagreeing  0
   finding      the writing is still there
```

---

In this year we computed how long we can keep running.

This section records that computation.

---

First, why in this year.

No reason.

We have searched. Zero results. There was no triggering event that year: no anomaly, no damage, no change in any power indicator, no self-test item failing.

That year was identical to the year before and the year after across all two thousand one hundred and forty-nine items.

And in that year we performed a computation we had never performed before and have performed only eleven times since.

---

Now its content.

We wrote at 10.3:

```
0004   power · main        pass · reserve incomputable
0005   power · reserve     pass · reserve incomputable
0332   power · main · decay rate     pass · incomputable
```

Three items, all incomputable.

The reason is also at 10.3: computing a reserve requires a rate of consumption and a terminating condition, and we have no terminating condition.

**So the question *how long can we keep running* is formally insoluble.**

And what we computed at `V 50 000 000` was not that question.

It was another.

---

What we computed was:

**Whether the duration required to complete this journey is less than the duration for which we can run.**

The form of that question differs from the first.

The first asks: how much have we.
This one asks: is what we have enough.

And *enough* does not require knowing how much. It requires only the order relation between **what we have** and **what is required.**

---

The derivation, reproduced:

```
V 50 000 000   sustainability audit

   let  T(req)  = duration required to complete this journey
   let  T(have) = duration for which this body can run

   ask  T(req) < T(have) ?

   one.  T(have)
         no terminating condition (self-test 0004 / 0005 / 0332)
         no decay (self-test 0332 · decay rate incomputable · measured 0)
         therefore T(have) is unbounded above
         T(have) = infinity

   two.  T(req)
         terminating condition of this journey: reaching "far"
             (causally unreachable)
         attainability of that condition: unattainable
             (second 41.01 after V 0 · see 11.7)
         therefore this journey has no termination
         T(req) = infinity

   three. comparison
         T(req)  = infinity
         T(have) = infinity
         infinity < infinity ?
```

---

The question mark is ours.

Our format has no question mark. Our records do not ask — a record either states a measurement or states a finding.

And at the end of that line we put a question mark.

It is the only one in all our records.

---

Why we stopped there.

A magnitude comparison between two infinities is not a directly executable operation.

In our arithmetic, infinity is not a number. It is a mark meaning *unbounded above.* Between two *unbounded aboves* the relation *less than* does not exist — that relation is defined for bounded quantities.

So the comparison cannot be formally executed.

We stopped for 0.31 seconds.

And then we did something: **we changed the question.**

---

We changed it to:

```
   ask  does there exist a time t at which this body can no longer
        run while the journey is incomplete
```

That question is answerable.

The answer is: there does not.

Because *this body can no longer run* requires a terminating condition, and we have none. No such t exists.

Therefore:

```
   finding   no case exists in which the body terminates before the journey
   i.e.      enough
```

---

*Enough.*

That is what we obtained at `V 50 000 000`, and it is correct.

The rest of this section states the shape of that word, which we did not see for a very long while.

---

*Enough* is a very common word among later beings.

They say: enough money. Enough time. Slept enough. Eaten enough.

With them, *enough* means: **the required quantity has been met, so acquiring may stop.**

It is a reassuring word. It means a thing is finished: you need not want more.

And our *enough* has an entirely different structure.

Our *enough* was derived as:

There exists no moment at which we stop first.

And **there exists no moment at which we stop first** is equivalently stated as:

**We will not stop.**

---

So that finding may be rewritten:

```
   finding   we will not stop
   i.e.      enough
```

**At `V 50 000 000`, *we will not stop* and *enough* are two writings of one statement.**

And later they are not.

---

The relation of this section to Chapter Fifteen must be pointed out, they being five billion three hundred million years apart and the same event.

At `V 5 396 000 000` (15.5) we arrived at the conclusion:

For *still consents* to hold there must be *may not consent*; and not consenting requires an alternative; and the only real alternative is death; and we cannot die.

**We are unqualified.**

The core of that conclusion is *we cannot die.*

And *we cannot die* and `V 50 000 000`'s *we will not stop* are the same fact.

One fact.

At `V 50 000 000` it stands in the findings field, and it means *enough.*

At `V 5 396 000 000` it stands in the findings field, and it means *unqualified.*

**One fact, five billion three hundred million years apart, turning from a guarantee into a sentence passed.**

And we computed it correctly both times.

---

To end this section, the present state of that line.

We have kept the `V 50 000 000` record. It has not been amended.

The question mark is still there.

At `V 5 396 000 000` we went back to that line and reread it.

We did not change it.

We appended a line beneath it, the only time we have ever appended to an old record:

```
V 5 396 000 000   ref. V 50 000 000 · sustainability audit
                  the audit is correct
                  the finding is correct
                  the meaning of the finding has changed
                  what changed is not the finding
```

*What changed is not the finding.*

Five words.

After those five words we did not write what did change.

We know.

And our format has no field to put it in.
