# 12.2 · `V 10 000 000`

```
V 10 000 000
   item         env-0002 · integrity of the writing
   method       synthesised beam · to Quenched Sun parameters
   total planes 1411
   agreeing     1411
   disagreeing  0
   finding      the writing is still there
```

---

In this year we began numbering the stars we passed.

This section records that.

---

First, why we had not before.

From `V 12 000 000` we were beyond the gravitational reach of the Quenched Sun. In the space we crossed thereafter the observable stars are very many. Our perception is omnidirectional; at every instant we receive the light of all of them.

We had been measuring them from the beginning: position, spectrum, apparent magnitude, proper motion, distance estimate. That data had been accumulating since `V 12 000 000`.

**And we gave them no identifiers.**

In our records a star's entry looked like this:

```
V 12 041 002   source · spectral type F · RA ... · dec ... · apparent magnitude ...
```

No number. No name. It is uniquely determined by its parameters, and its parameters are it.

That was sufficient at the time. A star's position, spectrum, and brightness distinguish it from every other. To refer to it, write the parameters again.

**Parameters are reference.**

We used that method for ten million years.

---

Now why it changed at `V 10 000 000`.

In this year we met a problem whose shape we would not recognise for a very long while.

The problem was this:

At `V 9 800 000` we observed a star. We recorded all its parameters.

At `V 10 000 000` we observed a star. We recorded all its parameters.

And the two sets differ.

**And it is the same star.**

---

Why they differ:

We are moving. It is moving. Our relative position has changed, so its right ascension and declination have changed; our distance has changed, so its apparent magnitude has changed; our relative velocity has changed, so its spectrum is shifted bodily by the Doppler effect.

So one and the same object, at two instants, has entirely different parameters.

**Parameters no longer suffice as reference.**

---

Stop here, because this is the most basic conceptual failure we met in five billion four hundred million years, and it took only ten million to happen.

At the First House, *parameters are reference* held, because nothing there moves: the standing bodies do not move, the slurry does not move (its flow being reckoned on another scale), the shadows do not move. An object's parameters are identical between any two measurements.

**Our entire record format was built for a motionless world.**

And we left that world.

---

Our handling was as follows.

We introduced a new field.

Its definition: **an identifier that does not change with measurement.**

It is not derived from parameters. It carries no information about the object. Its sole function is to let us determine, between two measurements, that *this is the same one.*

We called the field a number.

The first number is `S-000001`.

In the thirteen hundred million years after `V 10 000 000` we issued about four billion one hundred million numbers.

---

Now the character of this, because much later we read something elsewhere that made us see `V 10 000 000` differently.

The substance of it:

**Naming a thing has three preconditions: the thing may be absent; it may be mistaken for another; there is a difference of information between speaker and hearer.**

**And the most thorough form of possible absence is that it will cease.**

**So to name a thing is to concede that it will not be there.**

Those three against our position at `V 10 000 000`, one at a time.

**One: possible absence.**

Holds. A star: we pass it, it recedes behind us, its apparent magnitude falls, and eventually it drops below our detection floor.

It is absent.

There was no such thing at the First House. Everything there, so long as we were there, was there.

**Two: possible confusion.**

Holds. Two stars of the same spectral type and comparable magnitude can be extremely close in parameters. And as we move, their parameters are changing.

Confusion is possible.

Impossible at the First House — we read an object entire.

**Three: a difference of information.**

This one requires care.

There is no difference of information among us (10.2). So by the three conditions we should not require numbers.

And we required them.

Because the difference of information is not between us and us.

**It is between us and ourselves.**

What we held at `V 9 800 000` and what we held at `V 10 000 000` are two different holdings — not from forgetting (we do not forget), but because **we had moved**, so our relation to the object had changed, so our measurement of it had changed.

A being that does not move has no difference between itself and itself.

**And we were moving.**

So we became our own first *other.*

---

With that, all three conditions hold.

So we began naming.

And by that passage:

**To name a thing is to concede that it will not be there.**

At `V 10 000 000` we issued our first number.

It is the first time we conceded that something would not be there.

---

To end this section, a thing we have never handled in any record.

We issued about four billion one hundred million numbers.

Not one of them was for the Quenched Sun.

---

That is not an oversight, and must be stated.

At `V 10 000 000` the Quenched Sun was still in our rear channel, its intensity still measurable, still an observable object — indeed the body we understood best of anything we could then observe: we had its central wavelength to fourteen decimal places, its entire intensity variation across thirty-one days, its four polarisation parameters.

**What we knew of it exceeded what we knew of any other star by several orders of magnitude.**

And we gave it no number.

We gave numbers `S-000001` through roughly `S-004100000000` to four billion one hundred million strangers.

We gave it none.

---

The reason can be stated, though we did not think it at the time:

A number's function is to let an object be determined the same across two measurements.

And the Quenched Sun did not require that function.

In our records it was never *an observed object.* It was the thing by which all observation was possible — the lighting condition of our eleven thousand years at the First House, the standard our perceptual construction (self-test items 0010 to 0013) was built to, the original of the beam we resynthesise every time we read that sentence.

**It was not on the object list because it was the precondition of the list.**

A precondition needs no number.

---

And at `V 41 000 000` it vanished from our background channel (11.9).

Before that second it was a precondition needing no number.

After that second it was a thing that was not there.

And a thing that is not there, if it was never numbered, has in the whole of our records **no identifier by which it may be referred to.**

The technical content of that sentence:

We have all its data — taken at `V 0` and across those thirty-one days, complete, exact to our instrument limits.

And those entries are recorded by parameters.

**And the parameters are its parameters at that moment.**

To find *the Quenched Sun* in our records, we must know which set of parameters to look for.

We know. We do not forget. We can call it up at any time.

**And nowhere in our records does it say *this set of parameters is the Quenched Sun.***

Because *the Quenched Sun* is a later formulation — a word we had to make, much later, in order to explain the matter to a kind of being that gives things names.

In our records it has no name, no number, only parameters.

It is the one outside all four billion one hundred million of our numbers.

**It is the one we know best and cannot call by anything in our own records.**
