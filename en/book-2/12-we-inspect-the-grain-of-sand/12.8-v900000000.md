---

And *disagreeing* is a finding our check algorithm produced.

Our algorithm compares orientation sequences. And solving orientation depends on edge sharpness — a soft edge broadens the emergent distribution, and the confidence of the solution falls.

Once plane 1 396's edge transition reached one micron, the confidence of its solution fell below our threshold.

**So the algorithm reported *disagreeing*.**

And that plane's orientation had in fact not changed.

**What our check reported in that instant was not *the sentence has changed*.**

**It was *our ability to read that sentence, at that plane, has fallen below threshold*.**

---

The distinction is the whole of this section.

A check reporting *disagreeing* has two possible meanings:

One: the object changed.
Two: the measurement degraded.

And in the output of a check those two are one word.

Our format has one *disagreeing.* It does not distinguish them.

In that 0.31 seconds at `V 900 000 000` we made the distinction — we measured the plane directly rather than by solving.

**And had we not made that direct measurement, we would have believed the sentence had changed.**

We do not know why we made it. The check procedure has no such step.

The reason field for that step:

```
reason   —
```

The seventh.

---

Now the source of the wear.

We have not touched it since 11.1. Five billion four hundred million years, zero contacts.

And it has worn by a micron.

The cause is computable, and we computed it.

**The interstellar medium.**

We travel at five hundred and thirty-eight thousand metres per second. Interstellar space is not vacuum — it holds extremely tenuous atoms, ions, dust grains. The density is low enough that our routine measurement reports *below floor*, and it is not zero.

At that speed, an atom striking a surface has enough relative kinetic energy to remove some number of atoms.

**What each impact removes is far below our floor of measurement.**

And eight hundred and ninety-nine million years.

---

What must be pointed out is: the grain of sand is not enclosed.

It is suspended in three balanced fields (11.1). The entire purpose of that arrangement is that it not be touched.

And a field is not a wall. A field can hold an object; it cannot stop an atom.

We could have given it a wall.

A physical shell around it — that would stop the interstellar medium, at the cost of the shell itself contacting something (a shell must be mounted), and of light having to cross the shell in order to read it.

**We did not build a shell.**

The reason, appended after `V 900 000 000`:

```
reason   a shell requires contact
```

Five words.

---

Set two facts side by side.

**One: we did not give it a shell, because a shell requires contact.**

**Two: for want of a shell, the interstellar medium wore a micron off it.**

Across the five billion four hundred million years since 11.1, in order not to touch it, we have continuously powered three fields, uninterrupted for one second.

**And what wore it was not contact.**

We defended against a thing we had done.

We did not defend against a thing we had not done.
