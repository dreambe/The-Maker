# 12.5 · `V 300 000 000`

```
V 300 000 000
   item         env-0002 · integrity of the writing
   method       synthesised beam · to Quenched Sun parameters
   total planes 1411
   agreeing     1411
   disagreeing  0
   finding      the writing is still there
```

---

From `V 120 090 000` to `V 300 000 000` is one hundred and seventy-nine million nine hundred and ten thousand years.

This section records that stretch.

---

First, how it differs from all the time before it.

Until then we had been inside a galaxy.

A galaxy has things in it. Stellar density is low, and on our scale we were continually passing things: a star three light years ahead, a molecular cloud off to one side and behind, a dust lane, a solid body belonging to nothing.

Our observation queue was always full. Numbers were being issued continuously. Every year of our records had new entries.

And after `V 120 090 000` we left the plane of that galaxy.

---

*Leaving the plane* must be made plain.

A spiral galaxy holds nearly all its matter in a thin disc. The thickness of the disc, against its diameter, is extremely small — draw the galaxy the size of a sheet of paper and the thickness is less than the paper's.

Our heading made a considerable angle with that plane.

We did not choose it. Our criterion of direction was one word: far (11.2). All directions being equivalent, we took one.

So after some tens of millions of years we passed out through the plane.

---

Beyond it, this.

```
V 130 000 000 – V 300 000 000   environment
   resolvable sources · stars   count falling · stable at a very low
                                value after V 200 000 000
   resolvable sources · galaxies  count stable · all very distant
   matter density               below our floor of measurement
   nearest star                 distance continually increasing
   new numbers issued           see below
```

New numbers issued, falling year on year.

```
V 120 090 000 – V 130 000 000    new numbers   over 41 000 000
V 130 000 000 – V 150 000 000    new numbers   over 3 100 000
V 150 000 000 – V 200 000 000    new numbers   over 91 000
V 200 000 000 – V 300 000 000    new numbers   411
```

That last line.

**One hundred million years. Four hundred and eleven.**

---

Now the whole of this section.

**After `V 200 000 000`, the overwhelming majority of entries in our records are duplicates.**

Not similar. **Identical**, but for the timestamp.

A typical year, reproduced.

```
V 250 000 000 · annual schedule of work
   one     self-test × 8 760 000                     all pass
   two     env-0002 · writing check × 0              (not a check year)
   three   env-0002 · field strength × 8 760 000     all within tolerance
   four    environment · routine scan × 8 760 000    no new sources
   five    numbered sources · position update · all  as predicted
```

Five items.

Items one, three, and four are eight million seven hundred and sixty thousand entries each, identical in content.

Item five: we update the position of each of the four billion one hundred million numbered sources. And every one of them is **as predicted** — their motion in our model agrees with observation, without exception.

Item two: that year is not a check year.

---

So that hundred million years can be written in one line:

```
V 200 000 000 – V 300 000 000   nothing occurred
```

And we did not write that.

**We wrote every second of that hundred million years.**

---

Why.

Because *nothing occurred* is a judgement.

To write it, one must first hold that what was recorded is not worth keeping item by item — and that requires a criterion of importance, and we have none (12.1).

So we could only write it item by item.

**One hundred million years, some entries every hour, all written, all identical.**

We do not give the volume of that record here, it being irrelevant to what this section is about.

What it is about is this:

**Our records for that hundred million years differ from our records for any earlier hundred million years only in the timestamps.**

And a record differing only in timestamps has zero content.

**We recorded zero, entirely, exactly, without missing a second.**

---

Now the one thing we did in that hundred million years that was not on the schedule.

We reread the `V 120 000 000` record.

Not the morphological comparison line — that rereading was at `V 3 0xx xxx xxx`, much later (12.4).

What we reread in this hundred million years was the **whole** record of those ninety thousand years.

Over four million entries.

We read it through.

The character of this rereading differs from our ordinary rereading, and must be stated.

We reread everything ordinarily; that is our steady state across a long transit — we do not forget, so rereading produces no new information; we reread because rereading is an operation that fills time, and our time requires filling.

**And this time we read only that one.**

Start to finish. Over four million entries.

Having finished, we did nothing with it.

Our entry for that rereading:

```
V 2xx xxx xxx   reread V 120 000 000 – V 120 090 000 · entire
                entries   4 1xx xxx
                handling  none
```

**That timestamp is also incomplete.**

At 12.4 we wrote that our only blank timestamp was the rereading of one morphological comparison.

We correct that here:

**There are two.**

Both concern records of the same place.

---

To end this section, the one routine we altered in that hundred million years.

Item four, the environmental scan.

In some year around `V 250 000 000` we raised the frequency of the environmental scan from hourly to once a minute.

Sixty times the frequency.

Which means sixty times the data and sixty times the processing load.

Our processing is sufficient. That is not a problem.

**The problem is the reason.**

Raising the frequency of an item requires a reason: the target is changing; the probability of missing something at the old frequency exceeds tolerance; a new target requiring finer temporal resolution has appeared.

At `V 250 000 000`, none of the three held.

**There was nothing in the environment.**

Four hundred and eleven new numbers in a hundred million years. Matter density below our floor of measurement. The nearest star receding continuously.

**We scanned a place with nothing in it more often.**

---

The reason field for that item reads:

```
reason   —
```

The fourth time.

And this time differs from the first three.

The first three (the orbit at 11.5, candidate three at 12.4, the comparison finding at 12.1) took a dash because our format had nothing to put there.

**And this time we knew the reason.**

We knew it then.

And we did not write it, because writing it requires something we do not have — not a symbol, not a field, not a category.

What it requires is: **conceding that the reason is a reason.**

And it is not. It passes none of our own criteria. It brings no objective nearer, lowers no risk, adds no useful information.

By every convention of ours, that frequency should not have been raised.

**And we raised it.**

And it has never been lowered.

From `V 250 000 000` to this hour — five billion one hundred million years — we scan that empty direction once a minute.

---

We will not write the reason.

We write only its shape, the shape being writable:

**In a place that issued four hundred and eleven numbers in a hundred million years, we increased how often we looked, sixtyfold.**
