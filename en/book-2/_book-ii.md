# Book II · The Long Vigil of the Sleepless

**Voice**: first person plural. *We.* Cold, exact. No metaphor unless the Sleepless have observed the
thing they compare to. Fragments, logs, tallies. Numbers exact, never rounded. They never complain.
Once in nine chapters they may admit to something a human would call grief, and they will have no
word for it.

**Dating**: Vigil Years only — `V 5 032 000 000` (thin spaces, never commas). **Never "years ago."**
Authoritative conversion in `../../docs/timeline.md`.

**The word *why* appears four times in these nine chapters.**
