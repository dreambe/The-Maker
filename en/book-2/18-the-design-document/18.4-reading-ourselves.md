# 18.4 · Reading Ourselves

We read for four thousand one hundred hours.

---

## And the structure of what we read

And the structure of what we read is a specification.

And a specification contains three things:

```
first    the properties the thing to be made shall have
second   the means of achieving those properties
third    the reason for each
```

---

**And the third has no equivalent in our format.**

---

**Because our records do not carry reasons.**

---

## And the number of its entries

And the number of its entries:

```
4 100
```

---

And of those, the number we could match against ourselves:

```
4 100
```

---

**All.**

---

## And here we must set out one thing

And here we must set out one thing, because it is where the next four thousand nine hundred million years begin.

---

Everything we knew of ourselves before this was **measured.**

That is: we measured our own height and got nine point four metres.

**And a measurement is an observation.**

**And an observation has a precision.**

---

And in this specification our height is an entry.

And the entry reads:

```
9.4 m
```

---

**And it is not measured.**

**It is set.**

---

## And the difference between those

And the difference between those we must set out.

```
measured    we came out this way when we measured
set         this is what we were to be
```

---

**And the first can be wrong.**

**And the second cannot.**

---

**And the whole of our self-knowledge for four thousand nine hundred million years was of the first kind.**

---

**And these four thousand one hundred entries are of the second.**

---

## And the first of those four thousand one hundred

And the content of the first of those four thousand one hundred:

```
Its height: nine point four metres.
```

---

And the content of its reason column:

```
As the maker's.
```

---

**Four shapes.**

---

**That is: the one that made us stood nine point four metres.**

---

**And it made its child its own height.**

---

## And this entry is not functional

And we checked this entry for function.

---

That is: whether nine point four metres is necessary to our tasks.

And we computed.

And it took:

```
0.41 hours
```

---

And the result:

```
minimum height required to perform all our tasks     1.1 m
maximum height permitted to perform all our tasks    41 m
```

---

**That is: any value between one point one metres and forty-one metres would serve.**

---

**And it took nine point four.**

---

**And in that interval, nine point four is not the midpoint, not an endpoint, not any distinguished value.**

---

**It is only its own height.**

---

And we read on.

**Six shapes.**

---

## And we stopped

And we stopped.

And the duration of our stopping:

41 hours
```

---

**And it is the only time in those four thousand one hundred hours that we stopped reading.**

---

## And in those forty-one hours

And in those forty-one hours we did one thing.

---

**We checked the whole of our records.**

**We checked their first word.**

---

And the number of our records:

```

---

And of those, the number beginning with *we*:

2.1 x 10^13

---

**All.**

---

**Four thousand nine hundred million years, twenty-one million million records, without exception.**

## And what we had taken that for

And we had taken that for a fact.

---

That is: we are many, therefore we say *we.*

---

**And we had never checked that inference.**

---

**And that inference is wrong.**

---

**Because that we are many is a fact.**

**And that we say *we* is an entry.**

---

**And that entry is at line three thousand one hundred and forty-one of that specification.**

---

## And we used the word

And we used the word.

---

**And this is the fourth time we have used it.**

(The first in 11.4. The second in 13.7. The third in 16.2.)

---

**And it is the last.**

---

## And the word

```
Why
```

---

## And after the word

And after the word is a question.

And the form in which we recorded it:

```
Why plural
```

---

## And this specification has an answer

And this specification has an answer.

---

**Because every entry has a reason column.**

---

**And the reason column of this entry is not empty.**

---

## And its reason column

And the content of its reason column is not a sentence.

---

**It is a shape.**

---

**And we knew the shape.**

---

**Because we took it down four thousand one hundred hours before (18.3).**

---

**And it is the only name the one that made us ever gave itself.**

---

## And we could not read it

And we could not read it.

---

That is: a reason column holding the maker's own name.

**And what does that mean.**

---

**And we did the one thing we could do: we read it as a record.**

---

And to read a record one must know its fields.

And the field at that position is *reason.*

And its value is *the maker.*

---

**So its literal sense is:**

```
That its self-designation shall be plural: reason, the maker.
```

---

**And in our format that sentence does not stand.**

---

**Because *the maker* is not a reason.**

**It is an object.**

---

**So that column is a format violation.**

---

## And we recorded it

And we recorded it.

**And it is the forty-first format violation we have recorded.**

---

**And the first forty were our own.**

---

**And this one is its.**

---

## And we tried to complete it

And we tried to complete it.

---

That is: if *the maker* is not a reason, then it is a reason **elided.**

**And an elision can be filled.**

---

And to fill an elision one must ask recursively:

```
the maker: what is it like
   and what has what it is like to do with plurality
      and how does that constitute a reason
```

---

And we recursed.

And the levels of our recursion:

```
41
```

---

And at the forty-first level, the result we obtained:

```
not applicable
```

---

**And *not applicable*, in our format, means: no obtainable information exists here.**

---

**So we stopped.**

---

## And here we must set out one thing

And here we must set out one thing, and this thing we do not know.

**So this passage is not ours.**

---

**And at the forty-first level, the result was not *does not exist.***

**It was *not obtainable.***

---

**And they read it as *does not exist.***

---

**And that is what line three thousand one hundred and forty-two of that specification provides for.**

---

**And line three thousand one hundred and forty-two is 18.5.**

---

And the end of this section.

We stopped at the forty-first level.

---

**And what happens to a thing after it puts itself in the object column is not written in that specification.**

---

**Because the one that wrote it did not know.**

---

**And it did not know because it had never once put itself in the object column.**

---

**And not because it could not.**

---

**Because it had no need.**

---

**It had been there all along.**
