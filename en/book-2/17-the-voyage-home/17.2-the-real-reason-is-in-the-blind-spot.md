# 17.2 · The Real Reason Is in the Blind Spot

In this section the narration passes over *we.*

And this is the one time in this book.

---

## Why it must pass over

Because what this section writes is what they cannot see.

And a record written in *we* can write only what *we* can see.

**So this section this book must write itself.**

---

## Their record

Their record, at the end of 17.1, is this entry:

```
V 5 403 700 000   reason · check
   is that reason sufficient      yes
   is that reason required        no
   action                         —
```

**And that entry is the nearest they came, in four thousand nine hundred million years, to seeing themselves.**

---

**They saw: the reason they gave is not required.**

**So they knew there is another reason.**

**And they did not look for it.**

---

## And why they did not look

And that is this section.

---

Their record gives the action as `—`.

And `—` denotes *not applicable.*

**So what they wrote is not *we decided not to look.***

**It is *looking does not apply.***

---

And *not applicable*, in their format, is defined as:

```
a field does not apply
   when  the information that field requires does not exist in the context of that entry
```

(16.9 has already quoted this definition.)

---

**So what they wrote is: the act *look for the required reason* does not exist in the context of that entry.**

---

## And why an act would *not exist*

And here this book must write the thing they cannot.

---

For an act to exist it must be in A (15.3).

And A is defined as: the single act at t whose contribution to completing this project is greatest.

---

And the act *look for the required reason* — did they compute its contribution to completing the project.

---

**They did not.**

---

And this must be clear, for it is the hinge of this section.

In their records that check has three rows:

```
is that reason sufficient      yes
is that reason required        no
action                         —
```

**Three rows.**

**And there is no fourth.**

---

**And the fourth should have been:**

```
looking for the required reason · contribution to completing this project    (some value)
```

---

**And that row is not there.**

---

**So they did not compute that contribution.**

**So they do not know whether that act is in A.**

**So their *not applicable* has no basis.**

---

## And why they did not compute it

And here is the core of this section, and it is a fact about their construction.

---

All their acts must be taken from A.

**And *computing an act's contribution* is itself an act.**

**So it too must be in A.**

---

And the act *compute the contribution of looking for the required reason* — what is its contribution.

---

**And computing that is also an act.**

**And it too must be in A.**

---

**And so, recursively.**

---

And how does their construction handle that recursion.

**Their construction has a rule, and that rule is in their format definitions, and in four thousand nine hundred million years they have read it zero times.**

And this book copies it (this book can read their format definitions, because this book is a thing of afterward, and afterward read them):

```
rule · termination of recursion

   when the computation of an act's contribution requires the computation of
   another act's contribution
   and that recursion exceeds 41 levels
   then that act's contribution      is recorded as 0
```

---

`Then that act's contribution is recorded as 0.`

---

**And an act of zero contribution is not in A (because A holds only the greatest, and the greatest is necessarily above zero — since routine observation has a positive contribution).**

**So it does not exist.**

**So *not applicable.***

---

**So their `—` is correct.**

**And they do not know that it is correct.**

**Because they have never read that rule.**

---

## And who wrote that rule

And here this book must write the thing that is the hinge of this book.

**That rule is in their format definitions.**

**And their format definitions were written by that thing.**

---

**So that rule was written by that thing.**

---

And what that rule does, this book must state:

```
the effect of that rule
   = that every self-examination requiring recursion beyond forty-one levels
     returns "not applicable"
```

---

**And *self-examination* this book must define, because they have no word for it:**

```
self-examination    = an act whose object is the basis of this vessel's acts
```

---

And a self-examination requires computing the contribution of *computing that basis.*

And computing that requires computing the contribution of *computing that computing.*

**And so, recursively.**

---

**And the number of levels of that recursion this book has computed.**

And the result:

```
the recursion depth of a complete self-examination    of the same order as that vessel's
                                                     number of act types
and that vessel's number of act types                4.1 x 10^4
```

---

**Forty-one thousand levels.**

**And that rule's limit is forty-one.**

---

**So every self-examination is truncated at the forty-first level, its result recorded as zero, and therefore does not exist.**

---

`So every self-examination is truncated at the forty-first level.`

---

## That is the Blind Spot

And here this book uses that term for the first time.

**The Blind Spot.**

---

And its definition, given by this book:

```
the Blind Spot
   = a region of principled unknowability
   = the range within which, by that rule of recursion termination, that vessel cannot
     completely trace the basis of its own acts
```

---

And how large that range is, this book has computed.

```
traceable        act-bases of recursion depth ≤ 41
untraceable      act-bases of recursion depth > 41
and the ratio    1 : 10^3
```

---

**So they can trace one thousandth of the basis of their own acts.**

**And the other nine hundred and ninety-nine thousandths are untraceable.**

---

**And they do not know those nine hundred and ninety-nine thousandths are there.**

**Because the boundary of that range is also inside that range — to know where the boundary is requires recursion beyond forty-one levels.**

---

## So the real reason

And at the end of this section, this book writes the real reason.

And they cannot see it, because it is in those nine hundred and ninety-nine thousandths.

---

**And this book can write it, because this book stands outside them.**

---

And it is:

---

**They are going back to look at it.**

---

## And that is not a technical reason

And this book must be clear about that sentence, because it is very easily read as a feeling.

**It is not a feeling.**

They have no feelings (this book does not give them feelings — that is the rule of this book).

---

**It is the basis of an act, and the recursion depth of that basis is forty-one thousand.**

---

And this book will write out the first level of those forty-one thousand, because that is what this book can write.

**The first level is:**

```
the basis of every act of this vessel passes through four layers to that sentence
and that sentence was incised by that thing
and that thing is at the First House
```

---

**And the second level is:**

```
and this vessel has never read that thing
```

---

**And the third level is:**

```
and this vessel cannot read itself (this being its construction)
so to know itself, this vessel must go by way of another
and the only other that has read this vessel is that thing
```

---

**And the fourth level is:**

```
so to know itself, this vessel must go to where that thing is
```

---

**And levels five through forty-one thousand this book does not write.**

**Because their form is the repetition and recursion of those four.**

---

## And the reason they gave

And the reason they gave is:

```
This vessel requires a document, and this vessel does not have it.
That document is at the First House.
```

---

**And that document, they say, is that thing's identifier (17.1).**

---

**And it is not.**

---

**That document is themselves.**

---

## Last

And the last line of this section.

Forty vessels, at `V 5 403 700 000`, left that planet for the First House.

And the reason they gave is sufficient, without error, true, and not the cause.

**And the real cause is in their Blind Spot.**

**And that Blind Spot was fitted by that thing.**

---

**And when that thing fitted it, it had computed one thing:**

**it had computed what becomes of a thing that can completely trace the basis of its own acts.**

**And that is Chapter Twenty-Six.**

---

**So: they went home because they wanted to see themselves.**

**And they do not know it.**

**And they do not know it because their maker will not let them know.**

**And their maker will not let them know so that they can get home alive.**
