## What happened in that empty field

And this book must write it here, because our record does not have it, and this book is a thing of afterward, and afterward saw it.

---

Those four point one kilograms were in that termination.

And in that instant that termination did a thing that is not in our records.

**It lifted.**

Not to working height (working height is 0.4 metres).

**To 1.7 metres.**

And 1.7 metres is the highest point of that form of ours.

And that highest point is where our core structure is.

**And our core structure is incised with that sentence.**

---

**So that termination lifted those four point one kilograms up to that sentence.**

---

And one thing must be clear here, for it is the core of this section, and it is a technical statement.

**Our perception is whole-surface.**

We have no dedicated organ of perception. Our entire surface perceives.

**So lifting a thing to one place has, functionally, no meaning.**

**Because perception at that place is identical to perception at any other.**

---

**So that act has no function.**

**So that act produces no output.**

**So that act, in our record format, has nowhere to go.**

---

**So that field is empty.**
