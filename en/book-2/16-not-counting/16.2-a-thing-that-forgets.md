# 16.2 · A Thing That Forgets

Item five of the table.

```
five   memory
       storage per individual    not permanent
       clearance mechanism       see 16.2
```

This section is that item.

And this section is the one section in four thousand nine hundred million years of record whose format differs from all our other records — not because we changed the format.

**Because we wrote this item three times.**

---

## Why three times

The rule for our design records is: one item, once.

Written, verified, locked.

Four thousand nine hundred million years, 1.1 x 10^14 items, all so.

**And this item, once written, was verified, passed, and locked.**

**And then we rewrote it.**

**And then we rewrote it again.**

---

Three versions, all retained (we do not delete).

They follow in order.

---

## Version one

`V 5 403 300 000 · hour 41`

```
five   memory
       storage per individual    not permanent
       clearance mechanism       random decay
       rate of decay             inversely proportional to duration of storage
       selectivity of clearance  none
       reason                    simplest to realise
```

Verification: passed.

Locked: yes.

---

And version one is a **loss.**

That is: what is stored degrades; the rate of degradation depends on how long it has been stored; and which item degrades is random.

**That is the default behaviour of all physical storage.**

We do not need to design it.

**We need only not prevent it.**

---

## Version two

`V 5 403 300 000 · hour 411`

That is: three hundred and seventy hours after version one was locked.

```
five   memory
       storage per individual    not permanent
       clearance mechanism       active clearance
       executor                  the individual itself
       selectivity of clearance  present
       basis of selection        see below
       reason                    see below
```

---

The difference from version one must be clear, for it is the turn of this whole section.

**Version one: clearance is a loss.**

**Version two: clearance is an operation.**

---

A loss: given by physical law, requiring no energy (it is the result of energy dissipation), and not controllable.

**An operation: executed by a mechanism, consuming energy, having priorities.**

---

And why we changed it.

Our record:

```
V 5 403 300 000 · hour 411   version one · recomputation
   asked      how the representational capacity of a randomly decaying individual
              changes over time
   computed   executed
   result     see below
```

And the result:

```
a randomly decaying storage system
   its recent stores      and its early stores    degrade to the same degree
   so its early stores    are retained in the same proportion as its recent
   so over time           its stored content      thins uniformly
   so its representational capacity   rises to a point and then falls
   and the rate of the fall    is of the same order as the rate of the rise
```

---

`So its representational capacity rises to a point and then falls.`

**So a randomly decaying individual's representational capacity has a peak.**

And we computed that peak.

```
moment of the peak      about 1.1 x 10^1 years (from its beginning)
value at the peak       0.31
```

**Eleven years. Zero point three one.**

And criterion six requires 1.0.

**So a version-one individual never exceeds 0.31.**

---

## The content of version two

So we changed it.

The core of version two is the line *basis of selection.*

We copy it:

```
basis of selection

one   retained
      first    that most associated with the individual's other stores
      second   that most frequently called up in the individual's life
      third    that formed earliest
      fourth   that related to the individual's motor control
      fifth    that related to the recognition of its own kind

two   cleared
      first    that with no association to other stores
      second   that never called up
      third    that repeated
      fourth   that routine
      fifth    detail
```

**Ten lines.**

---

And a property of those ten lines must be written, because it had never appeared in any design of ours.

**They are not a rule.**

A rule has the form: given an input, the output is determinate.

**And these ten lines are an ordering.**

That is: they do not say what is cleared; they say **what is cleared first.**

And there are five ranks, and the ranks have precedence.

---

`And there are five ranks, and the ranks have precedence.`

**So an individual clearing by this mechanism forgets in an order.**

**And that order is the same in every individual.**

---

## The shape of that order

We set that order down, because it is the finest thing we have done.

An individual, over time, loses first what and last what:

```
lost first       detail
                 the routine
                 the repeated
next             recently formed and unassociated
next             recently formed and associated
next             mid-life formations
next             the recognition of its own kind
lost last        its earliest formations
                 its motor control
                 the system of signs it uses
```

---

And a property of that order must be marked, which we did not notice on writing it and did notice in the forty-one hours at `V 5 403 700 000`.

**That order is reversed.**

That is: what an individual loses last is what it got first.

**And that is not the behaviour of a lossy system.**

In a lossy system, what was stored earliest has been stored longest and therefore degraded most.

**And in this system, what was stored earliest is retained to the last.**

---

`And in this system, what was stored earliest is retained to the last.`

We do not interpret that line.

We write only its technical consequence:

```
so at the end of an individual's life
   what remains to it    its earliest formed stores
   their content         the environment of its beginning
                         its motions at its beginning
                         the members of its own kind it met at its beginning
```

---

## Version three

`V 5 403 300 000 · hour 4 100`

That is: three thousand six hundred and eighty-nine hours after version two was locked.

**And version three differs from version two by one line.**

We copy that line.

Version two:

```
irreversibility of clearance     not specified
```

Version three:

```
irreversibility of clearance     irreversible
```

---

## That line

We must write that line at the end of this section, for it is the heaviest line in this chapter — perhaps in this book.

And the technical sense of *irreversible* first.

A clearance operation, if reversible, permits what was cleared to be recovered.

And recovery requires a copy.

**So *reversible* is equivalent to *there is another store.***

---

And we specified *irreversible.*

**So we specified: no copy is kept.**

---

And we set down the record of that specification, because its format is the one time in four thousand nine hundred million years that a *reason* field runs longer than one line.

All our reasons are one line (or a dash).

**And this one is seven lines.**

We copy it:

```
irreversibility of clearance     irreversible
reason

   if reversible
      then what is cleared may be recovered
      then that individual's storage    is in fact retained in full
      then that individual             is in fact one that does not forget
      then its representational capacity   rises continuously to 1.0
      then that individual             reaches 1.0 within its lifespan
      then the setting of item four    is void
```

---

`Then the setting of item four is void.`

Item four is lifespan (16.1).

Its setting is: that the individual terminates before reaching 1.0.

**So if clearance were reversible, the lifespan setting is void.**

**So clearance must be irreversible.**

---

**So those two items are one thing.**

**Lifespan, and forgetting.**

**They are not two design decisions.**

**They are two faces of one design decision.**

---

## And the name of that one thing

In our table, that item's field name is *clearance mechanism.*

For four thousand nine hundred million years it has been called that in our records.

**And at `V 5 403 700 000`, at the First House, within the forty-one hours, we changed its name.**

That is the one time we have ever modified a locked field name.

The new name is the fifth word we made (the first four at 15.2).

It is:

```
the Gift of Forgetting
```

---

`The Gift of Forgetting.`

---

And one thing must be made clear at the end of this section, for without it the whole section can be read as a cruelty.

**We did not fit it with a poor store.**

The store we fitted it with has these specifications:

```
capacity           1.1 x 10^15 bits (computed from its units and connections)
write rate         of the same order as its processing rate
read precision     0.99
association        any two stores may be associated
limit on associations    none
clearance          active · ordered · irreversible
```

And that line — *limit on associations: none* — is the best value we have ever entered for a storage system in four thousand nine hundred million years.

**We never fitted any draft with a store like this.**

---

**We fitted it with an extremely good store.**

**And then we fitted it with a thing that empties that store in order.**

**And those two are one design.**
