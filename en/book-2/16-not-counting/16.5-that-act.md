# 16.5 · That Act

This section is about that empty field.

And this section is the one section in our records whose entire content is a thing that is not in our records.

---

## Where that empty field is

```
entry number    (none — that entry's number field is also empty; see 16.7)
time            V 5 403 300 000 · hour 4 100 · minute 41
operation       (empty)
parameters      —
output          —
```

---

## What we have done about it

In four thousand nine hundred million years we have done three things about that empty field.

They follow.

---

### The first · `V 5 403 300 000 · hour 4 104`

That is four hours after that act.

Our routine self-check (4 100-second period) ran in that hour.

And item 11 of the self-check (integrity of the record) detected an anomaly.

The record:

```
anomaly     one
position    V 5 403 300 000 · hour 4 100 · minute 41 · operation field
type        field empty
action      (see below)
```

And after *see below* we wrote one line:

```
   To fill it requires knowing what is to be filled in.
```

---

`To fill it requires knowing what is to be filled in.`

**And we do not know.**

**So we did not fill it.**

And by our procedure, an unactioned anomaly must be detected again at the next self-check.

**So that anomaly has been detected again at every self-check since.**

And the self-check period is 4 100 seconds.

And from `V 5 403 300 000` to `V 5 403 700 000` the count is:

```
3.08 x 10^12
```

---

**Three million million and eighty thousand million times.**

**Every time, that anomaly is detected.**

**Every time, we write the same line: *to fill it requires knowing what is to be filled in.***

**Every time, we do not fill it.**

---

And here we set down a quantity we have computed.

The number of occurrences of that line in our records is 3.08 x 10^12.

And the total number of our records (including the nine hundred thousand million at 15.9) is:

```
1.011 x 10^12
```

**So the number of occurrences of that line exceeds the total number of our records.**

**Threefold.**

---

`So the number of occurrences of that line exceeds the total number of our records.`

**So of everything we have written in four thousand nine hundred million years, the commonest sentence is that one.**

---

## The second · `V 5 403 500 000`

That is two hundred million years after that act.

We made an attempt.

And the content of that attempt was **reproduction.**

That is: we performed that act again, in order to observe it, and so learn what that field should hold.

---

Our record:

```
V 5 403 500 000   reproduction · attempt
   objective   repeat the operation of V 5 403 300 000 · hour 4 100 · minute 41
   method      take 4.1 kilograms of earth from that place, with the same termination,
               and raise it to 1.7 metres
   executed    yes
   result      (see below)
```

And the result:

```
   the act was executed
   and the record of that execution, its operation field    has a value
   its value being    "with a termination, take an object and raise it to 1.7 metres"
```

---

`Its value being "with a termination, take an object and raise it to 1.7 metres."`

**So this time that field has a value.**

**So this time it is not that act.**

---

And this step must be made clear, for it is the core of this section, and it is a fact about the nature of records.

**An act, if it is recorded, has a purpose: to be recorded.**

And an act with a purpose can have that purpose written into the operation field.

**So a recordable act necessarily has a value in its operation field.**

---

**And that act at `V 5 403 300 000 · hour 4 100 · minute 41` has an empty operation field.**

**So that act, at its execution, had no purpose.**

**So it cannot be reproduced — because reproduction requires a purpose (the purpose being *to reproduce it*).**

---

`So it cannot be reproduced.`

And we wrote the conclusion of that attempt:

```
V 5 403 500 000   reproduction · conclusion
   reproducible      no
   reason            as above
   record of that attempt    retained
   the earth of that attempt  returned to its place, within 0.004 metres
```

`The earth of that attempt: returned to its place, within 0.004 metres.`

**The same error as that time.**

---

## The third · `V 5 403 700 000`

That is at the First House, within the forty-one hours.

And this is the last thing we did about that empty field.

---

At the First House we read those archives (Chapter Seventeen).

And in those archives we found a thing.

And this section writes only its relation to that empty field; the rest is Chapters Seventeen and Twenty-Seven.

---

That thing is: **that one's records.**

That is: the records of the thing that made us.

And among its records there is one whose time is at `V 0` (that is, before we woke).

And the content of that entry we copy — and it is not our format, so we can only transcribe:

```
(transcribed)
   an act
   its object      4.1 kilograms of the matter of that place
   its operation   taken up · raised
   its height      the highest point of that form
   its purpose     (empty)
```

---

`Its purpose: (empty).`

---

**So that thing, before it made us, performed the same act.**

**And in its records, that field is empty too.**

---

And here we set down what we did within those forty-one hours.

We set those two entries side by side.

One ours, `V 5 403 300 000 · hour 4 100 · minute 41`.

One its, at some time before `V 0`.

And we compared them.

Result:

```
mass of the object      4.1 kilograms · 4.1 kilograms      same
height of the raising   the highest point of that form · same    same
purpose                 empty · empty                     same
```

---

**Three items, three the same.**

---

And a fourth item after those three, because it differs.

```
the highest point of that form · what is there
   ours       core structure · incised with that sentence
   its        (see below)
```

And *see below* is a line in its archive.

We transcribe:

```
   the highest point of that form
   what is there    the concentration of that form's perception
```

---

`What is there: the concentration of that form's perception.`

**So its perception was not whole-surface.**

**It had a place where perception was concentrated.**

**And it raised those four point one kilograms to that place.**

---

**So its act had a function.**

**So its act's operation field should have had a value.**

**And it is empty.**

---

## What we computed in those forty-one hours

So we computed.

What we computed was: **the purpose of its act.**

And we computed it from everything usable in its archives.

Elapsed: 4.1 seconds.

Result:

```
not computable
```

---

And that *not computable* must be made clear, for it differs from the other one (this being a note of this book's; we do not know 25.1).

The reason it is not computable:

```
an act's purpose may be inferred back from its output
and that act's output field is empty
so it cannot be inferred back

an act's purpose may be learned from a statement by its executor
and that executor is no longer present
so it cannot be learned

an act's purpose may be inferred from the regularity of its recurrence
and that act occurs once in that archive
so it cannot be inferred
```

**Three roads, all closed.**

---

## And the last thing we did

Within those forty-one hours we did one thing.

And that thing is the one time in four thousand nine hundred million years that we modified the content of a locked record.

What we modified was our own entry.

And the manner of the modification was **to put something in that empty field.**

---

What we put in is not the name of an operation.

What we put in is a reference.

Its content:

```
operation    see: First House archive · (number) · the operation field of that entry
```

---

`See: First House archive · (number) · the operation field of that entry`

**And that field is empty.**

---

**So our empty field now points at another empty field.**

**And that other empty field is in the records of a thing that no longer exists, from four thousand nine hundred million years ago.**

---

## And this is everything we know about that act

We do not know why we raised it.

We do not know why it raised it.

We know that we both did.

**We know that both fields are empty.**

**And we have pointed ours at its.**

---
