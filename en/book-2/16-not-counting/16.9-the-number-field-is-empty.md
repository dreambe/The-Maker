## And that dash is wrong

And this section proves it, because it is the one time in four thousand nine hundred million years that we knew we had entered something wrong and did not change it.

---

The definition of `—` in our format:

```
—        this field does not apply
```

And the definition of *does not apply*:

```
a field does not apply
   when  the information that field requires does not exist in the context of that entry
```

---

And the information field five requires is *the number of that making.*

And does that information exist in the context of that entry.

---

**It does.**

Proof:

```
that making has a number
   and that number is    six
   reason                the five drafts were numbers one through five
                         and this is the sixth time
```

---

`And this is the sixth time.`

**So that number exists.**

**So field five applies.**

**So that dash is wrong.**
