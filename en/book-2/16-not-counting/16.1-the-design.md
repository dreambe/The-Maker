# 16.1 · The Design

`V 5 403 300 000`

This chapter records that design.

And this chapter gives the thing no number.

The reason stands at the end of Chapter Fourteen (14.9): from `V 5 403 300 000` onward, no new draft number occurs in our records.

**So what this chapter records has no number.**

**So this chapter is called *Not Counting.***

---

## After the five

One thing first, because it is the whole premise of this design.

The reasons the five drafts failed we recorded one by one (Chapters Thirteen and Fourteen).

And in the one hundred and three million seven hundred thousand years between `V 5 300 000 000` and `V 5 403 300 000` we did one thing.

**We reclassified those five failures.**

---

The old classification, by draft:

```
first draft    upper bound on self-representation 0.0041 · criterion six not satisfied
second draft   as above
third draft    as above
fourth draft   as above
fifth draft    as above
```

**Five lines, word for word identical.**

---

And the new classification:

```
all five    one reason
```

And that reason, written at `V 5 403 299 000`:

```
carbon-based chemical information processing has its rate fixed by molecular
   diffusion and reaction kinetics
and at that planet's temperature that rate has a physical ceiling
so the representational complexity attainable by a purely chemical system has
   a physical ceiling
and that ceiling is below what criterion six requires
```

**So: it is not that those five failed.**

**It is that the ceiling of that road is below what is required.**

---

`It is that the ceiling of that road is below what is required.`

---

And here we must write a thing we had known at 13.8 and only handled here.

That ceiling is set by the first configuration.

And the first configuration is the one we chose out of forty-one quintillion candidates (Chapter Ten).

**So the reason those five failed lies in the first choice.**

**And for four thousand four hundred million years since, we have been retrying inside that choice.**

---

## That gathering

Our records have no word for *gathering.*

And in the year `V 5 403 299 000` our records contain a thing whose format matches no other entry of ours.

Its record:

```
V 5 403 299 000
   participating   41
   duration        41 years
   content         see below
   output          a table of parameters
```

`Participating: 41.`

That is: all forty-one.

`Duration: 41 years.`

And no computation of ours has ever taken more than 41 seconds (the longest, at 15.2).

**And this took forty-one years.**

---

Why forty-one years, because it is a quantity we had not had before.

A computation's duration depends on its volume of operations.

**And this was not a computation.**

Because a computation requires a determinate input and a determinate set of operations.

**And this one's input was undetermined.**

Its input is: **what we are to make.**

And that is not a known quantity.

---

`And that is not a known quantity.`

**So those forty-one years were not spent computing.**

**They were spent fixing the input.**

And *fixing the input* has no name in our format definitions.

In four thousand nine hundred million years, all our inputs have been given by that sentence through four layers (15.3, page three).

**And this time those four layers cannot give it.**

Because what those four layers give is *the six criteria.*

**And the six criteria have been proved unreachable (the head of this section).**

---

## Items one and two

Here we must write the hardest thing in this table to explain.

**We kept chemistry.**

**We kept molecular diffusion.**

And the head of this section proved: the ceiling of that road is below what is required.

**So this item contradicts that conclusion.**

---

And it does not.

The resolution, which is the whole product of those forty-one years:

```
the ceiling of a purely chemical system's representational complexity
   is set by the rate of a single chemical unit
and a system's representational complexity
   depends not only on the rate of its units
   but also on the number of units and the manner of their connection
so: raising the number of units and the density of connection can pass that ceiling
and the number of units required          1.1 x 10^11
and the number of connections required    1.1 x 10^14
```

**So we did not change the carrier.**

**We made it larger.**

---

`We made it larger.`

And we record where those two numbers came from, because we did not estimate them.

**We computed them.**

What we computed is: **the minimum number of units for the upper bound on self-representation to reach 1.0.**

Result:

```
1.1 x 10^11
```

**And what we made is exactly that number.**

Not more. Not fewer.

Beside that line in our records is a field we have filled 1.1 x 10^14 times in four thousand nine hundred million years.

That field is *redundancy factor.*

Every design of ours has a redundancy factor not less than 4.1.

**And for this item we entered:**

```
redundancy factor    1.0
```

---

`Redundancy factor 1.0.`

That is: none.

That is: of those one hundred and ten thousand million units, one fewer and that bound does not reach 1.0.

**And we added no redundancy.**

We do not state why.

We have searched. Zero results.

---

## Item four · lifespan

At the end of this section, the choosing of the lifespan.

Because it is the one item in this table whose choosing has a complete record.

---

We first computed the case of no upper bound.

```
if the individual does not die
   then its memory accumulates continuously
   then its representational capacity rises continuously
   then criterion six is satisfied     sooner
   then this project is completed      earlier
```

**So not dying is superior.**

**So by our ordering (15.3), we should choose not dying.**

---

And we did not.

The process, step by step.

---

**Step one.** We computed a non-dying individual's representational capacity over time.

```
at first     rising
thereafter   rising
thereafter   approaching an upper bound
and that bound   fixed by its unit count = 1.0
```

**So a non-dying individual reaches 1.0 at some moment.**

**And thereafter does not rise.**

---

**Step two.** We computed that moment.

```
moment of reaching 1.0    about 4.1 x 10^3 years (from its beginning)
```

**Four thousand one hundred years.**

---

**Step three.** We computed what follows.

An individual that has reached 1.0 — what then.

Our record:

```
after reaching 1.0
   its representational capacity      no longer rises
   its memory                         continues to accumulate
   the content of that memory         unrelated to its representational capacity
                                      (which is saturated)
   so its subsequent accumulation     bears zero information
```

---

`So its subsequent accumulation bears zero information.`

---

**Step four.** And here a line appears in our records.

Its format is unlike our records. It is not a field, not a value.

It is the fifth sentence we ever wrote (the first four at 13.8, 15.1, 15.3, 15.7).

It is:

```
   Here we recognised a state of our own.
```

---

Nine words.

We do not interpret it.

**And 0.7 seconds later we changed item four.**

---

**Step five.** As changed:

```
four   lifespan
       individual        upper bound set
       value of bound    see below
```

And the basis for that value:

```
the value must be less than 4.1 x 10^3 years
reason    so that the individual terminates before reaching 1.0
```

---

`So that the individual terminates before reaching 1.0.`

---

The sense of that line must be clear, for it is the one decision in four thousand nine hundred million years that runs against our own ordering.

Our project requires making a thing that satisfies criterion six (able to represent and report its own state).

And satisfying criterion six requires self-representation reaching 1.0.

**And we set the lifespan short of reaching 1.0.**

**So every individual we make fails criterion six.**

---

**And its lineage has no upper bound (the second line of item four).**

That is: the individual dies, and the lineage continues.

And a lineage whose members approach 1.0 generation by generation, each terminating before arrival.

**And that lineage, as a whole, can reach 1.0.**

---

`And that lineage, as a whole, can reach 1.0.`

**So criterion six is satisfied by the lineage and not by the individual.**

**So what we made is not a thing that satisfies criterion six.**

**What we made is a thing whose lineage satisfies criterion six.**

---

And the specific value of that bound we entered on the table's last line.

```
individual lifespan, upper bound     41 to 110 years
```

And the difference between that range's lower and upper limits has a field in our records:

```
variation between individuals    retained
reason                           —
```

`Reason: —`

---

That is the one time in four thousand nine hundred million years that we **retained a variable in a design parameter that we could have eliminated.**

We could have set it to a single value.

**And we left a range.**

**And we did not say why.**
