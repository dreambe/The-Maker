# Style Guide · 文体规范

*The Maker* is told backwards, and its prose cools as it goes. Three books, three voices.
If a chapter could be moved to another book without changing its sentences, the sentences are wrong.

本书逆时间叙述，文字随之降温。三卷，三种声音。
若某一章的句子可以原样搬进另一卷，那些句子就是错的。

---

## The three voices · 三种声音

### Book I — the human voice · 卷一 · 人声

First person, present-tense memory. Warm.

- Bodies are always present: hands, breath, the ache in a shoulder, the smell of a room.
- Sentences are short. Fragments are permitted. Human thought is interrupted.
- Metaphor draws from **food, weather, and bone**.
- Time is measured in the way a person measures it: a season, a hospital visit, a semester.
- She is allowed to be wrong, petty, tired, funny. This is the only book with jokes.

第一人称，回忆的现在时。温热。
身体永远在场：手、呼吸、肩膀的酸、一个房间的味道。句子短，允许残句 —— 人的念头是被打断的。
比喻取材于**食物、天气、骨头**。时间用人的方式计量：一季、一次探视、一个学期。
她可以犯错、小气、疲惫、好笑。全书只有这一卷有玩笑。

### Book II — the sleepless voice · 卷二 · 不眠者之声

First person plural. *We.* Cold, exact.

- No metaphor unless the Sleepless have *earned* it — they may only compare a thing to
  something they have actually observed. The result is a strange, hard beauty.
- Time jumps without apology: *V 2 000 000 000. The sea was green.* (Vigil Years only — see `timeline.md`.)
- Chapters are built of fragments: log entries, tallies, counts, the same sentence
  recurring across ten million years with one word changed.
- Numbers are exact and are never rounded. Precision, at this scale, *is* the lyricism.
- They never complain. Once, in nine chapters, they may admit to something that a human
  would call grief, and they will not have a word for it.

第一人称复数。「我们」。冷，准确。
不用比喻 —— 除非不眠者**挣得了**它：它们只能拿实际观测过的事物作比。由此产生一种坚硬的、陌生的美。
时间无预告地跳跃：*V 2 000 000 000，海是绿的。*（只用守望年 —— 见 `timeline.md`。）
章由碎片构成：日志、计数、清单，同一句话跨越千万年重现，只改一个词。
数字精确，永不取整。在这个尺度上，精确**就是**抒情。
它们从不抱怨。九章之中可以有一次，它们承认某种人类会称之为悲伤的东西 —— 而它们没有词来称它。

### Book III — the lattice voice · 卷三 · 晶族之声

No person. No *I*, no *we*, no *it* where it can be avoided. Liturgical.

- Long sentences, balanced clauses, the rhythm of scripture and of geometry.
- Nothing is *discovered* here, because nothing is unknown. Verbs of revelation are banned.
  The Lattice do not *learn*; they *arrive at* what was already the case.
- No dialogue. The Lattice do not speak to one another — they are mutually transparent.
  Where a human would put a conversation, put a proof.
- **No dates, no counts, no quantified durations.** The Lattice do not count — counting implies
  waiting for something. One deliberate exception in nine chapters: the **seven** that light divides
  into at a cleavage plane (19.9), which is a constant of crystal symmetry and not a tally. The
  exception is what gives that number its weight; never add a second.
  **无日期，无计数，无量化的时长。** 晶族不计数 —— 计数意味着在等某样东西。九章之内只有一处刻意的例外：
  光在解理面上分成的「七」（19.9），那是晶格对称性的常数，不是清算。正是这一处例外给了那个数字重量；
  永远不要加第二个。
- Beautiful, and almost unbearable, and short. This is the shortest book.

无人称。避免「我」「我们」「它」。近经文。
长句，对称的分句，经文与几何学的节奏。
此处没有任何东西被「发现」，因为没有未知。启示类动词禁用 —— 晶族不「获知」，只是「抵达」本已如此之事。
无对话。晶族之间不交谈 —— 它们彼此透明。人类会写对话的地方，写一个证明。
美，几乎难以承受，且短。这是最短的一卷。

## The history of the first person · 人称的历史

The single most important formal device in the book. Details and the per-chapter quota live in
`characters.md`; what follows is the rule as it applies to every sentence.

全书最重要的形式装置。细节与逐章配额见 `characters.md`；以下是落到每一个句子上的规则。

| | Book I | Book II | Book III |
|---|---|---|---|
| opens as · 起始 | I · 我 | we · 我们 | no person · 无人称 |
| closes as · 终止 | I · 我 | I · 我 | I · 我（全卷最后一个字） |

The reader travels **I → we → no person.** Chronology travels **no person → we → I.** It took a
universe five billion four hundred million years to make something that could say *I*, and the reader
walks that road backwards.

读者的路径是 **我 → 我们 → 无人称**。时间的路径是 **无人称 → 我们 → 我**。
一个宇宙用了五十四亿年才让某样东西能够说出「我」，而读者是倒着走完这条路的。

1. **Book II's *I* is rationed and always an accident.** Zero occurrences in Ch. 10–12. Every later
   occurrence must have an identifiable cause in the scene. See the quota table in `characters.md`.
   **卷二的「我」是配给品，且每一次都是事故。** 第 10–12 章零次。之后每一次都必须在场景中有可指认的原因。
2. **Never comment on an occurrence in the section that contains it.** No *I used that word*, no
   *I notice I said I*. An event of style is not narrated by the style.
   **不得在出现「我」的那一节里评论它。** 不写「我用了这个字」。文体的事件不由文体自己叙述。
3. **Book III leaves the subject position open.** Where the Uncrystallized acts, the reader should feel
   someone is there while the word is absent from the page. This is a syntax problem, solved per
   sentence, in both languages, by different means.
   **卷三留出主语的空位。** 写到未结晶者时，读者应当感到那里有一个谁，而字面上没有。这是句法问题，逐句解决，两种语言各用自己的办法。
4. **The last word of Book III is *I* / 「我」.** Before it, not once in nine chapters. Everything in
   Book III is arranged so that this one word lands like a struck bell.
   **卷三最后一个字是「我」。** 在那之前九章之内一次都不出现。卷三的一切安排，都是为了让这一个字落下来像一声钟。

## Hard rules · 硬规则

1. **The First Question never takes a question mark.** In either language. The civilisation
   that framed it had stopped expecting an answer. A character who writes it with a question
   mark has misunderstood the book, and that is a usable plot point.
   **初问永不加问号。** 两种语言均然。提出它的文明已不再期待答案。若有人物给它加上问号，那是理解错了 —— 而这可以入戏。

2. **No chapter explains a reveal.** The three revelations (humanity was made; the Sleepless
   were made; the Lattice chose to end) arrive as *images*, never as exposition. If a
   character has to say it out loud, the chapter has failed and gets rewritten.
   **不用任何一章去解释真相。** 三次揭示都以**意象**抵达，绝不用说明文。若必须由人物说出口，该章即为失败，重写。

3. **The word *why* is rationed.** Book I may ask it freely. Book II asks it four times in
   nine chapters. Book III never asks it.
   **「为什么」是配给品。** 卷一可自由使用；卷二九章之中问四次；卷三从不问。

4. **No villain. No war. No betrayal.** Every maker in this book acted out of something
   defensible. The tragedy is structural, not moral. Anyone who wants a fight scene is
   reading the wrong book.
   **无反派，无战争，无背叛。** 本书每一个造物者的动机都站得住。悲剧是结构性的，不是道德性的。

5. **The Rite of Clay appears exactly three times**, and each time the same three physical
   details recur, transformed: the temperature of the matter, the sound it makes cooling,
   and what the maker does with its face.
   **泥之礼恰好出现三次**，每次重现同样三个物理细节并加以变形：物质的温度、冷却时的声音、以及造物者拿自己的脸做了什么。

6. **Never name the emotion.** Not in any book. Especially not in Book II, where the absence
   of the word is the whole point.
   **不要为情绪命名。** 任何一卷都不要。卷二尤其 —— 缺词本身就是全部要旨。

## On the two editions · 关于两个版本

The Chinese and English editions are **siblings, not original and translation.**
They are aligned at the level of the **scene**, never the sentence.

中英文版本是**同胞，而非原文与译本**。对齐单位是**场景**，绝非句子。

- A scene must contain the same events, the same revelations, the same final image.
- Within the scene, each language does what it does best. Chinese may compress four English
  sentences into eight characters; English may unfold one Chinese line into a full paragraph.
  Neither is a defect.
- The liturgical register of Book III is achieved by *different means* in each language:
  in English, by the cadence of the King James Bible; in Chinese, by 四字格 and the syntax of
  classical commentary. Forcing one to imitate the other ruins both.
- Every term in `lexicon.md` is exempt from all of the above. Those are fixed.

- 同一场景必须包含相同事件、相同揭示、相同的收尾意象。
- 场景之内，各语言各尽其长。中文可将四句英文压成八个字；英文可将中文一行展成一整段。皆非缺陷。
- 卷三的经文感在两种语言里以**不同手段**达成：英文靠钦定本圣经的节奏，中文靠四字格与注疏句法。强迫一方模仿另一方，两败俱伤。
- `lexicon.md` 中的术语不受上述任何条款豁免。那些是钉死的。
