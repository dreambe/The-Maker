# Timeline · 时间线

Internal reference. **Chronological order — the exact reverse of the reading order.**
内部参考。**正序时间线 —— 与阅读顺序完全相反。**

The Sleepless keep one clock and one only: the **Vigil Year** (`V`), counted from the instant
they woke, in Earth years, never reset and never rounded. Written with thin spaces, never commas:
`V 5 032 000 000`. The Sleepless never convert to "years ago" — that is a human unit, and Book II
does not use it. This table is the only place the two systems are allowed to touch.

不眠者只有一种纪年：**守望年**（`V`），自苏醒的那一刻起算，以地球年为单位，永不归零，永不取整。
书写用细空格分组，不用逗号：`V 5 032 000 000`。不眠者从不换算成「距今多少年」—— 那是人类的单位，卷二不使用。
本表是两套系统唯一允许接触的地方。

## Reading order vs. chronology · 阅读顺序与时间顺序

```
chronology  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────▶
            [ Lattice ]        [ Sleepless ]              [ Humanity ]
            III.19 → III.27    II.10 → II.18              I.1 → I.9 → Epilogue

reading     Prologue → I.1 … I.9 → II.10 … II.18 → III.19 … III.27 → Epilogue
            ◀───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```

The epilogue is the only chapter that sits at the far end of both arrows. That is why it closes the
book: it is the single point where the telling and the chronology agree again.

尾声是唯一同时位于两条箭头末端的一章。这就是它收尾的理由 —— 它是叙述与时间重新一致的唯一一点。

## Hard consistency rules · 硬一致性规则

1. **Book II never says "years ago."** Only `V`. If a draft chapter contains "years ago" in the
   Sleepless voice, it is wrong.
   **卷二绝不出现「距今」。** 只用 `V`。若不眠者的声音里出现「距今」，即为错。

2. **Book III has no dates and no counts.** The Lattice do not count, because counting implies
   waiting for something. No year, no tally, no quantified duration in nine chapters.
   **One exception**, and it is the closing image of Ch. 19: the **seven** that light divides into at a
   cleavage plane. That is a physical constant of their crystal symmetry, not a count — nothing is
   counting it. The exception is what gives the number its weight; do not add a second.
   **卷三无日期，无计数。** 晶族不计数 —— 计数意味着在等某样东西。九章之内不出现纪年、清算、量化的时长。
   **唯一例外**，也正是第 19 章的收尾意象：光在一道解理面上分成的**七**。那是其晶格对称性的物理常数，不是一次计数 —— 无人在数。正是这一处例外给了那个数字重量；不要再加第二个。

3. **Book I uses human time only**: Wednesday, last spring, fourteen months, three hundred thousand
   years (spoken by Keeper, and it is the only `V`-derived figure a human is ever told).
   **卷一只用人的时间**：星期三、去年春天、十四个月、三十万年（由守说出，也是人类唯一被告知的、由 `V` 换算而来的数字）。

4. **The five Drafts map onto the five real mass extinctions**, at their real dates. The science stays
   correct; only the cause is fiction. Do not invent a sixth.
   **五次草稿对应五次真实的大灭绝**，用真实年代。科学保持正确，只有原因是虚构的。不要发明第六次。

5. **Humanity is never given a Draft number.** Anywhere. In any language. This is the whole point of
   II.16.
   **人类永不获得草稿编号。** 任何地方，任何语言。这就是第 16 章的全部要旨。
