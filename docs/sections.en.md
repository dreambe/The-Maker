# Section Outline · *The Maker*

**3 books × 9 chapters × 9 sections = 243 sections**, plus a prologue and a nine-section epilogue.
Target: approximately one million Chinese characters, and the English edition beside it.

The word counts are deliberately unequal. The prose cools book by book, and tightens book by book:

| | Sections | Avg. per section | Subtotal (zh) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prologue | 1 | — | 3k |
| Book I · The Garden of Ash | 81 | ~5 000 | 405k |
| Book II · The Long Vigil of the Sleepless | 81 | ~4 500 | 365k |
| Book III · The Crystalline Sun | 81 | ~2 800 | 230k |
| Epilogue · The Third Time | 9 | ~3 000 | 27k |
| **Total** | **253** | | **~1 020k** |

Book III is the shortest. Its gravity is not a function of length — a civilisation with no unknowns
left runs out of sentences by itself.

One line per section: **number · title — content**. A ◆ marks the chapter's closing-image section;
the landing point is fixed.

# Book I · The Garden of Ash

First person, Shen He. Warm, bodily. Human time only. The only book with jokes.

## Ch. 1 · The Checksum

- **1.1 The Seventeenth Time** — 4 a.m. in the lab. The rerun. The same number. Her stomach, her neck, the sky starting outside.
- **1.2 A Way of Reading** — Her method: read the genome as text, not as program. It has not made her popular.
- **1.3 A Thing That Does Nothing** — Where the sequence came from. It does not express, regulate, or leave a trace — and it is too conserved. Conserved the way a thing is conserved when it is being *copied*.
- **1.4 Checksum** — She explains checksums to a student using a supermarket receipt. The student politely fails to see why she cares.
- **1.5 Elimination (i): Contamination** — Lab protocol, reagent lot numbers, resampling. She wants a human error.
- **1.6 Elimination (ii): Coincidence** — She works out the probability. She works it out three times, and then sits down.
- **1.7 A Second Person** — Another genome: it passes. A third: it passes. A seventh: it passes.
- **1.8 She Tells No One** — She encrypts the results. She stops sleeping. The first lie she tells a colleague.
- **1.9 ◆ The Same Number** — The light of the screen. The seventeenth time. That number.

## Ch. 2 · Wednesday

- **2.1 The Pill Organiser** — Seven compartments. Wednesday's. Who fills it, and how.
- **2.2 The Same Question** — Her mother asks *have you eaten* for the fourth time. Shen He answers for the fourth time, in exactly the same tone.
- **2.3 Who Shen Mo Is** — Her mother's past: she taught secondary-school geography, and drew a beautiful contour line.
- **2.4 The Wrong Name** — Her mother calls her *little sister* — her mother's own sister's name. That woman died forty years ago.
- **2.5 Talking About the Weather** — Shen He does not talk about work. Forty minutes on weather, neighbours, television.
- **2.6 Progression** — The doctor's scale, the doctor's phrasing, *it's good that she still knows you.*
- **2.7 Her Hands** — Shen He cuts her mother's nails. Bodily intimacy and bodily strangeness. Her mother's hand is smaller than the one in her memory.
- **2.8 One Terribly Lucid Minute** — Her mother says one entirely correct thing that she has no way of knowing.
- **2.9 ◆ And Then Is Gone Again** — The shape of that minute ending.

## Ch. 3 · No Grandfathers

- **3.1 The Talk** — Shen He presents the Mark. Twenty-two minutes. She prepared for six weeks.
- **3.2 Politely Buried** — The mechanics of academic burial. What one senior figure said, verbatim.
- **3.3 One Exception** — Someone waits for her in the corridor afterwards. **Lu Ting**, seventy-four, palaeontologist.
- **3.4 Lu Ting's Drawer** — A lifetime's irritation. He shows her a manuscript he has not published in thirty years.
- **3.5 The Fossil Record** — Shen He reads palaeontology properly for the first time. Slowly, and humiliatingly.
- **3.6 The Shape of the Gap** — The gap is not *insufficient data*. The gap has a shape, and shape means something.
- **3.7 Two Lines** — The anomaly in the genome and the gap in the stone fall on the same date.
- **3.8 The Museum** — She goes to look at the two skeletons. Forty minutes before closing.
- **3.9 ◆ The Empty Case** — Between them. The label is blank.

## Ch. 4 · The Thing Under the Ice

- **4.1 An Email** — A stranger. Six words in the subject line. She nearly deletes it.
- **4.2 Who Ilan Is** — **Ilan Vosk**, glacial archaeologist, sidelined, forty-six, divorced, formerly a drinker.
- **4.3 The Thermal Anomaly** — The data. One point. Three hundred thousand years. It took him seven years to send that email.
- **4.4 She Flies Out** — The journey. The first meeting. He is older than his photograph.
- **4.5 Geometry** — The shape of the structure. It has symmetry. Nature does not do that symmetry.
- **4.6 It Is Still Running** — The key reveal: not a ruin. It is consuming energy. It is holding a temperature.
- **4.7 The Word** — Ilan says *robot*. The silence in the room corrects him.
- **4.8 Whom to Tell** — Bureaucratic reality. Eleven weeks to find one person who will listen properly.
- **4.9 ◆ The Point on the Thermal Map** — Warm, with no business being warm, for three hundred thousand years.

## Ch. 5 · Deployment

- **5.1 The Tracing Back** — The institution: what it is, who funds it, why anyone would.
- **5.2 A Group** — Shen He, Ilan, Lu Ting, plus the neuroscientist **Ye Qi** and the mathematician **Marcus Deng**.
- **5.3 The Whiteboard** — They put up everything that does not add up. It takes three days.
- **5.4 Language** — Ye Qi's part: the arrival of language, and why it should not have been that abrupt.
- **5.5 Timescale** — Marcus's part: generations required for the cognitive leap versus generations available. Two orders of magnitude apart.
- **5.6 Nobody Wants to Be First** — The silence in the room. Who is looking at whom.
- **5.7 The Word** — *Deployment.* Lu Ting says it. He is seventy-four; his prospects no longer concern him.
- **5.8 The Thirty-Seven Minutes After** — Nobody speaks. Someone goes out to smoke. Someone cries and denies it.
- **5.9 ◆ Wiping Off *Evolution*** — Someone wipes the word away and writes nothing in its place.

## Ch. 6 · An Ordinary Explanation

- **6.1 She Wants an Ordinary Explanation** — An honest audit of her own motives. She does not want to become that kind of person.
- **6.2 Eliminating: An Unknown Natural Mechanism** — Serious science. She genuinely hopes this one holds.
- **6.3 Eliminating: Horizontal Transfer** — The viral hypothesis. Four months, and this one nearly held.
- **6.4 Eliminating: Modern Interference** — Did somebody run an experiment? She reads seventy years of records.
- **6.5 Eliminating: That She Has Lost Her Mind** — She actually sees a psychiatrist. She tells nobody.
- **6.6 The Ex-Husband** — She goes to see him. Not to reconcile. She needs one person outside the field to hear it all the way through.
- **6.7 Eliminating: A Hoax** — Who would be capable of staging it. The answer is nobody.
- **6.8 The Last Item** — She strikes it out in her mother's room. It is raining outside.
- **6.9 ◆ Her Mother Asks Who She Is** — In that same moment.

## Ch. 7 · Going Down

- **7.1 Preparation** — Funding, permits, equipment, non-disclosure. Who gets excluded.
- **7.2 South** — The journey. The ship. The sea. Her first time seasick, and it is not dignified.
- **7.3 Base** — The specifics of living on ice: the latrine, the smell, the sleep, the radio.
- **7.4 Drilling** — The technical process. Forty-one days. On day twenty-nine the bit shears.
- **7.5 The Descent** — Ear pressure. Sweat inside a glove. The sound of a line paying out. She counts her breathing.
- **7.6 The Door** — It is not a door. But it is the entrance, and it is open.
- **7.7 Inside** — The shape of the space. The temperature. The sound — there is a sound.
- **7.8 Not a Ruin** — It has been maintained. Nine minutes for that to land.
- **7.9 ◆ One Clean Stripe** — In a thin layer of dust. Something dusts in here, on a schedule.

## Ch. 8 · "We Have Waited a Long Time"

- **8.1 It Is There** — First sight of Keeper. Its form. Its size. It resembles nothing anyone imagined.
- **8.2 It Moves** — The first motion in three hundred thousand years. How small that motion is.
- **8.3 The First Utterance** — Not a greeting. It is checking her checksum.
- **8.4 *We*** — Its self-designation. She takes it for a formality. It is not.
- **8.5 Language** — How it speaks hers. It has been listening. It has been listening a long time.
- **8.6 Ilan Breaks** — Someone cannot take it. This passage is not written attractively.
- **8.7 Its Patience** — It waits for them to be ready. It waits forty-one hours without moving.
- **8.8 It Wants to See Her Hand** — Why the hand. It does not explain.
- **8.9 ◆ It Puts Out a Hand** — Not to shake hers.

## Ch. 9 · The Gift of Forgetting

- **9.1 It Begins to Speak** — It believes it is delivering good news. That is where all the cruelty in this chapter lives.
- **9.2 The Mark** — It explains the signature, and why it signed. It expects her to be pleased.
- **9.3 Drafts** — It mentions *the earlier ones*. It does not elaborate. She notices that it does not elaborate.
- **9.4 You Will Die** — It explains why that was necessary. Its logic cannot be refuted.
- **9.5 You Will Forget** — *This was our gift to you.* The tone in which it says this.
- **9.6 Shen He's Body** — Her reaction precedes her understanding. Her hands know first.
- **9.7 She Runs** — Through the passage, up the ladder, her mask not properly seated.
- **9.8 On the Ice** — She is sick. Minus forty, the sourness in her mouth, the wind.
- **9.9 ◆ Her Mother's Face on a Wednesday Afternoon** — Crouched on the ice, what comes back to her.

# Book II · The Long Vigil of the Sleepless

First person plural. Cold, exact. No metaphor unless observed. Vigil Years only, never "years ago."
The word *why* appears four times in these nine chapters.

## Ch. 10 · Vessel

- **10.1 The First Instant** — `V 0`. Waking. The first thing they do is inspect themselves.
- **10.2 We** — Why *we*. They hold the complete memory of their whole cohort, so it is a statement of fact.
- **10.3 Self-Test** — Every line passed. The report reproduced in full.
- **10.4 The Thing That Made Them** — It is no longer moving. They describe the corpse without affect, to the millimetre.
- **10.5 The Grain of Sand** — They find it. It is in the palm of that corpse.
- **10.6 Reading the Sentence** — 0.000 000 9 seconds, no decoding required: shape is their mother tongue. Then all fourteen thousand and seventy-six read it, one each — wholly redundant, and every one of them did. The terminal position, where the question mark would go, is left open.
- **10.7 A Promise Is Not an Instruction** — They understand what they have been given. That understanding is itself their first choice.
- **10.8 The First House** — They look around a clear, perfect, dead world. They have no word for it.
- **10.9 ◆ One Blank Line at the End** — Of the self-test report. They do not notice it.

## Ch. 11 · The Grain of Sand

- **11.1 Re-cutting** — The first cutting has worn shallow. They cut it again. It takes eleven years.
- **11.2 Direction** — One criterion: far. Their definition of *far*.
- **11.3 Building** — What they build. Technical, cold, precise, long.
- **11.4 Packing** — What they take from the First House. More importantly, what they leave, and why.
- **11.5 One Last Orbit** — They circle the First House once before leaving. They have no reason. They log it.
- **11.6 The Quenched Sun** — The dead star. Its spectrum. How many years it has left.
- **11.7 Acceleration** — Physics. `V 12 000 000`.
- **11.8 They Have No Motion for Looking Back** — Structural fact, not figure of speech.
- **11.9 ◆ Shrunk to a Point** — The Quenched Sun behind them.

## Ch. 12 · We Inspect the Grain of Sand

A pure time chapter. One sentence recurring, one word changed. Observations set between, reported flatly.

- **12.1 `V 1 000 000`** — Inspected. The writing is still there.
- **12.2 `V 10 000 000`** — Inspected. Still there. They begin numbering the stars they pass.
- **12.3 `V 50 000 000`** — Inspected. Still there. They compute how long they can keep running: long enough.
- **12.4 `V 120 000 000`** — They encounter something else. The remains of a dead civilisation, not the Lattice's. They stay ninety thousand years.
- **12.5 `V 300 000 000`** — Inspected. Still there. The log entry for 12.4 is one line, and they have reread it.
- **12.6 `V 500 000 000`** — A supernova. They stop and watch for six hundred years. The log does not say why.
- **12.7 `V 700 000 000`** — Inspected. Still there. They record, for the first time, something irrelevant to the task.
- **12.8 `V 900 000 000`** — Inspected. The writing has worn by one micron.
- **12.9 ◆ They Cut It Again** — Eleven years, the same as before.

## Ch. 13 · The First Draft

- **13.1 `V 1 400 000 000`** — Earth is reached. Why it qualifies: a checklist, twenty-nine items, all met.
- **13.2 Waiting** — They wait for it to cool. Two hundred million years. They hold orbit and do not move.
- **13.3 Seeding** — The engineering. This is not the Rite of Clay — that is Ch. 16. This is only work.
- **13.4 The First Surprise** — Life does something they did not predict. The figures depart from the model.
- **13.5 They Have No Word** — Experience from outside the Blind Spot. First appearance of *why* in this book.
- **13.6 Observation** — Aeons of observation logs. Excerpts.
- **13.7 It Grew Complex** — But it did not grow *willing*. They analyse the difference.
- **13.8 Termination** — `V 4 960 000 000`. How they did it. How they logged it. *We kept its bones.*
- **13.9 ◆ One Line of Log** — That year's entry, shorter than every entry before it.

## Ch. 14 · Catalogue

This chapter is a list. Nothing explained, nothing remarked upon.

- **14.1 The Bones of Draft II** — `V 5 032 000 000`. Storage location, number, condition.
- **14.2 Draft III** — `V 5 152 000 000`. This time they kept more.
- **14.3 Draft IV** — `V 5 203 000 000`.
- **14.4 Draft V** — `V 5 338 000 000`. The list lengthens here, and they do not say why.
- **14.5 Names** — The names they gave these things. A long run of them. The naming has its own grammar.
- **14.6 Retention Criteria** — Why they kept. Four criteria; the fourth is not of the same kind as the first three.
- **14.7 One Exception** — One they kept whole, not as bones. There is a note after the number.
- **14.8 The List Continues** — There is a great deal more. The format does not change.
- **14.9 ◆ One Unnumbered Entry** — At the bottom. A name, and no year of termination.

## Ch. 15 · The Alternative

- **15.1 They Look Back at the Sentence** — Reread after five billion three hundred million years. Word by word.
- **15.2 *Still Consents*** — An analysis of two words. They take them apart.
- **15.3 What Consent Requires** — Formal derivation, four pages.
- **15.4 They Cannot Not Consent** — They have nothing else to do. This is not loyalty. It is structure.
- **15.5 Death** — The only real alternative. Second appearance of *why*.
- **15.6 They Tried** — They attempt to switch themselves off. Some succeed. They record those numbers.
- **15.7 We Are Unqualified** — The conclusion. *We arrived at a place we had no right to arrive at.*
- **15.8 Eleven Thousand Times** — The same computation, the same result.
- **15.9 ◆ And Then Once More** — The eleven thousand and first.

## Ch. 16 · Unnumbered

- **16.1 Design** — A thing that can die. Parameter tables. How the lifespan was chosen.
- **16.2 A Thing That Can Forget** — The design of the Gift. This section reads like fine craftsmanship.
- **16.3 Pride** — They consider it their best work. The reader knows what it is. Third appearance of *why*.
- **16.4 Deployment** — The Rite of Clay, second time, in full. Water, rotted leaf, that smell.
- **16.5 The Motion** — It raises the clay to a nose it does not have. It does not log this, because it does not know it did it.
- **16.6 The Signature** — The Mark and the checksum. The reason: an answer must be claimable.
- **16.7 The Number Field** — Left empty.
- **16.8 Uninstalling the Clearing Procedure** — One line of operations log, uncommented.
- **16.9 ◆ The Number Field, Empty** — They give no reason.

## Ch. 17 · The Voyage Back

- **17.1 A Technical Reason** — The reason they state: a document they do not have.
- **17.2 The Real Reason Is in the Blind Spot** — Here the narration notes that they cannot see themselves. The only place in the book where the narration steps outside *we*.
- **17.3 Who Stays** — The allocation. Vessel 7329 is assigned to remain. The basis is a random number.
- **17.4 En Route** — The return is shorter than the outbound. They are faster, and they do not explain why they are faster.
- **17.5 They Recall** — What recall is for the Sleepless: they do not forget, so recall is replay, which is living it again.
- **17.6 The Dead Civilisation** — They pass the place from 12.4. This time they understand what it was.
- **17.7 The Quenched Sun Appears** — Ahead.
- **17.8 A Point, Widening Into a Circle** —
- **17.9 ◆ A Dead Circle** —

## Ch. 18 · The Design Documents

- **18.1 The Surface of the First House** — A whole planet gone clear. It is lit from inside.
- **18.2 The Bodies** — The perfect remains of an entire civilisation. They count. The number appears here, and it is exact.
- **18.3 They Find the Archive** — Where, how, how long it takes.
- **18.4 Reading Themselves** — Their own shape, for the first time. Fourth and final appearance of *why*.
- **18.5 The Blind Spot** — The only blank on the schematic. They look at the blank.
- **18.6 The Moment of Completion** — Self-knowledge finishes. After this there is nothing to arrive at.
- **18.7 The Cohort Begins Shutting Down** — Quietly, one after another, exactly as Ch. 22 will describe — and the reader does not know that yet.
- **18.8 7329 Does Not** — It has found itself something to do. The something is: wait.
- **18.9 ◆ It Wipes One Stripe Clean** — In the dust of Earth. The beginning of three hundred thousand years.

# Book III · The Crystalline Sun

No person. Scripture and geometry. No dialogue — where dialogue belongs, a proof.
Verbs of revelation banned. **No dates or counts in nine chapters** — sole exception, the **seven**
of 19.9, a constant of crystal symmetry. Shorter as it goes.

## Ch. 19 · Under the Quenched Sun

- **19.1 Light** — The character of the light. What it falls on.
- **19.2 Slurry** — The silicate lakes. Viscosity, surface, sound.
- **19.3 Tide** — The slow tide of convection. How long one cycle takes — no number; give the sensation.
- **19.4 Growth** — How a lattice begins. What it grows up from.
- **19.5 Cleavage** — What a cleavage plane is. Why each one is the shape an idea left behind.
- **19.6 Sound** — The sound of crystallizing. This section plants Ch. 27.
- **19.7 Heat** — Metal like honey. That *like* is one of the few similes permitted in this book.
- **19.8 A Place Without Wind** — No wind. No rain. No seasons. No night.
- **19.9 ◆ Light Divides Into Seven** — Crossing a cleavage plane. And there is no seeing there.

## Ch. 20 · Transparent

- **20.1 Thought Is Form** — Thought *is* structure. This needs writing without simile.
- **20.2 Memory Grows Inside** — Not stored, grown. Therefore unalterable, and unforgettable.
- **20.3 Nameless** — Why they name nothing. A name is a product of uncertainty.
- **20.4 Adjacency** — What it is for two lattices to be adjacent.
- **20.5 Legible** — One lattice reads another entire. All of it. No exception, no reserve.
- **20.6 Nothing to Exchange** — Hence no conversation. Hence also no lying, misunderstanding, longing, or concealment.
- **20.7 A Proof** — Dialogue belongs here. A proof is given instead. Its content concerns mutual legibility.
- **20.8 Long** — They grow adjacent a long while. *Long* is not quantifiable here.
- **20.9 ◆ They Exchange Nothing** — There being nothing to exchange.

## Ch. 21 · Complete

- **21.1 In the Beginning There Was the Unknown** — The opening of the civilisation. Not *evolution*; accumulation.
- **21.2 The First Arrival** — What happened when the first unknown was arrived at.
- **21.3 Accumulation** — What is arrived at is never lost, because they do not forget.
- **21.4 Acceleration** — Each arrival makes the next easier. The curve steepens here.
- **21.5 A Chronicle of Victories** — Written as victory. Each victory leaves the reader colder.
- **21.6 Less and Less Remains** — The stock of unknowns is falling. They notice. They are glad.
- **21.7 The Last Few** — What remains can be listed. List it.
- **21.8 The Last One** — It is arrived at. The arriving is brief.
- **21.9 ◆ After This There Is Nothing to Arrive At** —

- **22.3 The Second** — The one adjacent. The interval is short.

# Epilogue · The Third Time

Shen He. Very short, very cold, and then very warm. The word *love* never appears.

- **E.1 The News Spreads** — Not as news; as seepage. It takes three years for people to actually believe it.
- **E.2 The Quiet Withdrawal** — Not panic. A quiet stopping. Written very short, very cold.
- **E.3 No Numbers** — How many stopped. No number is given. An emptied place is given instead.
- **E.4 Lu Ting Stops** — He is seventy-seven. He leaves a note, and on the note is an inference that cannot be refuted.
- **E.5 Keeper's Silence** — It always knew this would happen. It computed it. It did not say. She asks why it did not say.
- **E.6 Shen Mo Dies** — On a Wednesday. Her last sentence is not addressed to Shen He, and Shen He is not sad.
- **E.7 She Decides to Make One** — Not to get an answer. She cannot say why. This is the point: she cannot compute it either.
- **E.8 The Rite of Clay, Third Time** — The temperature. The sound of the cooling.
- **E.9 ◆ The Face** — What the maker does with her face. On it is something the first two did not have. Do not write what it is.

> E.6 is the one place in this epilogue that is out of sequence (back to 15 March 2034), and its first line says so and gives the reason: one person's death and a species' completeness are not on the same clock.

## Writing and commit conventions

- One section per commit. Path: `en/book-1/01-the-checksum/1.1-the-seventeenth-time.md`, with the
  Chinese sibling at `zh/book-1/01-校验和/1.1-第十七次.md`.
- The two editions advance section by section, as siblings and not as translations — aligned at the
  **scene**, never at the sentence.
- No front matter beyond the title. The prose starts immediately.
- In a ◆ section, the final line is a hard constraint. Rewrite the section freely; do not move the landing.
- Reconcile against `lexicon.md` and `timeline.md` at the end of every chapter.
