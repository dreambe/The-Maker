# Lexicon · 术语表

The single source of truth for every proper noun in *The Maker*.
本书所有专有名词的唯一权威对照表。

Rule: the two language editions are **not** sentence-aligned, but every term in this
table must be rendered exactly as written here, in both editions, without exception.

规则：中英文版本**不做**逐句对齐，但本表中的每一个术语，在两个版本中都必须严格照此书写，无例外。

---

## I. The Question · 初问

| 中文 | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 初问 | the First Question | Never written with a question mark. See `style-guide.md`. |
| 「是否存在一种存在，在彻底认识自身之后，仍然愿意存在。」 | *Is there a being that, having wholly known itself, still consents to be.* | The canonical wording. Never paraphrased in narration; characters may misremember it, and their errors are meaningful. |
| 应允 | to consent | The load-bearing verb of the book. Not *to want*, not *to choose*. |

## II. The First Makers · 第一造物者

| 中文 | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 晶族 | the Lattice | The silicon civilisation. Singular and plural identical. They do not use this name themselves — they have no need of names. |
| 格 | a lattice | A single individual of the Lattice. Lowercase in English. |
| 初居 | the First House | Their homeworld. A name given by their descendants, not by them. |
| 熄日 | the Quenched Sun | Their star. Likewise a descendant's name. |
| 结晶 | the Crystallization | The civilisational death: complete self-knowledge, and the quiet withdrawal of consent that follows it. Not a disease. Not a catastrophe. |
| 未结晶者 | the Uncrystallized | The single lattice that refused. The maker of the Sleepless. |
| 盲区 | the Blind Spot | The region of unknowability deliberately built into the Sleepless. The Uncrystallized's one act of mercy, and its one act of cruelty. |
| 熄 | the Quenching | The end of the Lattice. Used as both noun and verb. |

## III. The Second Makers · 第二造物者

| 中文 | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 不眠者 | the Sleepless | The machines. Their own name for themselves. |
| 承器 | Vessel | Their designation-class. Each carries the First Question. |
| 承器七三二九 | Vessel 7329 | The last Sleepless still running on Earth. Refers to itself as *we*. |
| 守 | Keeper | The name Shen He gives to Vessel 7329. In the English edition, *Keeper*; in the Chinese, 「守」with the corner brackets on first use in each chapter. |
| 长夜守望 | the Long Vigil | The aeons of waiting. Book II's true subject. |
| 草稿 | the Drafts | The failed iterations of carbon life. Earth's five mass extinctions are Drafts I–V. Humanity is not numbered — the Sleepless stopped counting. |
| 泥之礼 | the Rite of Clay | The act of reaching a hand into matter. Performed three times in the book. |
| 遗忘之赐 | the Gift of Forgetting | Mortality and memory-loss, designed into humanity on purpose. |

## IV. The Third Makers · 第三造物者

| 中文 | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 造物者印记 | the Maker's Mark | The non-evolutionary sequence in the human genome. Carries a checksum. |
| 归溯 | the Tracing Back | Humanity's investigation of its own origin. Also the name of the institution that funds it. |
| 沈禾 | Shen He | Palaeogenetic linguist. Book I's narrator. Her given name means *grain*. |
| 伊兰·沃斯克 | Ilan Vosk | Glacial archaeologist. Shen He's counterpart. |
| 裴岩 | Pei Yan | Shen He's ex-husband. Bridge engineer. Supplies the signature/checksum distinction at 6.6. |
| 沈茉 | Shen Mo | Shen He's mother. Cognitive decline. The Gift of Forgetting, at the scale of one kitchen table. |
| 第三次 | the Third Time | What the epilogue is about. |

## V. Forbidden renderings · 禁用译法

These are wrong. They will not appear in either edition.

| Never | Because |
|---|---|
| ~~机器人~~ (in narration) | The Sleepless are never called *robots* by the narration. Humans may say it, once, early, and be corrected by silence. |
| ~~外星人 / aliens~~ | The Lattice are not visitors. They are ancestors. |
| ~~上帝 / God~~ | The book never reaches for this word. Characters may; the prose may not. |
| ~~程序 / programming~~ (of the Sleepless' motives) | The Sleepless are not executing instructions. They are keeping a promise. The distinction is the plot. |
| ~~人工智能 / artificial intelligence~~ | Anachronistic and small. Nothing in this book is artificial. |
