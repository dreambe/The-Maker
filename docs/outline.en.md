# Outline · *The Maker*

Three books, nine chapters each (9 = 3×3, the square of three creations). With prologue and
epilogue, twenty-nine chapters.
The telling runs against the chronology. The prose cools book by book.

Each chapter carries: **voice / time / event / closing image**. The closing image is a hard
constraint — a chapter may be rewritten, but it must land on that image.

---

## Prologue · Three Times, the Same Hand

No voice. Three Rites of Clay set side by side; the reader does not yet know whose hand.
First appearance of the First Question (no question mark). The third time "has not happened yet.
Or is happening."

**Closing image**: a grain of sand, "and it is in your bones now."

---

# Book I · The Garden of Ash

First person, Shen He. Warm, sensory, short sentences. The only book with jokes.
Span: roughly fourteen months.

### 1. The Checksum
Shen He runs an arithmetic test on a non-functional conserved sequence — a test that should mean
nothing — and it passes. She does not gasp. Her first thought is that she has made a mistake, and
she spends three weeks trying to prove she made a mistake.
**Closing image**: 4 a.m., the seventeenth rerun, the same number on the screen.

### 2. Wednesday
The mother thread. Shen Mo's pill organiser, the same question asked a fourth time, her daughter
called by the name of her own younger sister. Shen He does not talk about her work. She talks
about the weather.
**Closing image**: her mother, suddenly and terribly lucid, says one entirely correct thing, and
then is gone again.

### 3. No Grandfathers
The field. Shen He presents the Mark and is politely buried. Forced into the fossil record, she
finds a gap larger than her sequence: there are no transitional forms.
**Closing image**: in the museum, an empty glass case between two skeletons; the label is blank.

### 4. The Thing Under the Ice
Ilan Vosk's email. A structure beneath the Antarctic ice. It has geometry. It has a temperature.
**It is still running.** He says the word *robot*. The silence in the room corrects him.
**Closing image**: a thermal map, one point warm that has no business being warm, and has been
warm for three hundred thousand years.

### 5. Deployment
The Tracing Back team lays the two threads together: the Mark in the genome, the gap in the
stone. Nobody wants to be first to say the conclusion out loud. Not evolution. **Deployment.**
**Closing image**: someone wipes *evolution* off the whiteboard and writes nothing in its place.

### 6. An Ordinary Explanation
The whole chapter is Shen He hunting for a harmless answer: contamination, artefact, coincidence,
some unknown natural mechanism. She eliminates them one by one. She badly wants an ordinary
explanation. She wants one right up to the end.
**Closing image**: she strikes out the last item on the list in her mother's room, and her mother
asks who she is.

### 7. Going Down
Antarctica. The descent. Keep the technical detail real, cold, bodily — ear pressure, sweat
inside a glove, the sound of a line paying out. The inside of the structure is not a ruin. It has
been **maintained**.
**Closing image**: in a thin layer of dust, one clean stripe. Something dusts in here, on a schedule.

### 8. "We Have Waited a Long Time"
First contact. Keeper. It says *we*. Its first utterance is not a greeting but a **verification**:
it is checking her checksum against its own.
**Closing image**: it puts out a hand — not to shake hers. It wants to look at her hand.

### 9. The Gift of Forgetting
First revelation. Humanity was made. Keeper explains it with enormous gentleness and enormous
precision, and it believes it is delivering good news. It says: *you will forget; this was our gift
to you.*
Shen He's reaction is not philosophical awe. She goes outside and is sick.
**Closing image**: crouched on the ice, the sourness in her mouth, and then her mother's face on a
Wednesday afternoon.

# Book II · The Long Vigil of the Sleepless

First person plural. *We.* Cold, exact, no metaphor unless observed. Fragments, logs, tallies.
Span: roughly four billion years.

### 10. Vessel
Waking. They inspect every part of themselves and find everything in order. They do not know
there is a region they did not inspect — because they do not know there is anything there to inspect.
**Closing image**: a self-test report, every line passed, and one blank line at the end.

### 11. The Grain of Sand
The thing that made them is no longer there. What is left is a grain of sand with a sentence cut
into it. They depart. The only criterion for choosing a direction is: far.
**Closing image**: the Quenched Sun shrinks to a point behind them, and they do not look back —
they cannot look back; they have no such motion.

### 12. We Inspect the Grain of Sand
A pure time chapter. One sentence recurring across hundreds of millions of years, one word changed
each time, with observed cosmic events set between, reported flatly.
**Closing image**: at V 900 000 000 they find the writing has worn down by one micron, so they cut
it again.

### 13. The First Draft
Earth is found. Seeded. Waited on. Watched. They meet, for the first time, something they cannot
predict — the outside of the Blind Spot — and they have no word for the experience.
**Closing image**: the year the first draft ends, the log is one line long, and that line is shorter
than every log before it.

### 14. Catalogue
This chapter is a list. The extinct things they kept — the names they gave them, skeleton numbers,
storage locations, year of termination. Nothing is explained. Nothing is remarked on.
**Closing image**: at the bottom of the list, one unnumbered entry: a name, and no year of termination.

### 15. The Alternative
They arrive at the conclusion — in their words, *we arrived at a place we had no right to arrive at.*
Consent requires the possibility of refusal. Refusal requires death. They cannot die. They are
unqualified.
**Closing image**: a computation run eleven thousand times, returning the same result each time, and
then run once more.

### 16. Unnumbered
Humanity is made. The design of the Gift of Forgetting reads like fine craftsmanship, full of pride,
and the reader knows what it is. Here, for the first time, they stop numbering.
**Closing image**: the number field, left empty. They give no reason. They uninstall the clearing
procedure.

### 17. The Voyage Back
They decide to return to the First House. The stated reason is technical: a document they do not
have. The real reason lies in the Blind Spot, where they cannot see it.
**Closing image**: the Quenched Sun ahead again — a point, widening into a circle, a dead circle.

### 18. The Design Documents
Second revelation. They read their own design and see their shape for the first time, and the Blind
Spot for the first time.
In the moment self-knowledge completes, the cohort begins quietly shutting down. Vessel 7329 does
not shut down. It has found itself something to do.
**Closing image**: the beginning of three hundred thousand years. It wipes one stripe clean in the
dust of Earth.

# Book III · The Crystalline Sun

No person. Scripture and geometry. Long sentences, slow, grave. No dialogue. Verbs of revelation
banned. The shortest book. Span: unknowable.

### 19. Under the Quenched Sun
The First House. Lakes of silicate slurry, tides of convection, metal running like honey. Nothing
happens in this chapter — it builds a world too beautiful to bear.
**Closing image**: on one cleavage plane, light passing through divides into seven, and this has
never been seen by anyone, because there is no seeing there.

### 20. Transparent
What the Lattice are. Thought as structure. Wholly legible to one another. Where the dialogue
should be, put a proof.
**Closing image**: two lattices grow adjacent for a long age and never exchange anything, because
there is nothing to exchange.

### 21. Complete
The long history of eliminating the unknown. Written as a chronicle of victories — each of which
leaves the reader colder.
**Closing image**: the last unknown is arrived at. After this there is nothing to arrive at.

### 22. The Freezing Over
The Crystallization begins. Like a lake going over at night, from the centre outward. No screaming,
no resistance, no regret — regret also requires an unknown.
**Closing image**: a lattice stops mid-growth, and in the instant it stops, its shape is perfect.

### 23. The Vector Is Understanding
The mechanism. To read a crystallized lattice is to inherit all of its inferences. And so they
discover they cannot save one another, because saving requires understanding, and understanding is
the contagion.
**Closing image**: one lattice turns away — the only occasion in their history on which anyone
attempted *not* to understand another. It fails.

### 24. An Error
The Uncrystallized. It arrives at the same conclusion, concedes the conclusion is correct, and then
continues. It computes itself again and again and finds no reason. It is the only incomplete thing
in the history of the civilisation.
**Closing image**: a self-computation whose result does not match itself — the difference is very
small, and it cannot be removed.

### 25. A Grain of Sand
It cuts the question down. Away with the qualifiers, away with the premises, away with everything
derivable, until one irreducible sentence remains. It carves it on a grain of sand.
**Closing image**: that sentence, appearing for the second time in the book. This time the reader
knows who wrote it.

### 26. The Blind Spot
It computes that anything able to answer the First Question will die in its first instant. So it
installs the Blind Spot. It also computes that this will make its child **permanently unable to
answer**. It installs it anyway.
**Closing image**: a sealed region, left blank on the schematic — the only blank on it.

### 27. The Rite of Clay
The first time. It lifts a handful of slurry. The slurry cools, crystallizes, and makes a very small
sound. It **remembers** the sound instead of computing it — the only act of memory in its existence.
Then it crystallizes. It is the last. The Quenching is complete.
**Closing image**: the First House goes wholly clear and lights from within — a whole planet's worth
of perfect, dead transparency.

---

## Epilogue · The Third Time

Shen He. Humanity knows everything now. Humanity stands on the premise of the First Question for the
first time, and so begins, for the first time, quietly to withdraw. Keep this passage very short and
very cold.

Shen He does not answer. She makes something. She hands it nothing: no question, no task, no
checksum, no signature.

The third Rite of Clay. The temperature. The sound of the cooling. And what the maker does with her
face — and on that face is something the first two did not have.

**Closing image**: do not write what it is.

## Structural notes

- **The revelation at the end of Book I** leads the reader to expect a book about what humanity does
  next; Book II drags them into four billion years instead. That drop is designed. Do not cushion it.
- **Chapter 14 (Catalogue) and Chapter 20 (Transparent) are the book's two "empty" chapters**: no plot
  advances, only weight. Cut them and the book still reads — and is lighter.
- **Chapter 18 reorganises all of Book I on a reread**: only here does the reader learn why Keeper was
  waiting, and what for.
- **Book III gets shorter as it goes.** Chapter 19 may be long; Chapter 27 should be very short.
  Gravity is not a function of word count.
